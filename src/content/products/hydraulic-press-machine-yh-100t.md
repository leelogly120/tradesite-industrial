## Product Overview

The YH-100T Hydraulic Press Machine is a versatile 100-ton industrial press designed for metal stamping, deep drawing, blanking, bending, and forming operations. Its robust four-column construction ensures consistent pressing force and alignment across the entire work area.

Equipped with a modern PLC control system and 7-inch color touch screen, operators can store up to 100 production recipes, set precise pressing parameters, and monitor real-time tonnage curves. The proportional hydraulic system provides smooth, controllable ram movement from 15 to 100 mm/s.

This press is widely used in automotive parts manufacturing, electrical component production, hardware stamping, and plastic molding industries.

## Applications

- **Metal Stamping**: Electrical contacts, automotive brackets, kitchenware components
- **Deep Drawing**: Stainless steel pots, aluminum housings, sheet metal enclosures
- **Powder Compacting**: Ceramic parts, P/M components, magnetic cores
- **Assembly Pressing**: Bearing pressing, riveting, shaft pressing operations

## Safety Features

The YH-100T comes standard with dual-hand anti-tie-down controls, an emergency stop button, and a mechanical safety block for die maintenance. Optional Type 4 light curtains and safety PLC upgrade available for full CE compliance.
