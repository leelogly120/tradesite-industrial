## Product Overview

The CK6150 CNC Lathe is a proven flat-bed turning center designed for efficient production of shafts, flanges, bushings, and other rotational parts. With 500mm maximum swing diameter and 1000mm turning length, it covers the majority of CNC turning applications in small to medium batch manufacturing.

The machine features a one-piece cast iron bed with induction-hardened V-flat guideways, providing excellent rigidity and long-term geometric accuracy. The 82mm through-spindle bore accommodates bar stock up to 80mm, making it ideal for both chucking and bar work.

Over 2,000 units of CK6150 have been deployed across workshops in Southeast Asia, the Middle East, South America, and Eastern Europe, earning a reputation for reliability and ease of use.

## Applications

- **Shaft Manufacturing**: Motor shafts, pump shafts, transmission shafts with keyways and threads
- **Flange Production**: Pipe flanges, mounting flanges, bearing housings with precision bores
- **Bushing & Sleeve**: Bronze bushings, steel sleeves, wear rings with tight ID/OD tolerances
- **Thread Cutting**: Metric, imperial, and NPT threads with single-pass or multi-pass cycles

## Control System Options

**FANUC 0i-TF**: Industry-standard CNC with G-code programming, graphical tool path display, and Ethernet connectivity. Recommended for export markets.

**GSK 980TDi**: Cost-effective Chinese CNC system with conversational programming. Suitable for domestic users and budget-conscious buyers. Both include USB port and RS232 communication.
