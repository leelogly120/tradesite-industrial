## Product Overview

The **ARC-RF8** is the forming heart of the aerial roll forming system — a standing seam panel line engineered specifically to operate on ARC lift platforms at heights up to 32 meters. Where conventional roll formers live on the ground and depend on cranes to deliver their output, the ARC-RF8 rides the platform and feeds finished panels directly onto the purlins.

It forms standing seam (JCH-type), trapezoidal roof panel and clip-lock profiles from 0.3-1.0mm color steel, galvanized or aluminum-magnesium-manganese coil, in continuous lengths from 1 to 200 meters. A hydraulic shear with 5.5kW pump cuts panels cleanly at height, commanded from the ground.

Integration is the point: an aviation-plug connection ties the machine into the lift's Mitsubishi PLC cabinet, so forming speed, shearing and panel counting run from the **same radio remote** that drives the lift. Lift and forming functions can be integrated into one controller; trained site crew, supervision and the approved lift plan remain required.

## Applications

### Matched Line for ARC Lifts
Order together with any ARC-C or ARC-T model for a factory-matched, pre-commissioned aerial forming line.

### Profile Expansion
Add a second ARC-RF8 with different tooling to switch profiles between projects without re-tooling downtime.

### Replacement & Upgrade
Replace an aging ground former with an aerial-ready line and reduce repeated crane lifts in your panel logistics.

## Technical Highlights

- **Drive**: electric motor + reducer, VFD-controlled speed (18.5kW drive in the lift cabinet).
- **Shear**: hydraulic, 5.5kW pump motor, high-pressure stable cutting (Yuci Yuken / Rexroth type pumps).
- **Controls**: aviation-plug power/signal link, radio remote operation, Mitsubishi per-project and total panel counter.
- **Materials**: forms 0.3-1.0mm coil; suitable for roof and wall panel feeding.
- **Tooling**: customizable to regional standards — send profile drawings for confirmation.
