## Product Overview

The **FDDPT-35M** is ARCLIFT's flagship heavy-duty crawler under-ceiling aerial work platform — purpose-built for the world's most ambitious large-scale construction and maintenance projects. With a maximum working height of **35 meters**, a massive **12m×8m extended platform**, and an industry-leading **3-ton safe working load**, this machine redefines what is possible for reverse ceiling work at scale.

Unlike conventional spider lifts or boom lifts that force crews to reposition repeatedly, the FDDPT-35M delivers true **pick & carry** capability: it travels at full stroke while carrying heavy materials, tools, and up to 10 workers simultaneously. The oversized platform transforms into a mobile factory floor at height, enabling continuous ceiling panel installation, HVAC ductwork, lighting rigging, and structural steel connections without constant ground-level reloads.

At approximately **53 tonnes**, the FDDPT-35M's heavy-duty chassis is engineered around 700L high-strength steel plates, a French slewing bearing, and PC360 crawler chains — delivering the stability and durability that mega-projects demand. The dual power system — a **160hp diesel engine** paired with twin electric motors — ensures seamless transition between outdoor rough-terrain travel and indoor zero-emission operation in sensitive environments like airport terminals and enclosed stadiums.

Every critical component is sourced from tier-one suppliers: **Schneider Electric** control systems, **K3V112** hydraulic pumps, **GM** drive units, and **42CrMo** cylinder piston rods. Full remote control operation keeps operators safely clear of overhead hazard zones, while the integrated 5-ton crane enables on-platform material handling without additional equipment.

## Applications

- **Major Airport Terminals**: Large-scale ceiling panel installation, lighting grid setup, HVAC ductwork, and long-term maintenance at heights up to 35m across vast terminal spans.
- **Olympic & World Cup Stadiums**: Massive large-span ceiling construction and structural steel connections where conventional scaffolding is impractical and cost-prohibitive.
- **Convention & Exhibition Centers**: High-reach installation of acoustic panels, lighting rigs, AV systems, and event infrastructure with rapid platform repositioning.
- **Heavy Industrial Facilities**: Large-scale ceiling panel installation, overhead crane rail maintenance, and pipe rack work in steel mills, power plants, and manufacturing complexes.
- **Steel Structure Mega-Projects**: Large-span roof and ceiling work for logistics hubs, data centers, and industrial warehouses where pick & carry dramatically reduces cycle time per panel.

## Technical Highlights

The FDDPT-35M distinguishes itself from every other under-ceiling platform on the market through its **heavy-duty engineering philosophy**:

**Upgraded Powertrain**: The 160hp diesel engine (upgraded from standard 110hp) delivers the torque needed to move a 53T machine across uneven construction sites at 3.0 km/h, while the K3V112 hydraulic pump ensures precise, responsive platform movement under full load.

**Heavy-Load Platform**: With a safe working load of 1.2–3T and an integrated 5T crane, the FDDPT-35M handles materials that would require separate lifting equipment on other platforms. The one-button bidirectional platform extension (12m×3m → 12m×8m) and one-button platform leveling let crews maximize their working footprint in seconds.

**Imported Safety Systems**: Oil pipe explosion prevention, load sensing, violation operation alarms, outrigger leveling detection, and platform anti-top collision systems form a multi-layer safety envelope. The full remote control operation means the operator never stands directly under the work zone.

**All-Terrain Capability**: PC360 crawler chains, 30% gradeability, 360° steering, and full-stroke walking make the FDDPT-35M equally capable on muddy construction sites and polished concrete warehouse floors. The 1.27m minimum ground clearance and folding guardrails (3.17m transport height) ensure site-to-site mobility.
