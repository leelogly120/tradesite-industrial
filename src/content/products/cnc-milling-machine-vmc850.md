## Product Overview

The VMC850 CNC Vertical Milling Machine is our flagship product designed for medium to large-scale precision manufacturing. With an 850mm x 500mm worktable and BT40 spindle taper, it handles everything from prototype machining to high-volume production runs.

Built with a rigid box-way construction and precision ground ballscrews on all axes, the VMC850 delivers consistent accuracy of 0.008mm positioning and 0.005mm repeatability. The machine is widely used in mold making, automotive parts manufacturing, aerospace component production, and general precision engineering.

Our VMC850 has been exported to over 40 countries and is trusted by manufacturers who demand reliability and precision at a competitive price point.

## Applications

- **Mold & Die Making**: High-speed machining of complex 3D contours in hardened steel and aluminum
- **Automotive Parts**: Cylinder heads, transmission housings, brake components with tight tolerances
- **Aerospace Components**: Structural parts, brackets, and fittings in titanium and Inconel alloys
- **General Manufacturing**: Flanges, valve bodies, pump housings, and custom fixtures

## Technical Highlights

The VMC850 features a one-piece cast iron bed with FEM-optimized ribbing for maximum vibration damping. The spindle is directly coupled to minimize runout, and the ATC (Automatic Tool Changer) completes tool changes in under 3 seconds.

All machines undergo a rigorous 72-hour burn-in test before shipping to ensure long-term reliability.
