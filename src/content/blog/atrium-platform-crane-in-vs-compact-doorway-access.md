Choose a compact doorway-entry platform when it can traverse the complete occupied-building route and serve the actual overhead tasks. Buy a specialist crane-in package only when the proposed machine is suitable for transfer, the building can receive it, and the price covers a feasible exit as well as entry. Compare the completed work and restored building on both routes.

A manager planning an atrium refit might receive a low weekly price for a broad work platform and a higher rate for a compact machine. Both providers can quote a height class. One can arrive through the public entrance. The other needs a temporary opening and a lifting contractor. Before comparing rental or purchase rates, decide which route delivers a working platform to each task and takes it away again.

## Walk the compact alternative from delivery to the final bay

Begin at the place where the carrier can deliver. Follow the proposed machine through the outer and inner doors, turns, thresholds and finished circulation areas to its first standing position. Count temporary removals, protection and reinstatement in the offer. A clear width through one door says little about the alignment needed for the next turn.

Draw the operating positions separately. Measure each ceiling task from the actual supporting floor and mark horizontal offsets, balconies, furniture and other obstructions. Ask the access provider to identify the supported positions and crew work it can serve. Obtain the floor review against the proposed equipment and its actual configurations. Distinguish entry, movement through the building and use at the workface in that review.

Falcon describes compact atrium access platforms designed to pass through narrow spaces and deploy at the work location. Use the offered model's documents for your actual route and accepted work positions. A narrow entry configuration is useful only when the machine also has an adequate working position inside. ## Require two transfers in the specialist offer

A crane-in package starts with a specific access machine and its manufacturer's documentation for handling that unit in the proposed state. Give the appointed lifting specialist the machine identity and building constraints. Obtain that specialist's proposal covering access to the building, the intended transfer, reception at the designated level and subsequent extraction. The building owner and relevant engineers must accept the building interfaces and supporting locations.

The practical question is whether the building at extraction will still resemble the building at entry. A roof bay might be open while glazing is installed, then sealed before ceiling commissioning is finished. A facade opening might remain accessible but the external crane position could become a landscaped area. Put the entry and extraction dates on the main works programme and identify who controls each release.

For an atrium, the machine supplier, lifting specialist and building team must each accept their own part of the proposed package. ## Price an occupied atrium, including repeat attendance

For doorway entry, count every trip through public space and any out-of-hours attendance. A compact basket might need several reaches for work a broad platform can cover in one release. Require both providers to map the same crew tasks and price their proposed sequences. The larger deck earns a premium only on work it can actually serve from the accepted positions.

For specialist entry, include mobilization for both transfers, temporary building works, support reviews, control of occupied areas, machine attendance while retained inside, and restoration. Ask the principal contractor to price a delay in the ceiling completion date. Compare the cost of keeping the opening or external position available against a separately accepted later extraction. Avoid assigning future maintenance visits to the one-time entry price.

## Two hypothetical purchases that strand the project

**Mistake: the entrance is measured before the security lobby is fitted.** A buyer accepts the small machine against the first doors shown on the shell plan. The completed lobby adds a turn and fixed gate. The machine cannot reach the atrium on its booked shift. Obtain dimensions for the building state on the actual attendance date, including the return journey.

**Mistake: the roof opening closes with the platform inside.** A bidder includes a specialist delivery during erection, while the ceiling contractor's final testing takes place after enclosure. The planned extraction window belongs to another trade. Buy the full return transfer on a protected programme date, or select a compact unit that can leave through the completed building.
