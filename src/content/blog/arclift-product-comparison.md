## ARCLIFT Product Families Overview

ARCLIFT designs and manufactures aerial work platforms across four primary product families, each engineered for specific application categories. Understanding these families and their design philosophies helps narrow your selection quickly.

**Scissor Lifts (SL Series):**

The SL Series encompasses ARCLIFT's full range of scissor lifts, from compact electric models for indoor use to large rough-terrain units for outdoor construction. Scissor lifts provide stable, vertical-only elevation with generous platform capacities. The SL Series is ARCLIFT's most diverse product family, with over 20 models spanning 15-50 feet of working height.

Key design features across the SL Series include:
- Robust steel scissor arm assemblies with hardened pivot pins
- Proportional hydraulic controls for smooth, precise platform movement
- Fold-down guardrails for low-clearance transport and storage
- Integrated diagnostic systems for simplified maintenance
- Platform capacities from 500 to 2,500 pounds

**Boom Lifts (BL Series):**

The BL Series includes both articulating and telescopic boom lifts designed for applications requiring both vertical and horizontal reach. Articulating models feature multiple arm sections that navigate around obstacles, while telescopic models maximize horizontal outreach. Working heights range from 40 to 185 feet, with platform capacities from 500 to 1,000 pounds.

The BL Series stands out for its advanced control systems, which provide intuitive operation and precise positioning at height. Features like automatic platform leveling, load-sensing capacity management, and wind speed monitoring enhance both productivity and safety. [Explore the full BL Series lineup](https://arclift.com/products/boom-lifts).

**Vertical Mast Lifts (VM Series):**

The VM Series offers compact, lightweight vertical access solutions for applications where full-size scissor lifts are impractical. These machines feature telescoping mast designs that provide 15-30 feet of working height in a package narrow enough to fit through standard doorways. Platform capacities range from 300 to 500 pounds.

VM Series lifts are particularly popular in facility maintenance, retail environments, and data centers where space is limited and flooring may have load restrictions. Their electric drive systems produce zero emissions and minimal noise, making them suitable for occupied spaces.

**Material Lifts (ML Series):**

The ML Series is designed specifically for lifting materials rather than personnel. These machines feature higher platform capacities (up to 5,000 pounds) but are not rated for personnel access. They're used for installing heavy equipment, positioning building materials, and other material-handling tasks at height.

## Key Selection Criteria: Matching Equipment to Application

Beyond the basic equipment type, several key criteria should guide your selection decision.

**Working Height vs. Platform Height:**

Working height is the maximum height a person can reach while standing on the platform ?typically 6 feet above the platform floor. Platform height is the height of the platform floor itself. Always specify based on working height, as this represents the actual functional reach of the equipment. A machine with a 40-foot platform height provides approximately 46 feet of working height.

**Platform Capacity and Size:**

Platform capacity determines how many workers and how much material the machine can safely elevate. Standard capacities range from 500 to 2,500 pounds. Remember that capacity ratings include the weight of all personnel, tools, and materials on the platform. Oversized platforms are available on many models for applications requiring more working space.

**Indoor vs. Outdoor Operation:**

Indoor applications require electric power (zero emissions), non-marking tires, and compact dimensions. Outdoor applications may require diesel power, rough-terrain capability, and wind resistance ratings. Some machines are rated for both indoor and outdoor use, but verify specific capabilities before specifying.

**Transport and Access Requirements:**

Consider how the machine will be transported to the job site and how it will access the work area. Machine width, height (stowed), weight, and ground clearance all affect transportability. Machines that can drive through standard doorways, fit on freight elevators, or be transported on standard trailers offer logistical advantages.

## FAQ: ARCLIFT Product Selection

**Q: How do I determine what working height I need for my project?**
A: Measure the height of the work surface and add 6 feet (the approximate reach height of a person standing on the platform). For example, if you need to work on a ceiling at 30 feet, you need a machine with at least 36 feet of working height. Always round up ?it's better to have slightly more height than you need than to find yourself short. [ARCLIFT's application specialists](https://arclift.com/contact) can help you calculate your specific requirements.

**Q: What's the difference between platform capacity and restricted range capacity?**
A: Some aerial platforms have different capacity ratings for different operating ranges. For example, a boom lift might be rated for 600 pounds in its full operating envelope but 1,000 pounds in a restricted range (closer to the machine's center of gravity). The restricted range capacity is useful for heavier loads but limits the machine's reach. Always check the capacity chart for your specific operating conditions.

**Q: Can I use an electric scissor lift outdoors?**
A: Many electric scissor lifts are rated for outdoor use on firm, level surfaces in good weather conditions. However, they're not designed for rough terrain, steep slopes, or adverse weather. Check the specific model's outdoor rating ?some electric models are indoor-only, while others have limited outdoor capability. For consistent outdoor use on uneven terrain, a rough-terrain model is the better choice.
