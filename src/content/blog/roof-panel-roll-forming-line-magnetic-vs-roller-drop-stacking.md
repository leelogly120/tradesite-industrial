Choose a qualified roller-drop package when required products fall outside the magnetic offer's accepted material envelope and its actual support, release and nesting trial meets the finish criteria. Shortlist magnetic handling when every assigned product is eligible and its demonstrated transport and release serve the same orders. When both qualify, select the lower complete installed and supported total for the same order mix, period and accepted removable-stack endpoint. Send the fixed product mix and stack endpoint to both providers before comparing machine cycles.

Automatic stacking is already justified in this comparison. The buyer is selecting its mechanism and integration, rather than reopening [staffed runout versus an automatic stacker](/blog/roof-panel-roll-forming-runout-table-vs-automatic-stacker/). The roof-panel products and material mix are also fixed; this is not a proposal to enter another metal market.

## First mark the materials each offer actually covers

Make one coverage row for every assigned product. Identify substrate, alloy or grade, thickness, coating and protective film where present. Attach the panel drawing and the relevant surface requirements. Similar silver appearances cannot establish the same magnetic behaviour or acceptable handling contacts.

The samples below show distinct ribs and embossed areas. Their shape helps explain the nesting question: which faces contact the support equipment and the next panel? Use the actual ordered profile and material record to answer it during the trial.

![Several separate shiny ribbed and embossed sheet samples lie on a workshop floor.](/images/equipment/buyer-20261102-embossed-sheet-sample.webp)
*Retain the actual section, nesting direction and contact faces when specifying both automatic-stacking trials.*

[ASC offers magnetic and roller-drop panel stacking systems](https://www.ascmt.com/asc-stacker-roller-drop-magnetic). Its description separately lists pull-in, tamper and side-transfer options. That establishes real equipment categories and shows why a stacker heading is not a complete outfeed scope. Obtain each actual candidate's covered products and itemised arrangement.

If a required product lies outside a magnetic proposal, compare a qualified mechanical-drop route or buy an explicitly priced compatible exception path. Do not allocate the product to an unsupported machine because the rest of the catalogue uses it. If the dedicated exception route changes the layout, staffing or total cost, carry that change into the purchase comparison.

## Read the actual contact and release mechanism

The labels describe categories, not identical designs. Distinguish the actual magnetic contact arrangement from the mechanical support-and-release arrangement in the competing offer. Ask each provider to show how its equipment supports the approaching panel, transports or locates it, releases it to the stack and clears the completed order. Keep the contact surfaces and moving components visible in that drawing.

[Metal Rollforming Systems describes magnetic and drop styles](https://mrsrollform.com/accessories/sheet-stacker/) and separately identifies a motorized discharge table transferring completed orders to packaging supports. Its description connects the stacking operation to the next physical resource. Use your offered equipment's own arrangement and supported product envelope; another maker's dimensions and cycle claims do not define it.

Magnetic handling should not be priced as automatically contact-free or drop-free. [SALICO's magnetic stacker example](https://www.salico.net/innovation-and-technology/magnetic-stacker/) is expressly described for carbon-steel sheets; it transports sheets on driven belts and releases them by gravity above the pile. That is a bounded cut-to-length example, not proof that a roof-panel profile fits. It illustrates why the actual support, stop and release boundary matters even when magnets are involved.

## Use one acceptance record for both packages

Agree the endpoint as an identified accepted stack ready for its stated removal arrangement. Wrapping, strapping and dispatch identification belong in the price only where expressly included. Hold that boundary constant while completing the record.

| Purchase input | Actual datum or record | Owner | Selection consequence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Material eligibility | Substrate/grade, thickness, coating and film by product ___ | Material and stacker providers | Excludes unsupported magnetic products |
| Panel geometry | Released section, length extremes, nesting and reference datums ___ | Product owner | Defines the same stacking objects |
| Transport and release | Provider support/contact/release drawing by product ___ | Stacker designer | Compares real mechanisms and contacts |
| Surface and edges | Accepted specimen, inspection faces and check method ___ | Quality lead | Counts accepted panels after stacking |
| Order mix | Short/long batches, lengths, stack counts and changes ___ | Planner | Tests normal and difficult orders |
| Line integration | Outlet height from line-floor datum, direction, cut timing and responsibilities ___ | Line integrator | Completes the equipment interfaces |
| Stack transfer | Stack dimensions/mass, transfer equipment and collection route ___ | Handling and dispatch leads | Makes completed stacks removable |
| Complete comparison | Same endpoint/period; installation, service and retained work ___ | Buyer and finance | Awards the lower eligible complete cost |

Specify where appearance is checked. Comparing a panel before stacking with another after collection mixes inspection boundaries. Retain material receipt observations where they help distinguish incoming marks from later contact damage, but apply the final acceptance criteria at the same stated point in both trials.

## Trial the normal awkward orders

Use actual product and order records. Include the commercially important short and long panels, nesting arrangements, small batches and changes. The provider establishes the accepted mechanism and work method. The buyer records the resources and output inside the agreed observation period.

Follow an order through stack completion and transfer, then start the next assigned order. Record attended tasks, inspections, changes and collection interruptions as well as the machine's stacking activity. A continuous run on one easy steel panel is weak evidence for mixed lengths, different finishes and frequent small orders.

For either mechanism, obtain the care, access and compatible replacement-component requirements. Price competent support and the retained work the owner actually needs. A finish benefit or staffing saving earns credit only when the representative result and complete resource plan support it.

*Place the named former, downstream interfaces and service areas on one complete factory layout.*

## Transfer can determine the winning package

Draw the formed-panel outlet, stacker, inspection position, transfer equipment, packing station and collection route. Obtain the actual occupied envelopes from their providers, including service access. Identify whether a completed stack remains in the stacking position or moves to another defined support before packing and removal.

Price any table, conveyor, supports, controls integration and destination installation separately when they sit outside the base stacker quote. Give each interface a named owner. The line integrator must reconcile the former's actual output with the selected stacker, while the handling provider accepts the stack and its removal duty.

The eligible package with the lower complete total can change after these additions. Where one offer includes a workable transfer arrangement and another stops at a pile that blocks the next order, price the missing transfer scope before ranking them. Compare both through the same accepted removable-stack endpoint, including retained attendance and the owner-approved planning period.

## Two hypothetical stacking mistakes

**Mistake (hypothetical): a steel trial is counted as aluminium coverage.** A buyer accepts a magnetic offer qualified only for its painted carbon-steel panels, then schedules aluminium through the same outfeed. Restore the actual coverage list. Obtain an accepted roller-drop or separate compatible route for that order and include its resources before awarding the package.

**Mistake (hypothetical): a neat pile is mistaken for a removable order.** The trial finishes at stack formation, but the quote excludes the discharge table and the collection vehicle arrives after the next short order must start. Price a provider-accepted transfer/holding arrangement or a feasible collection calendar. Re-run the comparison through the same removable-stack endpoint with its space and attendance funded.

## Eight attachments for the line-outfeed RFQ

1. **Panel schedule:** controlled profiles, actual materials, gauges, coatings and protective films.
2. **Geometry record:** length extremes, inspection datums, nesting direction and stack counts.
3. **Quality basis:** visible-face, edge and contact criteria with accepted samples.
4. **Order mix:** repeat/short batches, changes, calendar and assigned exceptions.
5. **Former interface:** actual configuration, outlet height from supporting-floor datum, direction, cut cycle and integration owner.
6. **Outfeed layout:** stacker, inspection/service access and measured floor/utility constraints.
7. **Stack handover:** transfer equipment, stack objects, collection route and separate packing.
8. **Quote and trial:** destination, removable-order acceptance, retained staff and full installed/support costs.
