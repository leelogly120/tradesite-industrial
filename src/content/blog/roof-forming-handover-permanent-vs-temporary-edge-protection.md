Buy permanent engineered edge protection where the owner needs repeated roof access and the project can commission the relevant protection in time for the roofing work. Select temporary collective protection where the construction sequence needs a different arrangement, and include a funded access plan for later maintenance in the owner's handover.

## List the people and work present at each stage

Draw a short timeline from the first roofing activity through completion. Identify who installs temporary or permanent protection, who receives panels, who lays the roof and which other trades need access before handover. Then add the owner's actual future tasks, such as maintaining specified plant or inspecting roof details. A finished guardrail around a completed building cannot protect workers before it exists. A temporary system removed at practical completion cannot serve a maintenance contract that begins later. Both proposals must cover their own gaps.

Great Britain's HSE guidance on roof work addresses planning for construction and maintenance and the use of suitable protective measures. Use the applicable local requirements and specialist design for the particular building. The buyer's task is to give those specialists the dates, workfaces, supporting structure, intended access and material transfers they need for a complete offer. The roof-forming lift's outlet delivers material. Price personnel access and edge protection under their own accepted duties. ## Define the panel handover before ordering the perimeter

Mark the route by which sheets reach the roof and the location and period of each receiving activity. Have the protection designer, roofing contractor and handling specialist agree how the material handover is accommodated in their designs and work plans. Do the same for transitions between roof areas, later installation of plant and removal of temporary equipment. An uninterrupted line on an architectural perimeter drawing can conceal a real project handover that still lacks an accepted arrangement. Price that interface on both routes.

Compare construction and ownership on the same time basis. For the permanent route include design integration with the roof, early temporary provision where required, installation, inspection and ongoing ownership. For the temporary route include establishment, changes with the phases, hire period, removal, making good and the owner's plan for every later task. A maintenance visit that uses a separate access system should be priced at the actual visit frequency. Do not assume an entire building perimeter needs the same solution if the specialist has designed distinct zones.

## Buy the interfaces that remain after the roofers leave

Request the documentation that moves from construction to facilities management. It should identify the installed arrangement, permitted use, inspection and maintenance responsibility, and the process for later changes to plant or access. If future equipment obstructs the expected route, the value of a permanent system can fall sharply. Conversely, an owner who plans frequent service work should know the cost of repeatedly mobilizing temporary access. Compare those actual tasks rather than assigning a general safety premium to one proposal.

**Mistake: A permanent installation starts too late.** A building owner buys a permanent system in the final roofing package. The panel crew arrives during an earlier phase when the supporting construction is incomplete. Include a specialist-designed interim provision for that interval and compare its cost alongside the permanent package before award.

**Mistake: Temporary hire ends before the final handover.** The hire period ends after the main field sheets are laid, while closures and plant installation still require protected work. The team must rebook access and make another mobilization. Align removal with all affected trades, the actual site release and the owner's first maintenance arrangement.

Award a permanent arrangement where it serves real future work and the complete construction-stage package fits the program. Otherwise buy temporary protection for the actual construction phases and procure a named later access route. A combined purchase can also make sense where the owner needs permanent protection in a service zone and the other roof fields have a different accepted maintenance strategy. Identify the boundary and responsible parties on a drawing issued with the order.
