Choose a restoration coating when the responsible roof specialist accepts the existing roof for retention and defines a complete repair, detail-treatment and coating system for it. Choose panel replacement when that assessment requires renewal or the accepted project brief selects a new roof. Compare complete accepted solutions only where both qualify. A lower coating-material price cannot make an unsuitable retained roof into a restoration candidate.

For the equipment buyer, this decision determines whether the project needs a restoration work package, new-panel supply, or separately identified zones of both. Decide those zones before booking a production slot or buying a roof-forming lift.

## Establish what the restoration purchase contains

Roof restoration is a defined treatment of an assessed roof, not a purchase of paint by roof area. The specialist identifies the existing system, condition, repair scope, relevant interfaces and the acceptance required before and after application.

[GAF's silicone-over-metal restoration information](https://www.gaf.com/en-us/roofing-materials/commercial-roofing-materials/silicone-over-metal-coatings) separates roof qualification, substrate preparation, seam and fastener treatment, penetration work and coating application. This maker example shows why the whole treatment sequence belongs in a restoration quotation. Obtain the selected provider's project specification for the actual roof.

An inspection should produce located findings and a disposition for each affected zone. Procurement needs the accepted repair quantity, the material and detail scope, the work sequence and the finished result. The responsible specialists determine how the assessment is performed and which retained conditions qualify.

If renewal is required, build the replacement offer from the released new roof system. Include removal, any required underlying work, new sheets, components, temporary protection, installation and handover. Where a new roof is placed above an existing one, use the separate [overcladding versus tear-off comparison](/blog/metal-roof-overcladding-vs-tear-off-forming-lift-package/); that changes the assembly rather than restoring its existing weathering surface.

## Require a zone decision that can be purchased

A report saying “generally suitable” leaves a buyer without construction quantities. Ask the appointed roof specialist to identify the retained zones, repaired interfaces, replaced pieces and exclusions affecting completion. Use a dated drawing and a quantity schedule that the contractor can price.

Where selective sheet replacement precedes coating, define the replacement product and its interface with the selected treatment. The designer and treatment provider control compatibility. Keep the replacement order visible even when it is a small part of the restoration contract.

The facility operator also has a purchasing role. Record the actual areas released, activities that must continue, protection and handback requirements. A roof crew's access period and an equipment supplier's hire or ownership period should follow the accepted programme, including returns for inspection or completion.

## Fill in the whole-roof comparison

Prepare this table after the condition decision. Compare the same required building result and separate any scope that appears in only one alternative.

| Purchase field | Project entry to complete | Responsible owner | How it changes the choice |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Existing roof | Record system identity, assessment reference and located findings ___ | Roof specialist | Establishes eligible retained zones |
| Accepted outcome | Enter restoration or replacement design and zone drawing ___ | Roof designer and building owner | Fixes what the project buys |
| Restoration scope | List repairs, detail treatments, application and inspections ___ | Treatment provider | Prices a complete retained-roof solution |
| Replacement scope | Enter removal, underlying work, new sheets and components ___ | Roofing contractor | Prices a complete renewed-roof solution |
| Material quantities | Record accepted repair lengths, replacement pieces and treatment areas ___ | Surveyor and contractor | Prevents roof area becoming every trade quantity |
| Work interfaces | Name access, protection, handling and stage-release parties ___ | Main contractor | Allocates resources outside material supply |
| Production requirement | Enter new profile, length mix, quantities and delivery dates ___ | Procurement lead | Tests bought panels against own forming |
| Finished-project cost | Enter complete works, occupation, inspection and handover totals ___ | Commercial lead | Ranks the qualifying alternatives |

Include the cost of finding and resolving conditions left open by an initial survey. Allocate that investigation to a named specialist and a dated decision point. Price the resulting construction scope after release; do not bury an undefined roof-condition obligation inside a fixed equipment allowance.

## Make the new-panel quantity earn the production equipment

A restoration-led project often retains most sheets, but the actual assessment controls its replacement quantity. Obtain a finished-panel price for the released balance. Compare that price and delivery route with owned forming using the balance plus supported repeat orders.

For a large replacement programme, production location becomes a meaningful package choice. Factory-made panels bring a delivery and receiving task. Site forming brings equipment mobilisation, coil handling, workspace and output receiving. A roof-level former adds specific support and outlet interfaces. Compare the full route to accepted installed sheets.

A forming purchase should retain value after this refurbishment. Record the compatible future profile families and quantities supported by orders, rather than assuming every later roof can use one tooling set. For a one-off balance, bought panels keep the project focused on the accepted restoration and installation work.

## Two hypothetical refurbishment failures

**Failure case: coating is bought before the roof is accepted for retention.** A facilities buyer orders application by total roof area, then the specialist assessment requires replacement in several zones and additional detail repairs. Separate qualification from construction procurement and issue the released zone schedule before awarding the complete work.

**Failure case: replacement equipment is sized from the whole building.** A contractor buys production equipment for the full roof although the accepted scope replaces only isolated panels before restoration. Recalculate new-sheet demand and obtain a finished-supply alternative. Retain the former investment only when actual replacement and repeat orders justify it.
