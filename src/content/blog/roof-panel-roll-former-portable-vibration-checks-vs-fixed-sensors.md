Choose portable vibration checks when trained staff can take repeatable measurements at approved points during production and the route leaves time to diagnose a change and schedule a repair. Buy fixed sensors only at critical points when the portable route misses the required sampling interval or production window, or when there is no approved in-service access. Require a usable signal in the relevant running state and a named responder. Start by marking each candidate assembly and its normal running state on the drive drawing, then have maintenance estimate the interruption a failure would cause.

Use the row of forming stands below to picture how a point map works. Number the assembly, then label the exact housing and measurement direction on its drawing so a reading can be compared after service.

## Spend on components that can stop accepted output

Start with the main drive and any bearing or gearbox assembly whose failure interrupts a contracted order and leaves no timely production fallback. Ask the machine supplier to identify the actual assemblies and the maintenance lead to estimate the interruption from their repair plan. Assign each candidate a specific reason to measure. A hard-to-source drive reducer can justify closer surveillance than an accessible assembly whose replacement fits a scheduled stop.

Periodic data collection follows a defined route. Fixed systems collect readings from installed points. Either way, spotting a change and diagnosing its cause require different work. Include qualified interpretation in the monitoring price. [SKF condition-monitoring overview](https://www.skf.com/group/products/condition-monitoring-systems)

Start the point schedule from the machine's actual transmission drawing. It identifies the drive path and components whose housings the analyst must distinguish. [Roll-former transmission buying guide](/blog/roof-panel-roll-former-chain-vs-geared-transmission/)

If a walkaround covers accessible, stable-duty points, price its labor and analysis across all machines sharing the instrument. Use a fixed system where it covers a named gap, such as a critical drive that only runs in short order windows the maintenance route routinely misses. Ask the provider to state the actual collection interval and what is captured in that interval. A fixed sensor that records on a schedule and a continuous acquisition system can cover different events.

## Make the signal comparable across orders

A panel line can run different profile setups and speeds. Log the repeatable state in which a bearing's trend is evaluated, including profile or recipe, shaft speed or approved speed tag, and whether material is running. Discuss starts, cutting events and nearby equipment with the analyst before freezing the baseline plan. [SKF's batch-process application note](https://skftechnicalsupport.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/360032624454-On-line-Surveillance-Monitoring-in-Continuous-and-Batch-Process-Applications) explains why condition evaluation uses readings collected in identifiable, repeatable operating states.

Require the specialist to specify which defect family the proposal covers and what measurement supports it at the actual speed. A rising overall vibration value flags a change. The proposal should state which underlying measurement records the analyst will receive to investigate it, including spectrum or waveform data if required for the named fault. Agree how to reset or retain the baseline after a bearing, gearbox or tooling change. Keep the production team’s separate finished-panel checks in the release plan.

## Prove the handoff before buying extra channels

Request an acceptance demonstration using the proposed points and a normal repeat production state. For portable collection, have the appointed provider show repeatable readings from approved positions and a usable report. For fixed collection, ask the integrator and analyst to show state identification, data import and routing of a test notification to the named reviewer without creating a real machinery fault. Price mounting, installation, specialist analysis, software, and recurring upkeep over the same term for both offers. Assign custody and export rights for trend history. If a critical point cannot be measured at an approved position in a useful state with either package, compare a supplier-supported inspection or planned replacement route instead of buying that channel.

The upright shafts and collars beside the line frame show the kinds of components that need separate identities in the parts record. Give the analyst a route to the person who can plan work on the specific assembly flagged by a reading.

## Two hypothetical ways the purchase fails

**Mistake 1.** A dashboard compares slow narrow-profile runs with faster wide-profile orders. The alert changes each time the schedule changes, and maintenance starts ignoring it. Make the repeatable running conditions and data labels part of acceptance.

**Mistake 2.** A portable route specifies a bearing location that the supplier has never approved for in-service access. The company discovers that the scheduled running measurement cannot be collected under its approved work arrangements. Ask for a validated accessible point or price fixed measurement there before award.
