Invest in mineral-wool production when committed accepted orders require that panel family and fund its board preparation, bonding, handling and product-release resources. Invest in PIR production when the supported orders justify its foaming and materials-service plant for a qualified panel range. When either qualified family serves the same committed orders, choose the lower complete cost of filling them over the owner's stated period, including purchased exceptions. First separate core-specific orders; one total for insulated panels cannot justify either factory indiscriminately.

This decision sits inside an already justified owned continuous sandwich-panel business. The fork is the core-specific product range and complete process to operate. A conventional sheet former remains one metal-facing stage within that larger purchase.

## Divide the order book before dividing the plant budget

Build separate schedules for the actual PIR and mineral-wool panel families. Identify accepted facings, joint details, dimensions, required product evidence and dispatch dates. Keep orders tied to the product the customer accepts rather than moving them between columns to improve a machine's apparent utilisation.

[Ruukki's roof-panel range](https://www.ruukki.com/building-envelopes/products/roof-structure/sandwich-panels-for-roofs) includes both PIR and mineral-wool products. That establishes real saleable families, not interchangeable performance. The customer's requirements and the actual tested panel assembly determine which products belong in the funded programme.

Include qualification and market-entry work for a new range. For an established range, retain its actual product records and process requirements. Define the whole panel, including facings and joints.

The entry photograph below shows strip entering shaped rollers. Identify that metal-forming output on the process diagram, then follow the separately supplied core and assembly work through to the accepted finished panel. The investment boundary extends beyond the facing geometry.

## PIR production commits the business to a foaming process

A PIR project needs the actual specialist process proposal and its complete materials-to-output scope. [KINDUS identifies a PU/PIR foaming stage](https://kindus.com/products/sandwich-panel-line/continuous-pu-pir-sandwich-panel-line/) alongside facing preparation, double-belt processing, cutting, cooling and finished handling in its continuous-line category. These stations explain why a facing former plus a conveyor is an incomplete PIR plant budget.

Obtain the actual plant provider's material supply, storage, conditioning, utility and operating-resource requirements. Assign facilities work and the responsible qualified parties before ranking the investment. Buy the provider's supported process and operating handover; procurement should not derive chemical recipes or process settings from a machinery comparison.

The business case also needs a route for product release. Include the required trial, inspections, records and accountable release role for the accepted panel family. Budget accepted output through the agreed dispatch endpoint, including the handling and packing resources actually required by the proposal.

## Mineral-wool production commits different core resources

The mineral-wool route needs its specified board preparation and feed, bonding and laminating resources beside the metal facings. [KINDUS's mineral-wool line](https://kindus.com/products/sandwich-panel-line/mineral-wool-sandwich-panel-line/) names board feeding and adhesive stages within its complete process. Its displayed configuration also includes a separate side-PU stage for the products it describes. A mineral-wool core should therefore not be budgeted as a universally chemical-free manufacturing operation.

Use the proposed panel and process to specify stations, materials and services. Obtain their complete installed scope. Retain consumables, material preparation, rejected output and finished handling in the operating model.

The comparison is not won by deleting the other route's most visible station. A wool process has its own material movement, bonding, quality and dispatch obligations. Price those duties through the same accepted-output boundary as the PIR case.

## Put each whole factory through the same investment worksheet

[Hennecke's continuous metal-faced panel category](https://www.hennecke.com/en/loesungen/nach-maschine-anlage/sandwichpanel-anlagen/kontinuierliche-sandwichpanel-anlagen) covers PUR/PIR or mineral-wool core structures and integrated production through packaging. Use that complete boundary when comparing actual provider proposals. Count shared facing equipment once within each alternative, then retain the different core-process resources explicitly.

| Investment input and datum | Fillable project entry | Responsible owner | Decision use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Qualified product range | Core, joint, facing and product-drawing revisions ___ | Product authority | Establishes saleable output |
| Supported demand | Named accepted orders, core family, quantities and dispatch dates ___ | Commercial lead | Separates shared orders from core-specific revenue |
| PIR scope | Materials, foaming and downstream process references ___ | PIR plant provider | Completes the chemical-process purchase |
| Mineral-wool scope | Board preparation, adhesive and laminating references ___ | Wool plant provider | Completes the different core process |
| Facing interface | Section, material, finish and handover drawing ___ | Product/process integrator | Defines retained metal-forming supply |
| Operating factory | Layout, utilities, materials zones and premises-work records ___ | Facilities and process engineers | Prices an operable installed plant |
| Product release | Trials, tests, inspection and dispatch acceptance ___ | Quality/product lead | Funds accepted finished output |
| Complete business case | Installed/operating totals, purchased exceptions, period and owner test ___ | Buyer and finance lead | Compares common-order cost or separate investment cases |

For orders accepted in either qualified family, compare lower complete fulfilment cost over the same period and dispatch endpoint. For core-specific orders, test each separate demand schedule against the owner's stated investment criterion; they are different revenue cases. State which committed orders each investment serves and which work remains bought externally. Where a customer accepts either actual product, keep the same required delivery and documented acceptance outcome in that portion of the comparison.

## Shared metal equipment does not create shared core capability

A proposed facing-forming line needs the actual section, material and finished condition at its handover. Have the complete plant integrator define its interface with the downstream process and who accepts the resulting facing. Similar-looking roof ribs should not replace that controlled product definition.

The formed sheet below lies over rows of rollers. Its visible profile illustrates the facing-output boundary. Finished bonded-panel acceptance also includes the selected core, joint and composite-product records. Keep those separate outputs visible in the equipment schedule and payment for supplied scope.

![A long silver profiled sheet rests across rows of shaped rollers beside a grey cabinet in a workshop.](/images/equipment/buyer-20261101-profiled-sheet.webp)
*Price formed metal output separately from the finished bonded panel released for dispatch.*

Include factory space for the complete proposed material route, not just the common metal equipment. The process provider supplies equipment and operating requirements; the facilities team prices the actual building and utility work. Allocate staff, material custody, service and quality duties to the business that will operate the plant.

An occasional exceptional core family does not automatically justify a second full process. Obtain an accepted finished-panel supply quotation for that work and carry it beside the retained internal range. Keep prospective sales separate from committed demand when testing whether the core investment earns its complete resources.

## Two hypothetical factory budgets that count the wrong output

**Mistake (hypothetical): PIR contracts fund a wool-only programme.** A factory includes its signed PIR warehouse-roof orders in a wool-line utilisation forecast, although those customers have accepted only the specified PIR assembly. Remove them from internal wool demand or price an accepted outside supply. Rebuild demand by qualified core and joint family.

**Mistake (hypothetical): the wool quote stops before core preparation.** A machinery estimate covers facing formers and a laminator but omits board preparation, adhesive resources and product-release work. The competing PIR quotation includes its complete process through dispatch. Obtain the missing installed and operating scopes before comparing the factories; a lower machine subtotal cannot fund those duties.

## Eight RFQ attachments for the facing-forming proposal

1. Released metal-facing section, joint interface and drawing revision.
2. Facing metal grade, gauge, coating and required width.
3. Accepted facing quantities, length mix and product-family schedule.
4. Coil-to-discharge layout and downstream integrator's handover condition.
5. Forming-stage footprint, utilities and material-handling responsibilities.
6. Named specialist core-process provider and excluded supply boundaries.
7. Facing sample, dimensions, surface and handover acceptance record.
8. Required delivery configuration, production dates and destination.
