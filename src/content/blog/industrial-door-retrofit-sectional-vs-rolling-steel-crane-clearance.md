**Choose rolling steel when no offered sectional track clears the crane's full operating envelope and the accepted rolling assembly fits the header, jambs and service approach. Choose sectional when its track and operator stay clear throughout crane travel and the door meets the required duty. If both qualify, choose the lower combined quote for removal, installation and one future service visit. Issue a measured bay section with the crane's full operating envelope to both bidders. Shortlist ARC-F25 for adjacent crew positions only where the reviewed bay and floor allow it.**

Reserve an isolated work window for elevated door work. Use the full crane envelope to test the clearance of the returned bay. Give each bidder the opening elevation, a section into the building and the work positions for removal, installation and service.

The yard photograph shows a raised deck facing a factory wall with several openings. Give each opening its own ID and side clearances. A platform assigned to a bay on the programme still needs a usable setup position beside that opening.

## Find the space each door would occupy

A standard-lift sectional door follows vertical tracks before moving horizontally into the bay. [Clopay's commercial door information](https://www.clopaydoor.com/commercial-door-products/extreme) also describes high-lift variants with a longer vertical track section and full-vertical variants that keep movement up the wall. Request the exact track and operator drawing. High-lift tracks still need room where they turn into the bay. Full-vertical tracks need upper wall space and support for the standing door. Ask the supplier to price a qualifying variant before discarding all sectional options because the standard track clashes.

A rolling steel curtain coils at the opening. [Overhead Door's Model 610 information](https://www.overheaddoor.com/commercial/commercial-details/rolling-steel-service-doors-610) illustrates the curtain, wall-mounted guides, hood and face- or jamb-mounted drawing options. [Cookson's rolling door component guide](https://www.cooksondoor.com/architect-tools-and-resources/rolling-door-101) shows how the shaft, brackets and guides belong in the complete assembly. Compare the actual hood or coil envelope, guides and drive location in the selected shop drawing. Ask the provider to show available left- and right-side drive options if only one side has a usable service approach. Include the space needed to approach and service these parts after the new door is in use.

Ask the door designer and building specialists to compare open, moving and closed positions with the measured crane travel and the required passage. Use the crane operator's full operating envelope and the owner’s nominated vehicle dimensions. A photograph with the crane parked shows only one position. Require a complete product offer for the accepted cycles, exposure and operating brief before comparing access savings.

## Reserve a bay that carries on working

The facility operator and crane owner approve the isolated bay possession under the site's procedures. They identify which routes close, when the contractor receives the work area and when the bay returns to production. Ask the door contractor for the old components to be removed, the new components to be presented and each retained personnel task. Keep removal and new-component handling with the competent contractor. The access quotation should identify the people, tool duties and allowed task items on the proposed platform by visit.

Where one opening supports regular dispatch, price the required temporary closure or alternate vehicle route before award. Ask the door specialist to name the checks and repairs that require a later access visit and whether the necessary area is released during normal operations. Ask each bidder to price the same return visit, such as its first scheduled check of the drive and upper hardware. Award the lower combined retrofit and service quote if both offers retain the required passage and crane duty.

The interior photograph shows where the lowered equipment sits relative to the red overhead beam. Use the actual route sketch and bay section to agree when the machine can enter the area, where it stands, and when the door contractor releases the bay back to production.

## Two door retrofit mistakes that cost another shutdown

**Mistake (hypothetical).** A buyer lets the sectional bidder use its standard track while the crane is parked at the opposite end of the bay. The track occupies part of the crane's required travel. Give each bidder the full operating envelope and ask for a specific compatible layout before committing the opening.

**Mistake (hypothetical).** A rolling proposal fits the opening and coil above it, but its drive service face lands beside a permanent guard. When the actuator needs care, the quote assumes a floor position unavailable in the finished bay. Ask the specialist to show the service side and reserve or separately price an acceptable access arrangement.

## Door retrofit RFQ for the occupied bay

1. Measured opening schedule and the required vehicle passage.
2. Crane operating envelope, structural grid and nearby fixed services.
3. Actual sectional track or rolling-door drawings and drive sides.
4. Retained walls, supports and specialist-approved building interface scope.
5. Removal and delivery object lists with separate handling responsibilities.
6. Crew and tool schedules and personnel work positions from local floor and opening face.
7. Entry route, floor records, setup areas, blackout dates and service windows.
8. Destination, delivery and withdrawal scope and handback requirements.
