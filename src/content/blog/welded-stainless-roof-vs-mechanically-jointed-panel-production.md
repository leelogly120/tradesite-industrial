Choose a continuously welded stainless roof package only when the designer specifies or accepts that complete system and the bid includes qualified sheet preparation, specialist joining and project acceptance. Retain a mechanically jointed roof package when it satisfies the design duty and its authorized components, installer and evidence can be supplied together.

## Buy the joint and the details as part of the roof

Ask each bidding team to name the forming, joining and acceptance specialists for the roof it proposes. A similar raised outline leaves these responsibilities unresolved. Start the comparison at the completed roof. Identify its intended service, supporting construction, metal, joint architecture, terminations, penetrations and interfaces with adjoining work. Ask the designer which alternatives the project actually permits. A material or joining change can affect more than the main field seam. Obtain an accepted detail set and a responsibility schedule for each option before asking for panel prices.

Euro Inox describes continuously welded stainless standing seams using specialist resistance seam welding equipment. That establishes a genuine roofing process distinct from making a formed strip and mechanically completing an accepted seam. The equipment and specialist competences for each stage require separate identification. For the welded proposal, ask who prepares and forms the material, who delivers the approved joining package and who accepts the completed details. For the mechanical proposal obtain the system owner's exact panel section, clips, accessories and joint-completion requirements. ## Give both bidders the same finished roof area

Use the same support conditions, penetrations, edge length and installation dates. Include short returns and interruptions to the main runs. A quoted area rate for simple parallel field seams can miss most of the specialist attendance on a small roof crowded with details. Conversely, a large clear field can account for most of the reusable production work. Split the bill into repeat strip, boundary conditions, special pieces, joining, inspection and reinstatement after later trades.

Check the production proposal against actual metal and geometry. A former proven on a coated carbon-steel panel has evidence for that product. Request representative material, trial sections, inspection of the formed edges and the intended joining specialist's acceptance for the stainless system. Agree who pays for trials, tooling changes and material lost during acceptance. A supplier can form one portion of the roof while a specialist supplies details or even all the field joining. Name each handover and its output in the order.

## Test whether buying machinery leaves a sellable scope

Build a short order book for each supported system. Count only jobs whose specified roof, material, production authorization and installer can be provided. A signed job requiring a welded system contributes no mechanically jointed panel sale unless the designer and purchaser accept the alternative. When a welded-roof specialist contracts for accepted preformed strips, credit the producer only with the margin on those strips and assign the installation revenue to the specialist. Ask which party retains the revenue, equipment and liability for the repetitive forming work.

**Mistake: The formed edge is accepted by the shop alone.** A buyer funds tooling after inspecting an attractive trial piece. The roofing specialist has never accepted it for the specified joining system, and a detail revision changes the edge. Submit a traceable sample to the parties responsible for the completed joint and obtain the agreed acceptance before funding serial tooling.

**Mistake: A straight seam quotation is applied to the whole building.** The contractor obtains a price for repetitive joining and multiplies it by roof area. The drawings contain many penetrations and ends with distinct interfaces. Request a detail schedule, specialist price and inspection obligations before comparing that package with the complete mechanically jointed alternative.

Choose between the released assembly offers on completed roof cost, credible delivery and future obligations. Decide on the roll former separately using the material and eligible work it will actually produce. If the welded work is a single specialist project, a commissioned strip supply can serve it without committing the business to a new product family. If repeat accepted orders exist, include representative joining acceptance in the machine purchase test.

## Eight RFQ entries for a complete joining proposal

1. Attach accepted roof system alternatives and the designer's duty.
2. Provide exact material, strip condition and panel section drawings.
3. List roof area, run lengths and details by zone.
4. Identify forming, joining, installation and inspection parties.
5. Define specialist approval of representative formed material.
6. List closures, penetrations, boundaries and separately supplied components.
7. Request comparable completed assembly prices and schedules.
8. Identify the recurring panel-only orders supporting machinery investment.
