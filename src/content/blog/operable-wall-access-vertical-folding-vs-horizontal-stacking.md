Choose vertical folding when the required room layouts cannot surrender side storage and the accepted ceiling pocket and machinery fit. Choose horizontal stacking when its tracks and parking arrangement serve the required panel layouts or the ceiling excludes a folding package. When both qualify, choose the lower complete cost of installation and later access over the same stated period. Shortlist ARC-F25 for repeated adjacent head, track and pocket workfaces that fit its actual configuration; retain separate access for exceptions. First mark the wall states and service faces on the measured room section, then price the wall and personnel-equipment packages.

## Buy the room arrangements, then locate the overhead work

Hold the accepted acoustic and finish requirements, opening, required room divisions and completion date constant. The acoustic designer and wall providers establish which complete assemblies qualify. A headline sound rating does not establish the performance of the room, its adjoining construction or an alternative wall family.

Two operable walls closing the same opening still leave different installation objects. One raises its panels into the ceiling. The other moves panels along a selected suspension layout into a parking position. Identify the required deployed and stored arrangements before reducing personnel equipment to a single ceiling-height allowance.

[Skyfold's Classic Premium brochure](https://skyfold.com/files/bb96c7c0-f255-11f0-bdff-9741529f1ad6) identifies ceiling storage and closure panels. [Modernfold's single-panel systems](https://www.modernfold.com/en-US/products/operable-partitions/single-panel-systems) use specified suspension-track options. These are actual architecture examples. The purchased wall remains the particular assembly accepted for the building.

## A ceiling pocket creates a construction handover

The folding route purchases more than a moving panel surface. Obtain the actual support, hanger, drive-unit, pocket and adjoining acoustic-barrier scope, with a named provider for each. Record the installation state in which the drive and its service faces remain accessible.

[Skyfold's generic Classic Premium mechanical drawing](https://skyfold.com/document/1f68b190-f540-11f0-826c-c1b1a07f5297?open=true) identifies drive installation before pocket construction and shows access requirements beside the motor. It also warns against obstructions to the pocket and lifting paths. Use the issued project drawings for the selected wall; the generic details are a reason to price those interfaces, rather than build-to dimensions.

Put pocket construction and acoustic enclosure on the personnel-equipment programme. Identify the overhead work before closure, the retained service access afterwards and the contractor who accepts the transition. A platform released after panel installation does not automatically cover later enclosure finishing or a machinery visit.

Keep removable access provisions in the finished-room contract. A ceiling drawing showing a neat closed surface is insufficient if the agreed service position becomes inaccessible. Price the owner's actual future attendance and access commitments over the comparison period, using the wall provider's documented requirements.

## A horizontal stack distributes work along the selected track

For the stacking route, obtain the actual panel arrangement, suspension tracks, branches where specified and parking layout. Identify the support and enclosure work supplied with the wall and the work left to the building contractor. Do not price an illustrative straight track when the room brief requires another panel arrangement.

[Modernfold's Track Systems brochure](https://www.modernfold.com/en-US/document/7155b980-3d93-11f1-8cb3-fb7b447b80f2?open=true) distinguishes track families and panel configurations. Use the selected proposal to locate its continuous headwork, changes in direction, stacking bay and service points. The access period follows those located tasks rather than the closed-wall length alone. A specified multidirectional track serves a different panel route from a straight arrangement. Keep its selected track family and compatible panel system together in the quotation instead of adding an illustrative branch to a cheaper straight-track offer.

Check the approach beside the parking area as well as the main opening. An accepted stored-panel footprint still consumes room space. Installation staging, the personnel machine and specialist handling require their own space during the works. Show each on the same construction-stage drawing.

## Complete the room-to-workface purchase record

| Input | Fillable datum | Responsible owner | Purchase decision |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Required divisions | Layout IDs ___; opening/room datum ___ | Owner and acoustic designer | Admits the required room arrangements |
| Actual assembly | Product, finish and acoustic revision ___ | Wall provider and designer | Establishes the complete eligible wall |
| Folding overhead package | Pocket/motor/hanger/service coordinates ___; section revision ___ | Folding-wall provider and surveyor | Defines pocket space and personnel headwork |
| Stacking package | Track/branch/parking layout ___; support interfaces ___ | Stacking-wall provider | Defines distributed installation and storage space |
| Enclosure handover | Drive/track release ___; acoustic/pocket closure dates ___ | Main contractor and installer | Books pre-closure work and later access |
| Personnel duty | Height/offset ___ from setup-floor ID ___; crew count ___; tool/object quantities and masses ___ | Installer and access supplier | Tests the actual shared-deck task |
| Ground and handling | Entry/turn/setup dimensions ___; floor/support report revision ___; handling area ___ | Surveyor and appointed specialists | Tests equipment together without merging their duties |
| Complete award | Installed price ___; service period/visits ___; access/reopening cost ___ | Buyer and owner | Compares the same room result and period |

Require the same service period in both totals. An installation-only price and an offer including later access and agreed service attendance are different purchases. Record the actual inclusions and separately priced work, then compare the completed qualifying packages.

## Purchase the people positions independently of wall lifting

A shared deck warrants comparison where repeated adjacent head, pocket or track work needs concurrent crew and tools. A remote service point, obstructed return or isolated parking-bay detail needs its own equipment response. Assign those positions explicitly rather than crediting the main platform with every task because the wall is tall.

![A yellow personnel-access frame stands beside tall wall framing, with workers at different levels and rough ground below.](/images/equipment/buyer-20261029-wall-installation.webp)

*Keep wall faces and the base-support area in the same measured installation brief.*

Use the actual supporting-floor datum for heights and offsets. Give the access provider the complete crew, tool and permitted-object inventory, entry route and support records. The provider's proposed deck arrangement, task conditions and reactions then enter the responsible project review. Wall machinery, panels and their handling resources stay in the specialist scope.

## Two hypothetical wall-package mistakes

**Mistake (hypothetical): ceiling storage is bought without the enclosure handover.** A buyer preserves the room sides with a folding wall but closes the acoustic pocket before the drive-unit work and omits later access openings. Price the issued pre-closure work and retain its service faces in the finished-room contract before releasing the personnel equipment.

**Mistake (hypothetical): one straight opening stands for every room layout.** A stacking-wall offer closes the opening but excludes the branch and parking arrangement needed for another required division. Obtain the accepted layout, complete its support and track price, and locate the resulting crew positions before ranking either wall route.

## Eight RFQ attachments for the retained personnel platform

3. Simultaneous people, tools, permitted objects, dimensions/masses and their task positions.
4. Entry, turning, transport and setup drawings including overhead obstructions and parked-wall envelopes.
5. Floor/support records and the configuration-specific equipment reactions required by the structural reviewer.
6. Component delivery and separate specialist handling layout with reserved personnel work zones.
7. Drive/track installation, acoustic enclosure, commissioning and continuing service-access dates.
8. Destination, site power, transport/storage boundary and requested deck geometry, simultaneous crew/tool/object limits, support reactions and handover records.
