**Buy a central extraction system when the welding layout is stable, the ventilation specialist can demonstrate effective source capture at the simultaneous workstations and the permanent duct route is serviceable. Buy suitable mobile source-extraction units for changing positions when trial evidence shows effective control for the actual welds and the units can follow the work.** If neither proposal controls exposure as required, have the responsible specialist redesign the control package before comparing access costs.

The equipment decision begins at the weld. A filter in a central plant room and a wheeled unit beside a bench both depend on a capture arrangement that works for the process and position. A high-level fan simply moving air around a hall is a different proposal. Have the occupational hygiene or ventilation lead define the required control outcome and commissioning evidence.

## Follow a representative weld through the shift

Record the process, material, workpiece movement and welding position at each station. Include the busiest credible overlap between stations and the production change that moves the work to another bay. The UK [HSE guidance on welding fume controls](https://www.hse.gov.uk/welding/protect-your-workers/avoid-reduce-exposure.htm) discusses local exhaust ventilation and additional protection where required. The employer and its specialist should first assess whether they can avoid the exposure or reduce the work that creates it. Where local extraction alone leaves insufficient control, include suitable respiratory protection and any other measures identified by the assessment in the complete proposal.

For a central proposal, obtain the branch and capture arrangement for each weld position, the permitted simultaneous operation and the named party responsible for performance at the point of use. Obtain a maintenance access schedule for duct inspection, filter handling, fan service and testing. Locate the collection plant and show where replacement filters and waste go. The buyer needs the priced, commissioned network instead of a fan capacity figure detached from its branches.

For a mobile proposal, ask for a trial that includes the least convenient routine work position. [Plymovent's MobilePro-W3 data sheet](https://www.plymovent.com/sites/default/files/2022-11/PDS_MobilePro-W3_EN.pdf) describes a mobile welding-fume extraction product. The responsible specialist still needs to assess the intended process, discharge and workplace conditions for the proposed unit. Account for the number of separate tasks running together and the space to position each unit, arm or approved on-tool connection.

## Cost effective stations rather than installed hardware

Compare the complete cost of serving the same active weld stations during the same shifts. For a fixed network include design, supports, building penetrations, ducts, plant, controls, high-level work, commissioning and planned maintenance. Credit relocation flexibility only for positions the design actually covers. A central branch serving an abandoned cell does little for a job moved elsewhere in the hall.

For mobile units include the required simultaneous fleet, any additional supply or exhaust provisions, filter changes, relocation labour, training and inspection. Price changes to production space caused by the units or hose routes. Some workshops make a stronger case by installing a central system for repeat bays and providing separately accepted mobile coverage for occasional remote work. Include both systems' maintenance effort if choosing that arrangement.

## Two mistakes that underprice control

**Mistake one — dividing a single successful demonstration across every station.** A mobile trial worked beside one accessible bench. The production lead then assigns the same unit to two concurrent welds in different bays. Repeat the schedule with a named control method at each live task and price any missing units or fixed branches.

**Mistake two — placing a filter bank where it cannot be serviced.** A central bid fits inside the plant space on plan, but a later mezzanine removes the path for replacement components. Have the maintainer mark the access and removal envelope before purchase. Include revised location, servicing access and waste handling in the quote.
