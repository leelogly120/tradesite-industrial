## The AWP Market in 2026

The global aerial work platform market continues to grow, driven by infrastructure spending, urbanization, and the shift away from scaffolding toward mobile elevated work platforms (MEWPs). In 2026, the market is estimated at $14 billion, with Asia-Pacific leading both production and consumption.

The top 10 manufacturers represent a mix of established Western brands and fast-growing Chinese manufacturers. Each serves different segments of the market — from general construction to specialized ceiling work.

## 1. JLG Industries (USA)

JLG, a subsidiary of Oshkosh Corporation, remains one of the largest AWP manufacturers globally. Their product line spans boom lifts, scissor lifts, and telehandlers. JLG's strength is their dealer network and parts availability across North America and Europe.

**Best known for:** Boom lifts, scissor lifts, large fleet rental market.

## 2. Genie (Terex AWP)

Genie, part of Terex, is another dominant force in the boom lift and scissor lift market. Their S-65 and S-85 telescopic booms are industry standards. Genie focuses on reliability and total cost of ownership.

**Best known for:** Telescopic boom lifts, electric scissor lifts.

## 3. Haulotte Group (France)

Haulotte is Europe's largest manufacturer of aerial work platforms. They produce a full range of boom lifts, scissor lifts, and vertical mast lifts. Haulotte has been investing heavily in electric and hybrid power systems.

**Best known for:** European market presence, hybrid power systems, compact booms.

## 4. Manitou Group (France)

Manitou specializes in rough-terrain material handling and aerial work platforms. Their rotating telehandlers double as boom lifts. Manitou's strength is in construction and industrial applications where terrain access matters.

**Best known for:** Rotating telehandlers, rough-terrain platforms.

## 5. Zoomlion (China)

Zoomlion is one of China's largest heavy equipment manufacturers. Their aerial work platform division produces boom lifts, scissor lifts, and truck-mounted platforms. Zoomlion has been aggressively expanding into export markets.

**Best known for:** Large boom lifts, price competitiveness, export growth.

## 6. XCMG (China)

XCMG is China's largest construction equipment manufacturer. Their AWP line includes boom lifts up to 50m and scissor lifts for indoor use. XCMG benefits from massive scale and domestic market dominance.

**Best known for:** High-reach boom lifts, scale manufacturing, domestic Chinese market.

## 7. Sinoboom (China)

Sinoboom focuses exclusively on aerial work platforms — boom lifts, scissor lifts, and spider lifts. They've grown rapidly through competitive pricing and expanding product lines. Sinoboom now operates manufacturing in both China and Europe.

**Best known for:** Scissor lifts, competitive pricing, European expansion.

## 8. AIRO (Italy)

AIRO specializes in trailer-mounted and self-propelled boom lifts. They serve the European rental market with a focus on easy transport and quick setup. AIRO's compact booms are popular with contractors who move equipment between job sites frequently.

**Best known for:** Trailer-mounted booms, compact designs.

## 9. MEC (USA)

MEC produces a focused range of boom lifts and scissor lifts for the North American market. They differentiate through build quality and customer service. MEC's lifts are known for durability and low maintenance costs.

**Best known for:** Boom lifts, build quality, North American market.

## How to Choose

The "best" manufacturer depends on your specific application:

- **General construction boom lift:** JLG, Genie, Haulotte
- **Indoor scissor lift:** Genie, Sinoboom, Zoomlion
- **Rough terrain access:** Manitou, XCMG
- **Ceiling-level work:** ARCLIFT FDDPT series
- **Budget-conscious project:** Zoomlion, Sinoboom, XCMG

Don't choose based on brand name alone. Match the platform's specifications to your project's requirements — height, load, power system, ground pressure, and maneuverability.
