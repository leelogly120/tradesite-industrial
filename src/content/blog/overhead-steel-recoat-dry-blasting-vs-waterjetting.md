Choose dry abrasive preparation when the accepted coating specification requires a new metal roughness profile and the facility can accept the complete containment and shutdown arrangement. Choose water-only jetting when the coating specialist accepts the retained profile and the contractor can deliver the required capture, disposal and surface acceptance. Compare the complete qualifying methods through inspected preparation and facility handback, before assigning personnel access.

This decision assumes a full overhead protective-steel recoat has already been selected. The question is which specialist preparation contract to buy. A low cleaning rate per square metre is useful only after the method can produce the required substrate and the quotation includes the building protection needed to finish the work.

## Apply the profile gate before requesting a cleaning price

[Hempel's wet surface-preparation guideline](https://www.hempel.com/en-us/-/media/Hempel/Files/Technical-Guidelines/Surface-Preparation/Wet-surface-preparation-methods.pdf) explains a decisive distinction: removing old coatings, rust and soluble salts with water alone does not create a new metal roughness profile. Existing profile condition therefore matters. Wet abrasive preparation uses abrasive as well as water and is a separate process. Keep it outside a water-only quotation.

Have the coating specialist record the existing substrate condition and the preparation requirement for the selected new coating. Identify the assessed members and faces, the evidence location and the specification revision. If new profile creation is required, exclude a water-only offer from this two-method comparison. If retained profile is accepted, both methods still have to satisfy the specified cleanliness and coating acceptance conditions.

Make that eligibility decision before tendering the full campaign. Otherwise, the buyer spends time negotiating a waterjet price that cannot meet the specified endpoint, or assumes a retained profile is adequate because the old paint has been removed. Surface appearance is not the purchase specification.

An overhead member has several faces, connections and obstructed areas. Map the priced surfaces to the structural grid, and identify the spaces below that require protection. That location record links the coating scope to containment, waste collection and personnel positions rather than treating the hall as one undifferentiated area.

## Purchase the building-protection package with the method

For dry preparation, require the contractor's complete containment, dust-control, spent-material collection and facility-release scope. The facility lead must identify equipment, stock and adjacent operations that influence the accepted work zone. Price the agreed closures and reinstatement with the preparation contract. A cleaning quotation that leaves the owner to arrange the protection is a partial offer.

For the water-only route, require the corresponding water-capture, waste handling, disposal and moisture-management scope. Tie it to the actual work area, adjacent equipment and building handback requirements. Do not delete those items merely because dry dust is avoided. The cost comparison concerns two different complete protection packages, not the substitution of water for an abrasive consumable.

Hempel also identifies flash rust and inspection as important wet-preparation considerations. The coating specialist and inspector must define the accepted surface condition and release points for this coating system. Keep the preparation-to-coating sequence and any required reassessment within the contractor's priced programme. Name the release owners and the records each must deliver before the next contractor accepts the surface.

## Use one endpoint for both quotations

| Fillable input and datum | Responsible owner | Purchase decision controlled |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Member/face IDs ___; structural-grid drawing revision ___ | Surveyor and coating contractor | Fixes the same complete surface scope |
| Existing condition/profile report ___; assessed locations ___ | Coating specialist | Admits or excludes the water-only proposal |
| New coating and preparation specification revision ___ | Coating designer | Sets the required substrate endpoint |
| Dry containment and protection plan ___; facility-release windows ___ | Blast contractor and facility lead | Prices the complete dry route and disruption |
| Water capture, disposal and moisture scope ___; receiving arrangements ___ | Preparation contractor and facility lead | Prices the complete wet route and handback |
| Inspection stages ___; accepted surface condition before coating ___ | Coating inspector | Allocates inspection attendance and release-window occupation |
| Personnel/tool inventory ___; hose and connected-equipment interfaces ___ | Contractor and equipment supplier | Screens the actual access task and environment |
| Accepted preparation boundary ___; facility handback criteria and price ___ | Buyer and facility lead | Compares both contracts through the same endpoint |

Separate technical eligibility from commercial ranking. First remove an offer that fails the specified profile or protection requirement. Then compare the qualifying totals: preparation, protection, waste, inspections, agreed access, reinstatement and disruption through the same released condition. Record exclusions beside the responsible party and its separate quotation. State whether coating application is included or separately contracted, and hold that boundary equal in both totals.

A schedule should identify the handovers between preparation, inspection and coating, including who acts if the surface no longer meets the accepted condition at the next stage. A faster cleaning operation saves money only when its complete quotation also covers the agreed inspection attendance and any defined return-work obligation.

## Two errors that change the contract award

**Mistake (hypothetical): purchasing water-only jetting where a new profile is required.** A plant's full-recoat specification requires profile creation on affected steel. The buyer selects a lower water-only price because the demonstration removes the visible coating. Correct the tender by applying the profile gate to the actual condition report. Within this comparison, select a qualifying dry abrasive package; any proposed change to the preparation specification belongs with the responsible coating specialist before award.

**Mistake (hypothetical): comparing a wet cleaning price with a complete dry package.** One bidder includes dry containment, collection, inspection attendance and handback. The other prices waterjet cleaning alone, with capture and waste disposal left to the facility. Complete the wet scope and its release responsibilities before comparing totals. Complete the missing capture, disposal and release prices before awarding the campaign.

## Define personnel access after the preparation package

The [ARC-F25 crawler ceiling platform](/products/arc-f25-crawler-ceiling-platform/) is a personnel-access candidate for suitable overhead workfaces. Select access only after the actual preparation environment, task loads and interfaces have been accepted for the offered equipment. A platform's appearance in a coating plan does not establish compatibility with abrasive or waterjet work.

List the people, tools, connected hoses and equipment interactions for each proposed personnel task. Keep specialist preparation plant and component handling in their own equipment scope. The equipment provider needs the complete configuration and environment to assess access; the coating contractor remains responsible for its specialist method and the inspector for the agreed surface releases.

## Eight RFQ attachments for a complete campaign

1. **Located condition record:** existing coating condition, member/face IDs and structural-grid references.
2. **Coating specification:** accepted full-recoat scope and required substrate preparation revision.
3. **Profile evidence:** assessed retained-profile locations and the specialist's method-eligibility decision.
4. **Dry-route package:** containment, collection, waste, shutdown and facility-release quotation.
5. **Water-only package:** capture, disposal, moisture control and flash-rust acceptance responsibilities.
6. **Inspection programme:** preparation and pre-coating releases, handover owners and priced return-work boundaries.
7. **Personnel-access dossier:** workface heights from finished floor, people/tools/interfaces, entry/route dimensions, floor/support records and equipment environmental acceptance.
8. **Commercial endpoint:** destination, accepted handback criteria, dates, exclusions and complete comparable quotations.

Among routes that meet the required surface condition and facility-release criteria, award the lowest complete priced cost through the same agreed endpoint.
