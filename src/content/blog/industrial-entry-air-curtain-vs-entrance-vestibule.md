**Choose an air curtain when an accepted project selection provides the required separation across the operating entrance and the agreed traffic flow benefits from a clear passage without an added enclosed lobby. Choose a physical vestibule when the brief requires an enclosed transition space or the proposed air curtain cannot meet the accepted entrance conditions.** First issue the opening survey, operating traffic and environmental brief to the entrance designer; then compare the complete installed alternatives, including header access and future cleaning.

An air curtain discharges a controlled air stream across an opening. A vestibule creates a physical intermediate enclosure. They spend money on different boundaries. A factory with frequent pedestrian movement, a loading opening and an entrance serving sensitive production should have separate briefs rather than one device copied across every doorway.

The wall-side photograph below illustrates an approach restricted by adjacent buildings and stored material. At an entrance, reserve a measured installation strip as well as a passage for deliveries. The header can be reachable while the machine's proposed supports conflict with traffic.

*Place the installation machine and the temporary pedestrian or delivery route on the same entrance plan.*

## An open passage still needs a defined separation result

Specify what the owner needs to control: conditioned-air exchange, occupant discomfort or another explicitly assessed environmental duty. Include operating hours, door-open patterns, prevailing exposure and the plant's pressure relationship. Have the responsible designer identify the accepted result and evidence for the proposed arrangement.

[Berner's selection guidance](https://berner.com/knowledge-hub/) identifies opening width and height, temperature difference, traffic and application as selection inputs. Its [specification information](https://berner.com/specifying/) also treats integration and applicable vestibule-exception conditions as project issues. Obtain the relevant destination decision from the appointed building designer. Obtain that project-specific acceptance before replacing a required lobby with an air-curtain proposal.

For the physical alternative, draw the enclosed passage with doors in their actual operating positions. The owner's traffic lead should test the nominated passage against people, carts and any permitted vehicle movement. Record usable space and interference with existing exits through the responsible project team. Equal clear opening width at one door does not establish an equivalent usable entrance.

## Price what each alternative leaves in the building

The air-curtain offer needs the complete selected assembly, its support, power and control interfaces, installation and acceptance. Identify whether the actual proposal mounts above the opening, within a recess or in another accepted arrangement. That changes where personnel need to stand and which existing finishes or services require work.

A vestibule offer includes the enclosure and its actual door assemblies, building connections, finishes and associated services. Separate new work from retained construction. A delivered glazing frame is not the completed entrance. Where the existing doorway remains part of the finished system, identify its retained condition and any separately specified work.

Complete this comparison against one entrance and operating brief. Use the actual submitted arrangements rather than a universal air-curtain-versus-lobby saving.

| Purchase input | Fillable project datum | Information owner | Decision controlled |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Operating entrance | Opening ID ___; clear width/height from finished threshold ___; surveyed head obstruction ___ | Surveyor | Defines the selected assembly and task positions |
| Required separation | Operating state ___; accepted outcome and evidence ___ | Owner and environmental designer | Admits qualifying alternatives |
| Traffic pattern | Users, loads, open cycles and peak periods ___ | Facility traffic lead | Tests open-passage value and lobby circulation |
| Air-curtain arrangement | Actual model, mounting detail and control scope ___ | Entrance-system provider | Fixes complete header and connection work |
| Vestibule arrangement | Clear passage, door sweeps and retained interfaces ___ | Building designer | Establishes usable footprint and construction scope |
| Workface access | Head/side/service faces ___; elevations from local floor ___ | Installer | Defines personnel positions and equipment occupation |
| Building interface | Support detail, power and affected finishes ___ | Appointed building and electrical trades | Prices enabling work beside the equipment |
| Accepted total | Installation, closure, checks and maintenance access ___ | Buyer | Compares complete operating entrances |

If the air-curtain proposal fails the accepted brief, exclude it before costing its smaller construction footprint. If a vestibule meets the brief but prevents the required traffic operation, revise that layout before treating it as the fallback. The useful outcome is a workable qualified choice.

## Plan the installation visit and the retained service visit

Ask the installer to mark fixing, connection, adjustment and acceptance positions. A short header task at one opening often supports a localized access service. A continuous campaign across several broad adjacent workfaces supports a large-deck comparison when the route and proposed configuration fit. Keep those actual quantities in the business case instead of buying a platform from total door height.

The lowered crawler deck pictured here remains a substantial machine inside the building. Include arrival, waiting and withdrawal in the temporary entrance arrangement. The building reviewer needs the offered equipment's reactions and supporting positions; the operator supplies floor records and restrictions.

![A red lowered deck and gray lifting structure stand above crawler tracks and extended supports in a workshop.](/images/equipment/buyer-20261102-workshop-low-deck.webp)
*Reserve a supporting and waiting area outside the traffic route for the actual offered machine.*

Obtain product-specific cleaning and service requirements from the selected entrance provider. Mark accessible panels and removal space on the completed drawing. Price those retained tasks with the facility's real occupation windows. A construction-stage platform route that disappears behind the new vestibule is not a future service route.

## Two hypothetical entrance orders to avoid

**A width-only air-curtain order:** An estimator selects a device spanning the opening but omits the entrance exposure, pressure and operating brief. The supplier's project response requires another arrangement, after supports and access have been ordered. Complete the entrance selection first and release its mounting drawing to the access provider.

**A cheap lobby without a usable passage:** Another buyer compares an enclosure price with an installed air-curtain package. The lobby quotation excludes door assemblies and does not show the loaded cart turning through it. Obtain a coordinated operating layout and complete construction price before awarding either alternative.
