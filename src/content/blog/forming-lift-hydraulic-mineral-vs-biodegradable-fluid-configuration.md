Choose the machine provider's accepted mineral-fluid configuration when it meets the project's environmental requirements and working duty. Choose a documented biodegradable-fluid configuration when the project requires that outcome and the provider accepts the complete machine, initial fill and service arrangements for the same duty. If neither proposed configuration meets those conditions, source another compliant equipment package. Send the environmental requirement and machine duty together before prices are requested, so the proposed fluid becomes part of the factory-accepted equipment order.

The decision belongs before ordering a new forming lift and before its first fill. The lift architecture, compatible former and site work are already selected. Buying a different drum after the machine arrives changes the problem into an existing-machine conversion, with different responsibilities and evidence.

<!-- audit-section: buyer-intent -->
## Buy the accepted configuration at the factory boundary

A forming lift raises a compatible roof-panel former. Give the lift and the former separate equipment identities in the order. Record each hydraulic circuit and the party accepting its configuration, especially when the equipment comes from different providers. The new-machine offer should name the exact fluid, supplied components, factory test condition, delivery fill and handover records.

For an assembly comprising a crawler lift and forming bed like the one below, attach a separate equipment-list entry for each. Connect each entry to its circuit boundary and accepted fluid specification in the supplied configuration schedule.

![A blue forming bed rests on a low red crawler assembly outside a factory building.](/images/equipment/buyer-20261031-crawler-line-low.webp)

*Name the lift and forming bed separately, then attach the accepted fluid configuration to each relevant circuit.*

The purchasing consequence is practical: an alternative-fluid requirement enters the machine order early enough for the provider to accept the components and delivery condition together. A fluid-only quotation leaves the buyer carrying the gap between a supplied liquid and a supported machine.

## What the primary technical guidance actually changes

[Bosch Rexroth's hydraulic-fluid guidance](https://www.boschrexroth.com/en/de/service-and-support/hydraulic-fluids/) treats fluid as a common element across hydraulic components and distinguishes mineral and environmentally acceptable fluid categories. This supports considering the whole circuit when selecting a new configuration.

A named component example shows why an exact identity matters. The [Rexroth A4VG Series 32 manual, pages 6–7](https://dc-mkt-prod.cloud.bosch.tech/br/media/campaigns/mobil/bombas_e_motores_nacionais/a4vg_serie_32.pdf), connects fluid selection to that pump's operating requirements and seal variant. Use this as an example of component-specific acceptance; the accepted component list for the offered forming lift must come from its own provider.

[Danfoss's July 2016 Hydraulic Fluids and Lubricants guide](https://assets.danfoss.com/documents/latest/54030/BC152886484524en-000902.pdf), particularly pages 4, 9 and 28–32, makes machine and fluid-maker requirements central to selection. It distinguishes biodegradable fluid families with different compatibility implications and discusses mineral-fluid testing residues. For a new-machine order, this makes factory testing and the specified delivery condition a responsibility to settle before handover. The exact fluid product and accepted machine configuration define that responsibility.

Translate that evidence into a delivery obligation: the provider identifies the accepted fluid and components, states the factory test and initial-fill arrangements, and supplies the relevant records with the machine. This is the buyer's procurement conclusion from the guides, rather than a universal fluid substitution rule.

## Complete the new-machine decision sheet

Enter actual document references and named owners. Use the same environmental criterion, machine duty and ownership period for both offers.

| Purchase input | Owner | Datum to enter | Decision controlled |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Site environmental requirement | Project environmental lead | Requirement text and revision: ___ | Reason an alternative fluid is required |
| Lift and former circuit boundaries | Equipment integrator | Equipment identities, duty and circuit schedule: ___ | Complete equipment being accepted |
| Exact fluid identity | Machine and fluid providers | Product designation and acceptance reference: ___ | Admissible factory configuration |
| Components and materials | Machine provider | Accepted component, seal and material schedule: ___ | Compatibility of the supplied circuit |
| Factory test and first fill | Machine provider | Test fluid, delivery fill and responsible party: ___ | Handover condition and associated cost |
| Supported working conditions | Owner and machine provider | Agreed duty and service document revisions: ___ | Usable equipment for this project |
| Replenishment and waste resources | Maintenance manager | Identified stock, service equipment and outlet: ___ | Ongoing ownership burden |
| Complete ownership comparison | Procurement lead | Fixed period and accepted complete totals: ___ | Choice between eligible configurations |

Keep the completed sheet with the equipment purchase schedule. An environmental adjective in the tender summary is too remote from the delivered machine to settle the initial-fill responsibility. The acceptance reference should point to a particular configuration and document revision.

## Price the service resources before accepting the alternative

Where the owner already holds suitable stock and service resources for the accepted mineral configuration, include those existing resources in the comparison. Budget the additional identified replenishment stock, service resources and responsibilities required by the alternative accepted configuration. Price both routes over the same ownership period, alongside the machine configuration and initial fill.

Ask the maintenance manager to trace a routine replenishment request from the machine identity to the correct stocked product. Then trace the used-fluid collection arrangement. Danfoss's guide also addresses disposal obligations for biodegradable fluids; environmental selection still requires an identified waste route under the applicable project arrangements.

![A red raised forming-lift structure carries a light-colored enclosure beside a building above green-covered ground.](/images/equipment/buyer-20261031-crawler-roof-side.webp)

*Keep the raised assembly's equipment identity connected to its accepted circuit and service records throughout the project sequence.*

For a mobile equipment owner, use the real sequence of intended projects. A configuration justified by one project's accepted requirement also needs a funded service model at the following locations. Conversely, a low-cost mineral proposal that fails the first project's explicit requirement cannot earn the planned work. Compare usable configurations across the actual ownership programme.

## Two purchasing mistakes to avoid

**Mistake — hypothetical:** A contractor specifies biodegradable fluid in the tender but accepts a machine quotation describing only the standard mineral-fluid delivery. The contractor then orders alternative fluid separately for arrival day. The correction is to place the accepted whole-machine configuration, factory test condition and initial fill in the equipment order before award, with the provider responsible for the delivered condition.

**Mistake — hypothetical:** A fleet owner funds the new alternative-fluid machine but allocates the same unlabelled replenishment containers and service stock used across its mineral-fluid fleet. The purchase budget has omitted an essential ownership resource. Correct it by funding identified stock, segregated service resources and a named maintenance responsibility with the configuration.

Award the new machine against one configuration schedule covering its components, specified delivery fill and funded service resources. Keep any later change to an existing machine under a separate accepted assessment and instruction set, with its own responsibility and price.

<!-- audit-section: project-checklist -->
## Attach eight items to the forming-lift RFQ

1. Selected crawler or vehicle-mounted scope, included assets and destination.
2. Actual former identity, mass, mounting interface and utilities schedule.
3. Work positions, receiving elevations, outlet datums and panel run-out drawing.
4. Ground or support information, access routes and setup constraints.
5. Transport route, packing boundary and intended project-location sequence.
6. Environmental criterion and required maker acceptance of the fluid configuration.
7. Required factory-test, initial-fill, handover and owner-service responsibility schedule.
8. Working duty, ownership period and complete accepted-package price format.
