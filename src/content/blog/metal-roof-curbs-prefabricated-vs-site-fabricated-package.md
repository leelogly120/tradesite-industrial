Buy prefabricated curb assemblies when opening dimensions and the accepted roof details are stable, and the finished units can reach their installation positions. Buy specialist site fabrication when as-built interfaces must be resolved at the workface and the appointed designer accepts that complete fabrication and inspection route. Release adjoining panels against the selected curb detail, not a generic opening note. For repeated curbs on released identical openings, start with the prefabricated package; reserve site fabrication for the actual late dimensions or interfaces that require it.

This choice begins after the opening layout is selected. It concerns how the designed three-dimensional interface is supplied and completed. It is broader than choosing a shop or field brake for ordinary trim: a curb can include framing, support interfaces, weathering, insulation and service connections with different owners.

## Identify what the curb actually does

Separate the opening's weathering duty from any equipment support. Give the roof and structural designers the plant or service requirements and obtain their accepted details. The supplier needs the actual roof product, slope, opening, surrounding panel arrangement and service interface.

[Metl-Span's CFR roof design guide](https://metlspan.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/11/CFRRoofArchitecturalDesignGuide.pdf) describes factory-built curb assemblies for its named system and identifies associated roof and interior closure details. This illustrates the complete product category. Its dimensions and support provisions are specific to that construction, not universal specifications for another roof.

## Prefabrication: commit dimensions before the unit travels

A prefabricated route needs a released drawing covering the opening, roof geometry and connections. Name the person accepting those dimensions and the date at which they become reliable. Where the surrounding structure is complete, obtain the relevant as-built survey before manufacturing the fixed assembly.

Price the supplied unit's actual boundary. List framing, insulation, liners, weathering pieces, seals or other components where specified, with exclusions assigned to named trades. Keep the equipment's own connection and support responsibilities visible. A factory-built item can still leave significant completion work on site.

Obtain packing, transport, unloading and movement-to-roof arrangements for the real unit. A three-dimensional assembly can have a different handling route from flat panels or blanks. Choose prefabrication when the final geometry is stable and that complete delivery and installation route works.

For repeated identical units, retain separate location marks even where the product drawing is common. Position-specific roof slope or service details can create different assemblies that look similar on a bill of quantities. Label the delivered pieces against the accepted opening schedule.

## Site fabrication: mobilise the complete specialist operation

Site fabrication is suitable where the actual interfaces become available late or where the accepted construction requires specialist completion at the building. Obtain an itemised proposal covering measurement, fabrication, materials, tools, workspace, access and inspection. Keep the designed weathering and structural details under their appointed owners.

This route needs reliable access when final measurements and fabrication occur. Coordinate roof protection, weather exposure, nearby work and material storage through the main contractor's construction plan. Price the actual work window, not a workshop labour rate with site establishment omitted.

Ask which elements can be prefabricated and which must be completed at the workface. A split route can serve a stable main assembly with a designed site-finished interface. Compare that actual arrangement when it is accepted, rather than forcing every component into one production location.

Do not use site fabrication as permission for the installer to invent the roof detail. Retain the roof designer's released assembly and the named inspection owner's acceptance method in the site-fabrication order. Obtain the completed records required by the roof-system and project providers before the opening is handed over.

| Curb-supply decision | Fillable field and datum | Responsible owner | Route consequence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Functional duty | Service opening and separate equipment-support references ___ | Services and structural designers | Defines what the curb supplies |
| Roof interface | Panel system, slope, layer section and detail revision ___ | Roof designer | Qualifies the complete assembly |

| Site operation | Measurement, fabrication, access and workspace scope ___ | Specialist contractor | Completes the site alternative |
| Panel relationship | Adjoining marks, lengths and drawing cutoff ___ | Roof detailer | Protects the sheet-production release |

| Three opening dimensions | Structural opening ___; service connection ___; outer curb envelope ___ with drawing datums ___ | Surveyor and curb detailer | Commits the correct finished geometry to each supplier |

Record opening dimensions at their stated reference surfaces. The structural opening, service connection and outer curb envelope can be different dimensions. Supply the drawing identifying all three instead of a single unlabelled width and length.

## Sequence the curb with the surrounding roof

Put structure, curb delivery or fabrication, panel laying, service connection and final weathering into one programme. Assign the trade responsible for protecting the interface between visits. The roof cannot be counted complete because the field sheets have arrived if the opening assembly still lacks its final work.

The wrapped former below illustrates a separate equipment shipment. A line and a curb are different procurement objects with different delivery milestones. Coordinate them through the roof-zone programme while keeping their technical acceptance records distinct.

![A blue and orange forming line stands on timber sheets with clear wrapping around its front tooling.](/images/equipment/buyer-20261102-wrapped-former.webp)
*Keep machinery delivery, curb delivery and final opening completion as separate programme milestones.*

**Mistake (hypothetical): a prefabricated curb follows an early opening.** A prefabricated curb for a roof duct is ordered from nominal steelwork dimensions; the completed opening and service connection later occupy different reference positions. Set a named survey release for the affected assembly, or buy an accepted site-finished interface before committing its fixed geometry.

**Mistake (hypothetical): site fabrication means material only.** A bid includes metal blanks but omits specialist labour, working space, access and inspection. Complete that operation's price and handover scope before comparing it with a delivered prefabricated assembly.

## Eight RFQ attachments

1. Opening functions and separate equipment-support requirements.
2. Accepted roof assembly and curb detail references.
3. Surveyed dimensions with identified reference surfaces.
4. Prefabricated and site-fabricated supply alternatives.
5. Weather-sheet profile, material and adjoining piece marks.
6. Finished unit handling route or site fabrication workspace.
7. Structural, roofing and service installation milestones.
8. Destination, utilities and inspection/completion owners.
