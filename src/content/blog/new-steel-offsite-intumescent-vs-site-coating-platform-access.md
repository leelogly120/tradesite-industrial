Choose offsite application when the accepted coating system, fabrication release and protected delivery route make coated new steel ready for erection; buy personnel access for its remaining connections, repairs and inspections. Choose full site application when final site work or the accepted preservation route prevents that arrangement. Shortlist ARC-F25 for repeated shared crew workfaces only when its proposed configuration, floor route and actual coating environment are accepted. Obtain both prices through erected, coated and inspected steel. When both meet the required date, choose the lower complete cost and buy its retained personnel access.

## The purchase is an erected member in an accepted condition

Keep the selected fire-protection design and member schedule fixed. The competing purchases are a completed applied condition delivered through fabrication and erection, and an application campaign performed after erection. Buying coated steel changes who controls that condition before the site receives it. It also changes the quantity and distribution of work left for an elevated crew.

Give each member its fabrication revision, coating release and agreed delivery condition. Include connection zones whose work remains at site and the inspection records accompanying the member. An offsite offer that ends at application differs from one funding protected storage, transport, erection interfaces and the specified correction work.

The project's coating and fire specialists must accept the actual product, substrate, system and completed member coverage before procurement compares those routes.

## Offsite completion moves work into the delivery contract

Start with the fabricator's release date. A member still receiving changes has a different coating commitment from one released for dispatch. Identify the actual final fabrication and connection scope before buying a completed offsite condition. Put later site changes and their coating consequences into the responsible contractor's order.

Then follow the coated member through its proposed handling and storage. Obtain the supplier's preservation requirements and the corresponding priced delivery arrangement. Allocate condition checks at transfer, storage and erection, with an identified party for correcting damage before final acceptance. This purchases custody of a defined product condition rather than relying on an attractive dispatch photograph.

Product requirements differ. For example, [Jotun's SteelMaster 900WF application guide](https://www.jotun.com/api-b2b/v1/datasheets/download/merged?selectedFiles=55677) gives specific offsite transport, storage and weather-protection requirements. Those waterborne-product conditions belong to that product. Use the selected system's documents to price preservation; avoid carrying another coating's handling or exposure conditions into its order.

Offsite application is attractive when released fabrication, accepted preservation and an agreed erection interface remove a substantial repeat high-level campaign. Retain the specified site work. A small total repair area spread across many disconnected positions can still require significant access mobilisation. Where only some member groups qualify for offsite completion, price those groups separately from members requiring final site fabrication, with their actual site visits and coating releases.

## Full site work buys a campaign around the building programme

Choose the complete site route when the accepted coating sequence follows final erection or when the proposed offsite delivery condition cannot be maintained. Budget the preparation, application, specialist protection, inspections and corrections required by that route. The coating contractor defines the work method; the access buyer purchases its actual personnel workspace and site occupation.

Map member faces, connections and obstructions by building grid. Record task elevations from the floor supporting the platform, not just a roof-height label. Add the crew, tools and objects present together, including external hoses or cables that interact with the equipment. Keep application machinery and material handling in separately identified scopes.

A broad deck is a useful candidate where repeated adjacent faces benefit from a shared workspace and the complete setup fits. Use another accepted access arrangement for isolated or obstructed faces outside that proposal. The platform supplier's environmental acceptance must cover the actual specialist task; ordinary personnel equipment is not automatically suitable for a spray or classified atmosphere.

## Two hypothetical coating-purchase mistakes

**Mistake (hypothetical): coated delivery becomes zero site access.** The fabricator supplies coated members, but the erection contract leaves connection completion and damage correction to a site specialist. Procurement cancels the deck before those faces are inspected. Put the residual work and acceptance visits into the original access award, with appropriate point access for separated positions.

**Mistake (hypothetical): offsite application is priced as delivered completion.** An estimator selects a cheaper offsite application quote while transport protection and condition correction sit outside every contract. Add their actual providers, accepted handovers and costs before comparing the completed erected-steel alternatives.
