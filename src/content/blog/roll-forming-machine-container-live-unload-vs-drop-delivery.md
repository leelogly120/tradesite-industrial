**Book live unloading when the receiver has an accepted machine-unloading plan, the required people and handling equipment ready for the delivery appointment, and a realistic operation within the haulier's agreed waiting arrangement. Book a drop-and-collect service when the haulier accepts it, the facility can safely retain the container/chassis arrangement, and the complete cost of the extra visit and equipment use is justified.** If neither route has an accepted handling and empty-return plan, book an equipped receiving facility before dispatch.

This is a last-mile service choice for an actual containerised equipment order. It follows container fit, packing and carriage acceptance. It does not authorise using a forklift, crane or improvised ramp for a particular machine. The equipment supplier, packer and competent receiving/handling providers establish the actual unloading method and required resources.

## Waiting for a machine unload is a purchased service

[Flexport's FCL delivery guidance](https://support.portal.flexport.com/hc/en-us/articles/16598091081111-Trucking-fees-for-FCL-deliveries) distinguishes a driver waiting during live unloading from a container dropped for later collection. It identifies waiting, drop and chassis-use costs. Obtain the actual haulier's offer for this location and shipment instead of copying another service's allowance or tariff.

The useful live-unload duration includes access, positioning, authorised opening, handling and release of the empty under the actual agreed arrangement. A machine package requiring specialist work is not equivalent to ordinary pallet removal. Have the receiving contractor estimate and quote its complete operation from the issued packing and handling data.

The machine's length, base and wrapped head show why the receiver needs more than a container number. Obtain the actual package dimensions, masses and opening/handling requirements. The competent handling provider establishes the method and resources; the haulier quotes the delivery service that can accommodate that operation.

## A drop moves the constraint into your yard

A dropped container creates a retained equipment and space obligation. Specify whether the accepted arrangement leaves it on a chassis or uses another separately engineered placement service. Confirm access, supporting area and the handling provider's accepted working arrangement through the responsible site and transport professionals. The word drop does not describe ground placement or authorise it.

Obtain the pickup process and empty-return deadline from the actual providers. Name the person who reports the container empty and the party booking its collection. Include chassis use, container detention where applicable, extra visits and any facility charges in the comparison. A driver leaving the site does not stop every equipment charge.

## Keep unloading separate from assembly and commissioning

The receiving contractor needs to know the condition in which each assembly will stand after unloading. Identify its storage and support arrangements and who accepts custody. A former on its shipping base still needs the equipment-specific installation and acceptance process. Do not include that later work in a driver's waiting appointment unless the actual providers have explicitly accepted the scope.

For a split-supply order, book receiving around the packages that actually arrive. A lifting assembly, former and local chassis can have different delivery and integration events. Use a package-to-assembly map so a container is not retained simply because the later installation team cannot find the item's contents or destination.

## Two hypothetical last-mile failures

**Live unloading is booked before the specialist handling crew.** The truck reaches the gate, but the facility has only ordinary pallet-handling resources while the machine requires a different accepted method. The delivery cannot proceed as planned. Obtain the actual handling proposal and confirmed resource booking before selecting the transport appointment.

**A drop is treated as unlimited free storage.** The receiver has yard space but no named empty-notification or pickup owner. Retained equipment charges continue after unloading. Compare the complete quoted use period and assign notification, collection and return responsibilities before booking a drop.
