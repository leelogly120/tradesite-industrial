Add a defined soffit production cell when repeated accepted orders justify its profile, ventilation features, mating edges and installation-component basket. Buy finished soffit for occasional work and retain the weather-sheet line for roof orders. Require the offered configuration to cover each finished soffit variant and its additional manufacturing operations. Price one complete solid-and-vented soffit order before selecting shared or dedicated machinery.

The distinction starts at the building. A weather sheet forms part of the external roof covering. A soffit product encloses a specified underside or eaves area and follows its own support, finish, junction and ventilation requirements. Record the actual end use before allocating orders to a new line.

## The soffit order contains more than panel area

Obtain the accepted layout and component schedule from the product owner. Identify panel widths, lengths, visible finish, mating edges, support spacing, trims and closures. Distinguish solid panels from panels with specified ventilation features. The designer defines the required air path and product performance; the factory manufactures the released parts.

[Bradbury's soffit roll-former page](https://www.bradburygroup.com/en-us/products/soffit-rollforming-lines) describes equipment for steel or aluminium soffit panels, including smooth or vented product and lancing options. Use this specialised category to specify smooth and vented outputs separately, then require the actual equipment offer to identify which finished parts and operations it includes.

A vented product requires an accepted finished opening pattern and process, not a generic perforation allowance. Specify the finished geometry, location datums, edge condition and inspection requirement. Assign scrap collection and any subsequent cleaning or handling to the process proposal. Keep actual ventilation calculations and building design with the responsible designer.

## Use the busiest real order as the investment case

Choose a committed order containing the mix that matters commercially: solid and vented pieces, short lengths, corners, trims and finish changes. Map each item from incoming material to packed delivery. Identify which stages a proposed cell performs and which remain separate fabrication or purchased supply.

Compare that complete route with a finished-soffit supplier's quotation. Include stock, tooling, trial material, secondary work, inspection, packing and installation accessories. Where the project specifies a proprietary product, retain its accepted production and component route. A dimensionally similar panel does not automatically carry another product's evidence.

## Keep two production calendars visible

If the existing weather-sheet line is proposed for conversion, obtain an explicit configuration and changeover offer. Price tooling, labour, handling, acceptance of both products and the transition back to roofing. Schedule those periods against committed roof deliveries. Available floor space does not create extra line hours.

A separate cell earns its place when simultaneous orders and complete costs justify it. A shared cell earns its place when the provider supports both controlled products and their transitions fit the real calendar. Bought soffit remains a useful route for a small or irregular workload because it keeps the new product commitment separate from established roofing output.

This differs from [perforated acoustic roof-liner production](/blog/perforated-acoustic-roof-liner-vs-weather-sheet-production/). An acoustic liner is part of an accepted sound-control assembly; an eaves soffit has a different application, support and ventilation brief. Do not combine their order forecasts simply because both include openings in metal.

## Buy the accessory supply before advertising a range

Assign the trims, edge pieces, support components and product documentation promised in the sale. Use existing accepted suppliers where their products fit the selected system. Commission fabrication only for controlled drawings and an accepted process. Put material and finish matching requirements into those orders.

Set a delivery endpoint the customer can recognise: identified panels and listed accessories, packed by area or installation sequence. Compare owned manufacture and bought supply at that same endpoint. If the line quotation stops at ordinary unvented pieces, add the missing work before calling it a cheaper soffit solution.

## Two hypothetical range-expansion errors

**Mistake: Ventilation is omitted from the machine price.** A producer forecasts all soffit area as ordinary formed output, then discovers repeated vented orders need another process. Define the finished parts and require a priced process for every product family before buying the cell.

**Mistake: Accessories arrive in a different finish.** Panels meet the agreed appearance, but the order has no coordinated trim source. The delivery cannot complete the visible soffit field. Price and release the full component basket with material and appearance identities retained.
