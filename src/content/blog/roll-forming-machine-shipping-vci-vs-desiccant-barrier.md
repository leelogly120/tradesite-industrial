**Choose a supplier-approved VCI package when corrosion inhibition around the actual metal assemblies fits their material compatibility and enclosure requirements. Choose a designed desiccant-and-barrier package when controlled humidity is the specified protection approach and the route can maintain its sealed enclosure, monitoring and resealing requirements.** Use an approved combined system when the actual preservation design calls for both. Obtain the machine and preservation suppliers' acceptance of the complete system; never size a chemical or desiccant dose from a photograph.

This is an equipment-preservation purchase, separate from the strength of its crate or base. The pack needs to protect the former's exposed tooling, electrical assemblies and other identified surfaces from the conditions actually expected before installation. Start with the equipment supplier's materials and preservation constraints, then request a documented proposal from a qualified preservation provider.

## Describe the interval from packing to recommissioning

The exposure period ends when the receiving team completes the agreed opening and recommissioning work, rather than when the vessel reaches port. List warehouse stays, customs access, inland transport and any delay before installation. Give the preservation provider the actual planned environments and credible storage contingency from the project programme.

The German insurance industry's [Transport Information Service](https://www.tis-gdv.de/tis_e/verpack/verpackungshandbuch/11verpackungshandbuch_044.htm/) distinguishes coating, VCI and desiccant approaches and stresses the item's characteristics and protective-product requirements. It also identifies proper reclosure after inspection as important for desiccant packaging. This supports a purchasing question: who restores protection after a package is opened along the route?

![A long blue line rests on timber sheets with protective film around its striped near head.](/images/equipment/buyer-20261102-wrapped-former.webp)
*Specify which parts of the machine are enclosed and who restores that enclosure after inspection.*

The film visible around the machine head helps locate the protection boundary. A partial wrap and a fully designed preservation enclosure are different scopes. Obtain the actual package drawing and preparation record rather than assuming visible plastic provides a complete shipping or storage system.

## Compare complete protection proposals

[ZERUST's desiccant and VCI product information](https://www.zerust.com/products/vci-emitters/) includes products combining moisture control and corrosion inhibition. [Cortec's VCI bag information](https://www.cortecvci.com/products/vpci-packaging-products/vpci-126-vacuum-bags/) describes VCI packaging with additional barrier properties. Those examples demonstrate why the correct comparison is between defined systems, rather than a universal claim that one ingredient replaces every other one.

Have the equipment supplier identify material compatibility and any restrictions around electronics, coatings, lubricants, seals and precision surfaces. The preservation provider then states its actual product system, preparation, enclosure, maintenance and removal requirements. Record who approves the proposal for the supplied machine and who executes it.

## Customs access is a real preservation event

Inform the broker and logistics provider which packages contain preservation enclosures and what opening instructions accompany them. Assign a competent party with the approved materials and authority to restore the package after an authorised inspection. Preserve a record of the opening and restoration with its package identity.

If the actual route cannot maintain the accepted enclosure, return that fact to the preservation designer. Buy a revised system or change the handling/storage route. Do not continue using a protection-duration assumption after the conditions supporting it have changed.

At destination, distinguish receipt inspection from opening for installation. If the equipment will remain stored, maintain the supplier-approved protection and monitoring. If installation starts, book the required preparation and equipment checks. Unpacking a former early for convenience changes the exposure basis and the receiving team's obligations.

![Silver rolls, shafts and black adjustment blocks run along a blue line beside wooden cases.](/images/equipment/buyer-20261101-roller-line-detail.webp)
*Tooling surfaces and nearby components need a released opening and return-to-service plan.*

The visible roller train makes the production consequence clear. Corrosion control should preserve the condition needed for accepted output, and its removal must suit the actual surfaces. The buyer should obtain the equipment supplier's preparation instructions and include the work in the installation quotation.

## Two hypothetical preservation failures

**A sealed pack is opened and casually taped shut.** The initial design assumed a maintained barrier, but an intermediate inspection opens it and no approved restoration follows. Assign the restoration owner, required materials and record before dispatch. Have the preservation provider assess any actual breach against its design.

**A shipping package is used as indefinite outdoor storage.** The machine reaches the buyer before its workshop is ready. The original exposure and storage conditions no longer match the plan. Obtain an accepted revised storage arrangement and fund its monitoring rather than extending the original protection claim informally.
