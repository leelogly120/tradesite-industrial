**Choose early construction of the permanent service road when its design is released, it serves a genuine long-term need, and the owner accepts construction traffic and the conditions of early handover. Hire an engineered temporary trackway when the façade work has a finite duration or the final road cannot be released in time.** For either choice obtain separate acceptance of the operating positions for the offered machine. A road that carries a travelling platform is only one part of the access purchase.

A contractor prices panels for a temporary road. The developer points to the finished service road shown on the planning drawings. Both proposals appear to reach the same boundary; their costs and obligations can be very different. Compare the cost of advancing the permanent asset with the complete temporary scheme. Charging the entire permanent road to one façade package gives a distorted result if the owner already intends to build it later.

On an external elevation that survey needs the current surface, soil information, buried infrastructure, entrances, required turns and change in levels. A generic area of ground on a site layout is insufficient to commission a road or a working pad.

## Ask who takes responsibility before final completion

For a permanent road, obtain the approved pavement design, drainage, formation release and construction programme from the civil contractor. Ask the owner which layers or finished surfaces can be trafficked, by what plant and under what conditions. Define maintenance during early use, inspection afterwards and responsibility for any repair before final acceptance. An early sub-base is a distinct temporary operating condition even if it lies under the eventual road.

For a hired trackway, ask a specialist to design and quote for the actual ground and crossing duty. [Davis Track Hire describes temporary construction-site access](https://davistrackhire.com/sectors/construction/) based on the terrain and the loads to pass along the route. Obtain its proposed layout, installation and retrieval arrangements, inspection responsibilities and reinstatement exclusions. Hire terms for panels do not replace engineering acceptance of the complete ground system.

Request separate machine data for travel and work. The full working arrangement can put a concentrated reaction outside the traffic lane or onto an adjoining area with different ground conditions. The platform and access-route providers need compatible plans. Where supports extend past the road edge, the responsible engineer should define suitable working areas or revise the position. A wider line on a site drawing is not an engineering solution.

## Price the difference between now and later

First build a baseline with the permanent road constructed at its already-funded date. Then compare the incremental cost of building and opening it early against trackway hire, engineering, installation, maintenance through the works, removal and reinstatement. Include acceleration, interrupted utility work, temporary protection and early road damage only where the relevant bid or programme identifies them. Count any temporary access used by other trades once and allocate it openly.

If the façade contractor's dates are uncertain, compare a short and an extended hire period. A temporary scheme can win for a short campaign yet accumulate substantial cost when another package holds the elevation. The permanent road can appear attractive until the utility contractor needs to reopen it. Put dates, owners and change consequences on the same comparison. Neither option should receive a benefit for construction time the party responsible cannot release.

## Two mistakes at handover

**Mistake one — accepting an early road that belongs to nobody.** A hypothetical developer allows façade equipment onto an unfinished surface, while the civil contractor's warranty and damage allowance start after final handover. Agree the permitted early-use condition, inspection and repair responsibilities before opening the route.

**Mistake two — removing the trackway before the last elevation visit.** A buyer books panels only for installation. Rectification and final inspection still require personnel access after the ground is landscaped. Include those dates, retain an accepted alternative or make the reinstatement programme follow the last visit.
