# Roll Forming vs Pre-formed Panels: Which Saves Money?

For years, we used pre-formed panels delivered from the factory. Then we tried on-site roll forming. The difference was dramatic. Here's what we learned from 8 comparative projects.

## The Traditional Approach: Pre-formed Panels

### How It Works

1. Panels manufactured at factory
2. Cut to standard lengths (6m, 9m, 12m)
3. Packed and loaded on trucks
4. Delivered to site
5. Installed by workers

### The Hidden Costs

| Cost Item | Amount per 1,000 sqm |
|-----------|---------------------|
| Panel manufacturing | $3,000 |
| Transportation | $800 |
| Damage in transit (8%) | $240 |
| Waste from cutting | $300 |
| Storage on site | $200 |
| **Total** | **$4,540** |

### The Quality Problem

- 8-12% damage rate in transit
- Standard lengths only (waste at edges)
- Visible seams from transportation damage
- Inconsistent quality between batches

## The Modern Approach: On-site Roll Forming

### How It Works

1. Steel coils delivered to site
2. Roll forming machine produces panels
3. Panels cut to exact length
4. Immediate installation
5. Zero transportation damage

### The Cost Advantage

| Cost Item | Amount per 1,000 sqm |
|-----------|---------------------|
| Steel coil | $2,800 |
| Roll forming operation | $400 |
| Waste (2%) | $56 |
| **Total** | **$3,256** |

**Savings: $1,284 per 1,000 sqm (28%)**

### The Quality Advantage

- Zero transportation damage
- Exact lengths (no edge waste)
- Consistent quality throughout
- Custom profiles possible

## When Roll Forming Wins

### Best Applications

- **Large projects** (>5,000 sqm)
- **Custom lengths needed**
- **Remote locations** (transportation expensive)
- **Quality critical** applications
- **Tight timelines**

## When Pre-formed Panels Win

### Best Applications

- **Small projects** (<2,000 sqm)
- **Standard profiles** (no customization needed)
- **Immediate availability** (stock items)
- **Simple logistics**

## The Decision Framework

| Factor | Roll Forming | Pre-formed |
|--------|-------------|------------|

## Conclusion

For most ceiling work projects, on-site roll forming delivers:
