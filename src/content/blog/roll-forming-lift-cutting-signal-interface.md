Treat a cutting signal as former-only until the offer names an interface on the lift. Write that choice on the line specification, not as a guessed wiring note. The place for the combined spec is the [roll-forming line specification](/blog/roll-forming-line-specification-long-span-roof-panels/). Broader electrical boundaries stay in the [electrical interface guide](/blog/roll-forming-line-electrical-control-interfaces/).

| Phrase in the offer | What it usually means | What to ask |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cutter included with the former | A function inside that machine | Confirm on RFQ that the lift is not in the circuit |
| Signal to the lift | A named interface | Who supplies it, and the drawing number |
| No cutting words at all | No interface in this purchase | Leave it out; do not invent one |
| Same word on two suppliers' sheets | Two different scopes until compared | Ask each issuer what their sentence covers |

Two ways this goes wrong. The word signal is read as a plug between the lift and the cutter, and the site waits for a cable nobody sold. Or two offers both say cutting included, one means a shear inside the former, and the other means a future integration that is not in the price.

Add one line to the specification attachment:

- Cutting scope: former-only, named interface, or not in this purchase
- Former name and who answers for the cutter
- Catalog lift screen: 18.5m on ARC-T18, or 25m on ARC-T25 or ARC-C25
- ARC-RF8 only if the line is in the same buy; 0.3–1.0mm is the catalog sheet class
- Profile drawing revision
- Destination country
