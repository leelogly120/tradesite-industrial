Choose permanent roof safety mesh when the project’s competent team specifies it to prevent internal falls through the roof field and the owner will retain it for future work. Buy a temporary safety-net package when the assessment justifies fall arrest, higher-order controls have been addressed, and a specialist can cover every required bay through installation, rescue provision and removal. A lower hire price cannot turn a fall-arrest measure into a fall-prevention measure.

For a contractor planning roof-level panel production, the practical question is which bays can receive sheets on which dates. A machine outside the steel frame can deliver a sheet to the correct elevation while the receiving crew still lacks an authorised place to work. Put the field protection, roof-edge arrangements and panel handover on the same programme before booking the forming equipment.

## Which part of the roof does the proposal cover?

Give the competent designer and installation team a plan showing the fields between structural supports, penetrations, rooflights, perimeters and adjoining construction stages. Mark the working face as it moves. Ask them to identify areas where exposure can be eliminated, then the prevention measures for remaining work and any residual exposure requiring fall arrest. Price the combinations they accept for the actual site and jurisdiction.

Safe Work Australia's model code describes securely fixed roof safety mesh as protection against internal falls during roofing and later maintenance. It identifies separate exposure at edges and openings, and the need to protect the people installing mesh. Britain's HSE describes safety nets as specialist fall-arrest systems requiring competent installation, inspection and provision for rescue. Those are different duties. The project team should show the duty required in each area. [Safe Work Australia model code, sections 5.3 and 7.2](https://www.safeworkaustralia.gov.au/sites/default/files/2022-10/Model%20Code%20of%20Practice%20-%20Managing%20the%20Risk%20of%20Falls%20at%20Workplaces%2021102022_0.pdf) and [HSE guidance on safety nets](https://www.hse.gov.uk/construction/safetytopics/safety-nets.htm)

## What permanent mesh buys the owner

Where the chosen roof system admits permanent mesh, ask for the finished coverage drawing, specified product, structural interfaces and installation scope. Include the access and controls for the crew fitting it before an area becomes protected. The bid should state who inspects the installation and records acceptance before the cladding conceals it. Price this by released roof zone so the roofing scheduler knows which areas are ready.

Then ask the owner about later alterations. Who retains the drawings? How will a future service penetration be identified and the affected protection reassessed? If the maintenance plan assumes a retained control beneath a roof field, include the inspection responsibilities, limitations and intervention records in the handover. Estimate its value only for the areas and activities for which the long-term arrangement has been accepted.

A roof drawing with a mesh note across an entire plan can hide an interruption at a rooflight or equipment opening. Give those features their own contract lines. The responsible designer identifies the separate controls. The quantity surveyor can then distinguish a genuinely complete offer from one that simply multiplies the roof area by a mesh price.

## Purchase the full temporary-net service

For a temporary package, request the specialist's marked coverage and dates, support assessment, clearance requirements, access to install and remove it, inspections and rescue arrangements. Underlying construction matters. Stacked materials, a new services run or an operating production line can occupy space that the proposed net or rescue plan requires. Record when control of that space changes hands.

Schedule installation, handover, alterations between bays and final removal. Specify the consequence of a delay to the cladding crew. Does the contract include continued hire and inspections? Can the specialist return to remove the nets before a ceiling trade closes its access? Require the price for the actual sequence, including any separated visits. Assign the subsequent maintenance package its own arrangements once temporary protection leaves.

## Two mistakes that leave an unpriced work phase

**Mistake one.** Suppose a roof quotation includes installed mesh but omits the work required to protect the installers before the first field is handed over. The first coil arrives against a date that nobody can release. Put the specialist's access, installer protection, inspection and named handover ahead of the former booking.

**Mistake two.** A net hire ends when the last field sheet is fixed. A later rooflight installation still opens a work area, and equipment below blocks the original access route. Have the project team define the later work and its accepted controls before closing the temporary package. Price any extension, specialist return or separate arrangement against those dates.

## RFQ attachments for an installable roof programme

- Issue the roof plan with bay identifiers and phased handover dates.
- Mark fields, edges, rooflights and temporary openings individually.
- Attach the responsible team's accepted prevention and residual-arrest requirements.
- Identify the mesh installer, inspection witness and handover recipient.
- Give the net specialist the below-roof layout and competing work dates.
- Include required rescue provision, inspections, moves and removal access.
- Provide the panel profile, receiving elevation and site support information.
- Show later roof work, maintenance ownership and care of retained protection.
