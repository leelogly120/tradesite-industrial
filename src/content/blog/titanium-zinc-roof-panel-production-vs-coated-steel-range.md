Retain coated steel as the base range and purchase specialist zinc panels when demand is occasional or fabrication resources remain unfunded. Budget the zinc fabrication team, working environment and complete component basket alongside the available line hours. ## Build the order basket around the zinc system

[RHEINZINK describes titanium zinc](https://www.rheinzink.com/products/materials-surfaces/titanium-zinc/) as a zinc alloy with controlled additions including titanium and copper, supplied for roofing, facade and drainage work. It is a distinct material from metallic-coated steel. Give the tooling provider the selected zinc supplier's actual material specification and processing requirements for the product-specific qualification.

List accepted roof sections, surface variants, panel lengths, seam-completion method and project details in the committed order book. Separate repeated straight panels from tapered pieces, curved work, valleys, dormers and other exceptions. Decide which pieces your shop fabricates, which arrive from a specialist and which remain the installer's work.

Budget detailing capability alongside the main former. A business selling a complete zinc roof-material package needs a route for specified flashings, connections and drainage components. A repeated straight panel is one part of that basket. Obtain specialist prices for low-volume pieces rather than assuming the main line manufactures them.

## Temperature and site conditions change production resources

The material producer's [processing information](https://www.rheinzink.us/fileadmin/redaktion/RHEINZINK_US/Downloads/Technical-Documents/rheinzink-information-material-and-processing-107760-v000-en-us.pdf) addresses forming, jointing, transport and storage. Carry applicable requirements into the process proposal. For colder conditions, require the appointed specialist to define the accepted processing arrangement and its utilities, enclosure and programme cost. Procurement needs that priced scope before promising winter-site production.

Use the selected zinc provider's applicable project documents for the final process. Leave heating methods, machine adjustments and joining procedures to the responsible technical parties. The buyer's job is to fund and schedule the specified resources and assign each interface to a provider.

For roof-level production, coordinate the complete former and material route with the receiving roof. The elevated equipment beside ground-level coils below shows distinct locations. Decide how the qualified process, storage and output reception remain connected at the proposed site.

## Three routes to the first zinc order

**Purchase specialist panels and details** for an isolated project. Compare the delivered material basket with the internal resources needed to make it. This buys product for the roof while preserving established steel workload.

**Own repeat forming and buy complex details** when regular zinc panels justify qualified tooling but exceptional fabrication remains a smaller specialist workload. Write drawing-release and delivery boundaries between both suppliers. Assign mating interfaces and the complete component schedule.

**Build a wider zinc fabrication range** when contracted demand funds the process, detail shop and support. Demonstrate a representative order with its selected finish, seam interfaces and packed output before committing that range to sales. Include a realistic exception piece so the trial covers the work hidden outside the repetitive panel area.

The [ventilated versus unventilated zinc-roof guide](/blog/ventilated-vs-unventilated-standing-seam-roof-package/) addresses an already selected roof build-up. This producer decision selects fabrication and supply capabilities to own. Keep the designer's accepted assembly fixed while evaluating production routes. Within a chosen zinc range, the [natural versus preweathered surface decision](/blog/zinc-roof-panels-natural-vs-preweathered-surface/) separately controls supplied appearance.

## Two hypothetical zinc-expansion mistakes

**The machine is funded but details are missing.** A steel producer buys zinc tooling for a roof containing many special junctions. Nobody priced the accepted flashings and exception pieces. Build the material basket first and place every nonstandard piece with a named fabricator.

**A shop trial becomes an unpriced winter-site promise.** The quotation assumes identical resources at an exposed project without its seasonal working environment. Require a site-specific process and resource proposal before offering that production location.
