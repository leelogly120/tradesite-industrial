**Choose an accepted flexible-wrap assembly when the available service space favors its documented compact enclosure and installers can reach every required wrap, joint and fixing face. Choose an accepted board enclosure when the released retained-metal-duct detail calls for formed board cladding and the coordinated space allows its panels, joints and inspection work.** Have the fire/system designer release the exact assembly and penetration details first. Then compare complete installed protection and the access needed to inspect it.

The decision is between actual systems eligible for the same duct function and destination. It is not a choice between two generic materials carrying a claimed number of fire-resistance hours. The nominated assembly, its supporting details and the required fire exposure govern eligibility. Where only one complete system is accepted, price that system instead of inventing a competing alternative.

The broad deck below sits among roof members. A duct-protection task needs its own section showing all required faces, including the upper side and nearby penetrations. A clear underside alone does not establish installation coverage around the enclosure.

## Product thickness is not the complete space decision

3M describes [Duct Wrap 615+](https://multimedia.3m.com/mws/media/751620O/3m-fire-barrier-duct-wrap-615-technical-data-sheet.pdf?fn=3M-Fire-Barrier-Duct-Wrap-615-plus-Technical-Data-Sheet.pdf) as a flexible, space-saving alternative to traditional enclosure methods. Its [technical data](https://multimedia.3m.com/mws/media/751620O/3m-fire-barrier-duct-wrap-615-technical-data-sheet.pdf?fn=3M-Fire-Barrier-Duct-Wrap-615-plus-Technical-Data-Sheet.pdf) requires the applicable tested and listed system details for the actual application. Use that specific completed assembly in the coordinated section, including its required joints, access and installation clearances.

[Promat's duct-system information](https://www.promat.com/en-us/construction/products-applications/systems/ventilation--smoke-extraction2/) distinguishes cladding an existing sheet-metal duct from constructing a duct from boards. Those are different purchase boundaries. If the comparison is protection around a retained metal duct, keep that same retained duct in both offers. A self-contained board duct is a different mechanical alternative requiring the designer's explicit release.

Do not choose wrap just because the void looks congested. Obtain the installer and system provider's response to whether every required detail can be completed there. If the accepted assembly cannot be installed in the available space, change the coordinated route or assembly before ordering the access package.

## Fix the fire-protection boundary at every interface

The responsible fire and mechanical designers identify the duct function, accepted system and relevant exposure requirements. The specialist contractor names the exact products and details in its proposal. Keep ventilation and smoke-extraction duties distinct where their accepted systems differ.

Locate supports, penetrations, access panels, changes in section and interfaces with other construction. Assign the protection and reinstatement responsibility at each. A main-run material rate should not silently include incompatible penetration details or a generic access hatch.

[Promat's service-enclosure guidance](https://promat.helpjuice.com/ductwork-service-enclosures) explains that a board's reaction-to-fire classification is different from the fire resistance of a tested system. Its access-panel and damper entries also reinforce the need for application-specific details. Procure the accepted complete construction rather than combining attractive parts from different arrangements.

## Wrap and board create different field objects

For wrap, obtain the contractor's actual material, fixing and working sequence for the accepted system. Identify the faces it must reach and any external process or equipment interfaces affecting the platform. Avoid substituting a general roll-handling assumption for that task schedule.

For board, obtain panel dimensions, masses, field preparation and assembly details. Name the provider for any separate material presentation. The personnel deck needs its actual permitted item and load plan, independently of the equipment handling boards or supporting ductwork.

A broad deck is useful where repeated adjacent tasks fit its offered geometry. Obstructed upper faces, wall-side interfaces or isolated penetrations need separately identified coverage. Use the measured workface sections to assign equipment to every position.

The hall-route photograph shows unfinished ground and other machines. Use current floor or ground information for the proposed access and application resources. Inspection visits can occur after further building work; record that later route and supporting condition as well.

## Inspection is part of the installation purchase

Have the quality lead identify actual hold points and the evidence each requires. Connect them to location IDs and access dates. Do not remove the platform simply because the main material area has been installed while required high-level checks remain.

Compare complete wrap and board bids through the same accepted boundary. Include supports and penetrations assigned to the protection contractor, material handling, access, inspection and any specified finish. Where another contractor owns an interface, name that included parallel cost. A low straight-run rate is not the full campaign.

## Two hypothetical protection orders with hidden gaps

**Mistake (hypothetical): Compact material becomes a complete-fit claim.** A buyer chooses wrap from nominal thickness while a required upper-face detail is inaccessible behind an existing service. Obtain a section-specific installation response and resolve the assembly or route before buying materials and access.

**Mistake (hypothetical): A board product name replaces its system.** Another offer supplies boards but omits the accepted joint, support, penetration and access-panel details. It cannot be compared with a complete wrap assembly. Require the matched system and price every identified interface through inspection.
