Buy nonpenetrating surface supports only when the structural designer and roof system owner accept the plant, frame, bearing arrangement and complete roof build-up. Select supports connected to the structure where the design requires that load path. Release the affected panel lengths and flashings only after the selected support details are coordinated.

## Give the designer a plant schedule rather than a frame catalogue

List each item of plant, its operating and maintenance requirements, connections, support points and expected changes. Include the space needed to replace it, the personnel access route and the permanent service interfaces. An air-handling unit, its ducts and its associated pipes can have different support and penetration requirements. A surface-supported frame can address the unit's support without resolving a duct opening or a service crossing. Mark the function of each item on the plan to avoid buying the same support twice or omitting a weathering detail.

Give both proposed specialists the roof section, support layout, plant data, prevailing project actions and roof supplier's limitations. Obtain written acceptance for the path by which loads reach the structure and for the weathering arrangement. A flat support base visible in a catalogue does not establish a compatible seat on a ribbed roof. ## Track the affected metal work from support design to roofing

For a surface system, identify bearing or protection components accepted for the specific roof surface, how the roof will drain, access for inspection and who accepts the arrangement after the plant is connected. For a structural connection, identify the steel or curb scope, when it must be installed relative to the roof, and the specialist's approved flashing and closure details. The exact load design belongs to the engineer. Procurement needs enough information to determine which panels can run uninterrupted, which have coordinated ends or openings and which details are supplied separately.

Price the plant supported, connected and accepted on a weathered roof. For the surface route include approved spreader or protective components, roof assessment, setting out, any strengthening and future inspections that the design calls for. For the structurally attached route include structural work, procurement lead time, penetrations or terminations associated with that detail, specialist weathering and responsibility for handover. Include service openings common to both bids on both sides. Distinguish material quantities from the number of individual interfaces that need detailing.

## Tie machine orders to the released panel geometry

Compare the support overlay with the panel schedule and release unchanged field panels independently wherever the installation sequence permits. Identify the few roof zones whose cut lists, fabrication dates or site finishing depend on it. If the supports affect only a plant enclosure, commit the stable adjacent runs first where the roof system permits separate release. If a continuous run crosses several unresolved positions, protect the production slot and specify the final drawing cutoff. A roll former quote for uninterrupted field sheets does not include bespoke support frames or structural steel.

**Mistake: An unpenetrated support is priced as an unpenetrated roof.** A mechanical buyer chooses a surface frame and removes all openings from the roof estimate. The plant still has a duct passing through the roof. Put the duct, curb, weathering and commissioning responsibility back into both bids; then compare only the support-related difference.

**Mistake: The supports arrive after the panel package.** A purchaser releases a complete panel kit from a preliminary plant arrangement. The structural designer revises the support connections and several previously ordered details no longer fit. Release unaffected fields, hold the plant zone against a named design milestone and price any required revisions before manufacturing it.

Award the surface support where the complete approved design suits the roof and the service life plan. Choose the structural connection where that design is required or where its complete installed and maintained package offers the better outcome. Issue one coordinated interface schedule to the roof producer and mechanical contractor, with an explicit distinction between the plant's support and the building's service openings.

## Eight RFQ entries for roof and plant interfaces

1. Attach the plant schedule and maker's support requirements.
2. Provide the roof build-up, structure and current assembly revision.
3. Mark support points, drainage routes and service openings separately.
4. Name the designers accepting the load path and roof interface.
5. List fabricated steel, frames, pads, curbs and flashings by supplier.
6. Give panel mark groups, lengths and held roof zones.
7. Request construction-stage and retained inspection responsibilities.
8. State release dates, installed costs and plant replacement access.
