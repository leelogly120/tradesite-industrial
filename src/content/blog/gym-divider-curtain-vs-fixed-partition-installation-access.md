**Treat a retractable gym divider as an operable equipment system with its own supports, drive/control scope, commissioning and handover. Treat a fixed partition as a separate wall-construction package.** Buy personnel access after allocating those duties. A broad deck does not supply the divider's lifting mechanism or become a support for curtain components through a general fitout order.

Both packages divide a sports space, but their construction endpoints differ. A fixed partition is handed over as the specified installed assembly. An operable divider must also meet the nominated system's operating and acceptance requirements. Comparing only material area hides those additional obligations and the access visits associated with them.

## Start with the accepted division of the room

Have the operator and project designer release the intended room arrangement, including doors, circulation, stored equipment and the activities on each side. The access buyer should not select a partition type from the dimensions of an available machine.

For a moving divider, obtain its actual installed and stored geometry, building-support interfaces and specialist supply boundary. For a fixed partition, obtain its actual supporting frame, lining, junction and finish details. The building designer and selected product specialists determine those systems; procurement compares the resources needed to deliver each accepted result.

[Draper's gym-divider range](https://www.draperinc.com/gymequipment/gymdividers.aspx) includes different divider arrangements. Its [Top-Roll product information](https://www.draperinc.com/gymequipment/productdetail/185) illustrates that stored geometry and ceiling-level electrical connections belong to a specific product. Do not transfer that product's dimensions, ratings or installation details to another divider or an ARCLIFT platform.

## A moving system adds duties beyond hanging material

Ask the nominated installer to identify support work, specialist component placement, drive/control work, commissioning and user handover as actual deliverables. Assign a qualified provider to each. Include access to the relevant locations during the accepted system stages, without using the equipment enquiry to prescribe rigging, wiring or adjustment procedures.

For a fixed partition, allocate structure, lining, openings, finish and the specified inspections. It can also require several elevated stages. The comparison should therefore avoid the unsupported claim that one route always needs fewer visits. Use the accepted design and real provider scopes.

## Compare two complete fitout orders

Complete the worksheet from the room drawings and nominated product proposals. Identify the owner of each value so one bidder does not price another bidder's unprovided resource.

| Purchase field | Fillable project record | Responsible source and datum | Effect on access or cost |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Accepted room division | Operable/fixed ___; layout revision ___ | Operator and designer; room plan | Establishes the compared finished result |
| Building interfaces | Support/junction details ___ | Structural and product designers; issued sections | Defines installation locations and prerequisites |
| Product geometry | Installed ___; stored where applicable ___ | Nominated product supplier; system drawings | Identifies work and service positions |
| Specialist scope | Installation, controls and acceptance providers ___ | Main contractor; actual purchase boundaries | Exposes tasks outside platform supply |
| Material presentation | Components ___; handling provider ___ | Installer and handling specialist; delivered object schedule | Prices separate material equipment |
| Personnel tasks | Faces ___; levels ___; repeated positions ___ | Installer and surveyor; local floor/building datums | Tests the actual access arrangement |
| Working periods | Construction ___; checks ___; handover ___ | Programme owner; released court calendar | Establishes equipment occupation and returns |
| Completed cost | Product ___; specialists ___; access ___; reopening ___ | Buyer; same accepted handback | Supports the procurement choice |

An installer offering a complete operable system and a supplier offering only fabric are different purchases. Likewise, a fixed-wall lining price can exclude its supporting frame. Reconcile those boundaries before using either amount to support a platform investment.

## Keep specialist handling and personnel access separate

Obtain the actual dimensions, masses and handling information for the components supplied to the project. The appointed handling specialist defines how those objects reach their installation positions. A broad working deck should not inherit a curtain-support, frame-lifting or component-storage duty without an applicable documented equipment proposal and project acceptance.

Give the platform provider the crew, tools and simultaneous objects proposed for its personnel task. Keep the separately handled assembly visible on the coordinated layout, with its own provider and reserved space. Both proposals must address the same room condition and stage.

## Two hypothetical fitout purchases with missing capability

**Failure case: a curtain supply price becomes a complete divider budget.** The buyer allows for fabric and personnel access but leaves support work, the nominated operating system and commissioning outside every order. Obtain the full operable-system scope and named specialist appointments before committing the installation programme.

**Failure case: the platform is assumed to lift the divider components.** Another contractor purchases a deck for personnel and treats its apparent size as a material-handling solution. The specialist's required presentation equipment is unpriced. Keep those functions separate, then compare a coordinated layout that includes both actual packages.
