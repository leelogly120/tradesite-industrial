**Choose selected-bay wall renewal when the surveyed retained assembly and new-to-old interfaces have an accepted repair design. Buy whole-elevation renewal when the released design replaces the continuous wall assembly or a bounded repair cannot deliver the required result.** Include temporary enclosure, production-side release, handling and reopening in both prices. A smaller panel quantity is not automatically a cheaper completed project.

The decision concerns a vertical building enclosure. Work can affect the inside of a production bay while material handling and personnel access occupy the outside. A complete comparison connects those two sides. Copying an area rate from a roof-renewal job or a new-build wall order misses the retained-wall and operational interfaces.

## Turn the survey into actual renewal zones

Have the responsible building and wall specialists identify what remains serviceable, what receives a specified repair and what requires replacement. Locate those outcomes on the elevation. Include relevant supports, openings, junctions and previous alterations rather than mapping only visible panel damage.

For selected-bay work, obtain the accepted boundary between retained and new construction. Identify the actual replacement product and the released design for the intervening details. The responsible wall specialist must identify the retained assembly and accept those junctions before the replacement order proceeds.

For whole-elevation renewal, define the complete assembly and the starting condition supplied to the installer. Include support work and interfaces at corners, doors, adjoining elevations and the roof line wherever the released design requires them. Avoid calling an outer-skin supply quotation a complete wall-renewal package.

[Kingspan's wall facade information](https://www.panels.kingspan.com/en-gb/products/wall/rainscreen-facades) illustrates several product-system routes. The project's appointed designers must identify the selected assembly and retained interfaces. No manufacturer's catalogue can diagnose your existing wall from its appearance.

## Price the temporary boundary with production

The operator should identify which internal areas are released, for how long, and under which handback conditions. The responsible project specialists define any temporary enclosure, protection and environmental controls required by the actual work. Assign those duties to named providers and include their installation and removal in the programme.

Selected-bay renewal can reduce the area released at one time while adding repeated setup and closure operations. Whole-elevation renewal can reduce the number of construction boundaries while requiring a larger possession. Those are trade-offs to calculate from the actual programme, not universal claims about speed.

## Compare the same reopened building

Complete the table for selected-bay and whole-elevation proposals. The common endpoint is the accepted wall and the operator's agreed production-area handback, not merely the last panel supplied.

| Decision field | Fillable project entry | Responsible source and datum | Cost or access consequence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Existing condition | Retain/repair/replace zones ___ | Building and wall specialists; located survey | Establishes the bounded renewal scope |
| New assembly | Product ___; support/junction details ___ | Wall designer; issued elevation and sections | Defines construction operations |
| Retained interfaces | New-to-old boundary references ___ | Responsible designer; accepted repair details | Decides whether selected-bay work is viable |
| Production release | Internal areas ___; dates ___; handback ___ | Operator; production possession plan | Prices unavailable operating space |
| Temporary enclosure | Required scope ___; provider ___ | Project specialists; accepted temporary arrangement | Exposes work outside permanent cladding |
| Material services | Incoming/removed objects ___; handler ___ | Installer and handling provider; component schedule | Defines separate lifting, staging and waste scope |
| Personnel positions | Work faces ___; levels ___; setup zones ___ | Installer and surveyor; local ground/building datums | Tests the access configuration |
| Complete total | Mobilisations ___; closure ___; construction ___; reinstatement ___ | Buyer; same accepted handback | Supports the renewal strategy |

Where investigation is incomplete, purchase that investigation as a defined stage. Its output should be an actual condition and scope decision from the appointed specialists. Avoid locking the whole machine order to an invented final panel quantity before that decision exists.

## Keep new and removed material in the layout

The material provider must address the actual incoming components and removed items. Obtain dimensions, masses, condition assumptions and relevant handling information. Name the provider that presents replacements and manages the removed-material route. A personnel platform does not acquire a cladding-support or lifting duty because the contractor already owns it.

The same elevation should identify the workers' required sides and task positions. Some details require interior access, some exterior access and some separately scheduled approaches. Carry those distinctions into the equipment comparison instead of assuming an exterior deck completes every wall task.

The wall-access frame below makes the vertical workface relationship visible. Use it to discuss the location of people beside a wall; the actual project needs an accepted configuration for its own faces and material arrangement.

For a phased project, compare the full cost of repeating the access and temporary-boundary arrangement. For a whole-elevation project, include the larger staging and production-release requirements. An equipment quote based on one open bay should not silently become the proposal for the final occupied zone.

## Two hypothetical recladding orders with hidden work

**Failure case: local panel replacement has no accepted retained-wall detail.** A buyer orders matching-looking material for damaged bays while the surrounding assembly remains unidentified and its interface design is omitted. Resolve the actual repair design before committing selected-bay construction or compare complete renewal.

**Failure case: the whole-elevation price omits the temporary inside boundary.** Another bid includes new cladding and outside access but leaves production protection and reopening outside every order. Obtain those specialist scopes and compare both strategies at the same accepted building handback.
