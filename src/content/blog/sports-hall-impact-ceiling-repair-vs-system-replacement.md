**Buy a matched repair when the responsible ceiling specialists accept the retained assembly and specified replacement components together for the required sports use. Buy complete system renewal where that accepted repair route cannot be established.** Then price the access for inspection, repairs, restraints, panels and final acceptance. A panel that fits the opening is not, by itself, a complete impact-ceiling repair.

This decision goes beyond an ordinary tile replacement. The owner needs a ceiling assembly accepted for the specified hall use, with the components and installation details that support that outcome. A tender based only on visible damaged panels can miss the work that determines the true equipment period.

## Establish which assembly the hall actually contains

Obtain the existing ceiling records and a specialist condition survey. Identify panels, suspension, perimeter details, restraints, service openings and previous modifications. Map the condition by actual room and grid location. Where identity or condition remains unresolved, purchase a bounded investigation before treating an entire area as a known repair quantity.

[Rockfon's impact-resistance explanation](https://www.rockfon.co.uk/resources/faq/impact-resistance/) describes testing of the total ceiling system, including tiles and grid components. Its [Olympia Plus system information](https://www.rockfon.co.uk/products/rockfonsystem-olympia-plus-a-impact-1a/) also identifies a specific grid and hold-down arrangement. These are examples from Rockfon's own products, not an acceptance of your retained ceiling or a performance claim for ARCLIFT equipment.

The practical purchasing consequence is to order the actual accepted repair, not a similar-looking panel. Have the appointed designer and system provider identify compatible parts and the retained-system conditions. Where the nominated performance cannot be supported through that route, compare a complete replacement assembly.

## Make a repair scope that can be priced

Divide the survey outcome into accepted retention, specified repair and complete renewal zones. For a repair zone, list every required component and inspection, including work concealed after panels close. For renewal, include the actual support, perimeter, opening and service coordination scope rather than only the visible finish.

Keep damage investigation separate from assumptions about the cause. The ceiling and building specialists own the assessment. Procurement needs their resulting tasks, quantities and release decisions. The platform supplier receives the work positions and task loads, not an instruction to certify a ceiling through an equipment quotation.

The view beneath the deck below shows the working surface alongside roof members. Mark the actual fixing, restraint and inspection faces on the task sections. If repair includes a release between opening and closing, also price the equipment's waiting or return arrangement.

## Compare the accepted system outcomes

Use this worksheet with the hall operator, ceiling specialist and access buyer. Fill it from actual system and project records; no generic impact classification should be assigned from a photograph or product colour.

| Purchase field | Fillable project entry | Owner and reference basis | Decision use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Hall use | Required activities and ceiling specification ___ | Operator and designer; current use brief | Defines the accepted performance requirement |
| Existing assembly | System, panels, grid and restraints ___ | Ceiling specialist; records and located survey | Establishes the retained scope |
| Repair acceptance | Accepted component set ___; limits ___ | Responsible designer/system provider; written project decision | Decides whether matched repair proceeds |
| Renewal boundary | Support, perimeter and service scope ___ | Ceiling designer; replacement drawings | Prices the complete alternative |
| Work locations | Grid references ___; task faces ___ | Surveyor; levels from local floor and building datums | Defines the access positions |
| Inspection stages | Required checks ___; release owner ___ | Quality lead; inspection schedule | Determines reopening and closing visits |
| Site occupation | Court zones ___; protection ___; dates ___ | Hall operator; accepted possession plan | Prices lost space and reinstatement |
| Equipment basis | Crew ___; objects ___; supports/route ___ | Installer and equipment provider; actual configuration | Defines task load and building assessment |

A repair offer with clear retained-system acceptance can be compared with complete renewal. A quote for unidentified replacement tiles cannot. If the initial purchase is investigation, give it a defined output and price the construction decision after that output is available.

## Protect the sports programme in the complete comparison

The hall's unavailable period includes delivery, setup, ceiling work, checks, removal and floor handback. The operator should identify actual bookings and the conditions for reopening. Do not count a nominal school holiday or event gap as entirely available installation time.

Floor-finish protection and structural support are different purchases. Give the building reviewer the equipment supplier's configuration-specific reactions and the actual route. Give the floor specialist the proposed traffic and protection requirements. A covering selected to protect appearance does not answer the support question.

The hall-route photograph shows stones and wet patches in a construction setting. A finished sports court has a different surface, but its equipment delivery and supporting positions still need actual records and review. Mark the accepted complete arrangement on the court possession plan alongside material handling and removed-component routes.

## Two hypothetical repairs that leave the wrong result

**Failure case: a matching panel size replaces system acceptance.** A buyer orders tiles with the same face dimensions but does not identify the retained restraint and grid arrangement. The quotation answers appearance rather than the required ceiling system. Obtain the accepted component set and repair boundary before ordering material or fixing the platform duration.

**Failure case: access is booked only for visible damage.** Another estimate counts panel exchanges but omits the specified investigation and restraint work across adjoining locations. The crew needs additional visits after the original booking ends. Use the specialist's actual repair map and stage releases to price the complete campaign.
