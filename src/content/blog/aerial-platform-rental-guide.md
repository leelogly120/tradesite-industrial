# Aerial Platform Rental Guide: What to Know Before Renting

Renting an aerial platform can be more cost-effective than purchasing, especially for short-term projects. However, choosing the right rental equipment requires careful consideration. This guide covers everything you need to know before renting.

## When to Rent vs Buy

### Renting is Better When:

鉁?**Project duration < 6 months**
鉁?**Occasional use (< 500 hours/year)**
鉁?**Testing new equipment types**
鉁?**Budget constraints**
鉁?**Maintenance responsibilities unwanted**

### Buying is Better When:

鉁?**Project duration > 6 months**
鉁?**Regular use (> 500 hours/year)**
鉁?**Specific equipment requirements**
鉁?**Long-term cost savings desired**
鉁?**Maintenance control preferred**

### Additional Cost Factors

**Fuel/Electricity:**
- Diesel equipment: $15-25/day
- Electric equipment: $5-10/day
- Dual power: $10-15/day average

**Insurance:**
- Basic coverage: 10-15% of rental
- Full coverage: 20-25% of rental
- Liability waiver: 5-10% of rental

**Delivery:**
- Local (< 50 km): $100-200
- Regional (50-200 km): $200-500
- Long distance (> 200 km): $500-1,000+

**Training:**
- Operator training: $500-1,000
- Safety certification: $200-500
- On-site training: $1,000-2,000

## Equipment Selection Criteria

### Project Requirements

**Height:**
- Measure actual working height
- Add 2-3m safety margin
- Select equipment with adequate reach

**Platform Size:**
- Calculate worker requirements
- Estimate material needs
- Select adequate platform area

**Ground Conditions:**
- Identify floor type
- Calculate load requirements
- Select appropriate ground pressure

**Access Constraints:**
- Measure doorways and corridors
- Assess turning radius needs
- Verify equipment dimensions

### Application Requirements

**Indoor Use:**
- Electric mode required
- Low noise required
- Zero emissions required
- Low ground pressure required

**Outdoor Use:**
- Diesel power acceptable
- Weather resistance needed
- Extended runtime needed
- Rough terrain capability

**Mixed Use:**
- Dual power recommended
- Flexible operation needed
- Versatile equipment preferred

## Rental Agreement Essentials

### Key Terms to Understand

**Rental Period:**
- Daily: Minimum 1 day
- Weekly: 7-day minimum
- Monthly: 28-30 day minimum
- Long-term: 3+ months

**Insurance Coverage:**
- Physical damage: Covers equipment damage
- Liability: Covers third-party damage
- Personal injury: Covers operator injury
- Loss/theft: Covers equipment loss

**Maintenance Responsibilities:**
- Daily checks: Renter responsibility
- Scheduled maintenance: Varies by agreement
- Breakdown repair: Usually owner responsibility
- Wear items: Usually renter responsibility

### Red Flags in Rental Agreements

鉂?**Excessive insurance requirements**
鉂?**Hidden fees not disclosed upfront**
鉂?**Unreasonable damage charges**
鉂?**No maintenance support**
鉂?**Restrictive usage limitations**

## Rental Best Practices

### Before Rental

1. **Define requirements**: Height, platform, duration
2. **Research options**: Compare rental companies
3. **Get quotes**: Multiple companies
4. **Read agreements**: Understand terms
5. **Plan logistics**: Delivery, setup, removal

### During Rental

1. **Inspect equipment**: Document condition
2. **Train operators**: Ensure proper use
3. **Follow maintenance**: Daily checks
4. **Monitor usage**: Track hours and conditions
5. **Document issues**: Report problems immediately

### After Rental

1. **Return clean**: Equipment in good condition
2. **Document condition**: Photos and notes
3. **Complete paperwork**: Rental return forms
4. **Review charges**: Verify final invoice
5. **Provide feedback**: Help improve service

## Common Rental Mistakes

### Mistake 1: Choosing Based on Price Only

**Problem:** Cheapest option may not be best quality

**Solution:** Consider total value, not just price

### Mistake 2: Ignoring Insurance

**Problem:** Insufficient coverage exposes you to risk

**Solution:** Understand coverage options and choose appropriately

### Mistake 3: Skipping Training

**Problem:** Untrained operators cause accidents

**Solution:** Invest in proper training

### Mistake 4: Not Reading Agreement

**Problem:** Hidden fees and restrictive terms

**Solution:** Read and understand all terms before signing

### Mistake 5: Poor Documentation

**Problem:** Disputes over equipment condition

**Solution:** Document condition before and after rental

## Rental Cost Optimization

### Tips to Reduce Costs

1. **Book in advance**: Better rates for advance booking
2. **Bundle services**: Rental + training + insurance
3. **Longer periods**: Monthly rates often better value
4. **Off-peak rental**: Lower rates during slow seasons
5. **Multiple projects**: Combine rentals for better rates

### Negotiation Strategies

1. **Compare quotes**: Use competition to negotiate
2. **Volume discount**: Multiple units or long-term
3. **Package deals**: Bundle services for savings
4. **Loyalty programs**: Repeat customer benefits
5. **Referral programs**: Introduce new customers
