**For a released unitized curtain wall, buy specialist unit delivery and positioning together with personnel access for the retained anchors, connections, joints and closure checks. For a released stick system, buy access for its actual framing, field-glazing, sealing and acceptance stages.** Compare complete eligible packages through the same accepted facade and required closure dates. Choose the supported lower-cost route, or the accepted route meeting a required closure date the alternative cannot serve. Where the design fixes the system, price that system's full retained work.

The facade designer and system provider establish eligible assemblies and project performance. This buying decision concerns the installation and access commitments that follow. A factory-assembled label is insufficient: the actual offer must show what glass and framing arrive, what remains on site and which party accepts each handover.

## Read the quoted glazing state before reducing site access

Unitized supply is a real product family, but its commercial boundary still needs reading. [Kawneer's 2500 PG Wall information](https://www.kawneer.com/products/curtain-wall/2500-pg-wall-curtain-wall-system/) describes stock-length, fabricated and glazed supply options. Obtain the actual offered state rather than assuming a system name buys a complete pre-glazed unit.

For a factory-glazed unit, identify its approved unit drawing, glazing make-up, supplied components and factory acceptance records. State the handover condition and the work retained after arrival. For a stick proposal, obtain the actual preparation and delivery state of the members and glass. [Woodbridge describes its stick-system scope](https://www.woodbridgeglass.com/services/stick-built-curtain-wall/) as field assembly and glazing, with different levels of factory preparation.

The comparison therefore concerns specific supply contracts. Price the resources for the work each quotation leaves at the building. A unitized frame delivered without glazing and a finished glazed unit have different handling objects and remaining site tasks, even when their sales descriptions use the same system family.

## Structural release and weather closure need separate dates

Ask the facade team for the issued anchor, joint and building-interface details and the structural handovers required before its installation package starts. The building engineer owns the structural information and acceptance; the facade specialist owns its system response. Procurement records when those inputs become available and which installation resources rely on them.

These are system-specific interfaces. [WICONA's unitized-facade information](https://www.wicona.com/en-int/professionals/products/facades-wictec/unitised-facade) describes workshop-manufactured elements and identifies horizontal-joint arrangements related to slab-edge movement. Use the selected project's released details for its actual interface; another product's joint description is a category example, not a substitute design.

Keep unit placement, completed joints and accepted weather closure as distinct programme events where the contract distinguishes them. Identify perimeter interfaces, corners, opening units and exceptional zones separately. A main-field installation date should not silently become the building's complete enclosure handover.

Keep that review connected to the same construction stage used by the facade programme.

## Price replacement and exceptional zones with the main field

For unitized work, connect the delivery calendar to actual unit identities and required locations. Agree who receives and records supplied units, who resolves discrepancies and what the replacement transaction covers. Include the access and handling consequences of an affected location remaining open while surrounding work proceeds.

For stick work, allocate the corresponding member, glass and sealing responsibilities. Include the accepted route for replacement components and any required return to complete the zone. Use real supplier terms and programme assumptions; a generic installation advantage cannot price those events.

Specialist glass or unit handling remains a separate function from personnel access. The [cladding-platform and handling comparison](/blog/wall-cladding-platform-and-vacuum-lifter-package/) explains that equipment boundary. Here the selected facade system determines the actual objects, handovers and closure obligations to which both equipment proposals must respond.

## Two hypothetical facade handover failures

**Hypothetical failure case: a unitized system name becomes a factory-glazed delivery promise.** A contractor reduces site glazing and access allowances before reading an offer for a different preparation state. The missing work reaches site without a provider. Reconcile the delivered glazing state and remaining tasks before comparing installation totals.

**Hypothetical failure case: placed units are counted as accepted closure.** Another programme releases the access resource after the main units arrive, while perimeter interfaces and an exceptional replacement location remain incomplete. Price all specified closure and acceptance stages, including the actual return resources, before promising the finished zone.
