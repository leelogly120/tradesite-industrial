<!-- audit-section: buyer-intent -->
Choose a rechargeable ceiling-platform proposal when its documented duty and recharge requirements fit your shift calendar, and the facility can reserve a suitable bay and electrical supply for that arrangement. If either condition fails, change the facility plan or compare another documented power package before ordering. Every platform needs a parking plan; charging belongs in that plan only when the offered configuration includes rechargeable equipment.

For contractors working inside occupied buildings, the useful purchase is a platform that can return to work at the next agreed start time. A charger included in the quotation solves only part of that problem. The bay must remain available, the return route must remain usable, and the facility must accept the proposed charging arrangement.

The folded equipment below shows why parking needs a complete envelope. The deck, supports and projecting equipment extend beyond the tracks. Use the supplier's drawing for the intended parked state when allocating space.

![Green crawler equipment with folded upper assembly and extended supports beside an industrial building](/images/equipment/buyer-20261029-deck-folded.webp)

*Reserve space for the complete parked arrangement and access around it, rather than measuring only the crawler tracks.*

## Put the bay and the next shift on one sheet

Start with two records: a facility reservation and the offered equipment schedule. The facility team owns the usable space and supply; the supplier owns the configuration requirements. Compare their entries before treating the charging package as complete. An outlet location without its available supply characteristics is insufficient for that comparison.

Copy this table into the procurement brief. Fill the blanks with site measurements or the corresponding offer values; retain the drawing or document revision beside each entry.

| Input to record | Measurement datum and buyer entry | Responsible party | Decision the entry controls |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Reserved parking bay | Grid reference ___; clear length/width/height ___ between fixed boundaries | Facility manager measures; supplier supplies parked envelope | Accept the bay only when the complete arrangement and specified access space fit |
| Floor support | Slab area and drawing revision ___; joints/openings marked ___ | Structural reviewer compares floor records with supplier reactions | Approve the specific bay or allocate a different supported location |
| Power at the bay | Outlet ID ___; voltage/frequency/phase and available capacity ___ at that outlet | Site electrical lead records supply; supplier states charger input | Include required site electrical work in the purchase budget |
| Available charging interval | Bay released ___; next shift starts ___; interruptions ___ | Facility scheduler and contractor | Compare usable time with the offered recharge requirement |
| Return route | Narrowest opening ___; lowest obstruction ___ from route floor; route revision ___ | Site surveyor and supplier | Retain this bay or choose another reachable location |
| Charging equipment scope | Battery/charger identifiers ___; included items ___; purchaser items ___ | Supplier and purchasing lead | Compare complete packages and assign each excluded item |

Record installation, checks and handover as separate responsibilities. The purchasing team should receive the applicable charger information and facility acceptance record together. [IPAF's MEWP battery guidance](https://www.ipaf.org/en/resource-library/safe-use-maintenance-batteries-fitted-mewps) covers charging, storage and maintenance by battery type; its charging section refers users to the manufacturer's instructions for the actual arrangement. Use that document to scope the handover, with the site electrical lead responsible for the local supply interface.

## A nearby bay and a central bay carry different costs

Compare the available energy arrangements in the [dual-power selection guide](/blog/dual-power-crawler-platform-selection/) before applying this bay-and-shift review to a rechargeable offer.

A bay beside the work zone is preferable when it remains reserved throughout the required charging interval and its supply suits the quoted charger. Its advantage is a shorter return route. Its constraint is competition with the next trade: material deliveries, cleaning and overnight occupation all belong on the same calendar.

A central equipment area is preferable when it offers dependable access and a suitable supply while the work zone changes each day. The trade-off is the additional route and time needed to return the machine and bring it back. Include those movements in the shift plan using the supplier's documented movement conditions. A central bay is unusable for that shift if the return route is locked before the machine can enter.

The hall photo below shows a route with water, loose ground material and other equipment. Route readiness is a separate deliverable from bay readiness. Assign the contractor responsible for making that route available and the reviewer responsible for accepting it.

![Open steel hall with pooled water and loose material along the ground route beside access equipment](/images/equipment/buyer-20261102-hall-ground-route.webp)

*Include the condition and availability of the full return route in the bay reservation, especially while construction changes the floor.*

For an overnight programme, compare the supplier's stated recharge assumptions with the hours the facility actually releases. Do not count the entire gap between shifts if part of it belongs to cleaning or access restrictions. For consecutive shifts, request a proposal covering that duty explicitly. An extra charger does not resolve a schedule with no interval for the offered charging arrangement.

<!-- audit-section: limitations-not-fit -->
## Two purchasing failures to prevent

**Failure 1: the charger arrives, but the supply package is incomplete.** In a hypothetical hall fit-out, the contractor orders a rechargeable option and assumes a wall outlet is sufficient. The offer identifies a different supply requirement, and site electrical work was excluded. The installation start slips. Prevent this by matching the named outlet to the offered charger input before comparing prices, then assigning any adaptation work and its completion date.

**Failure 2: the overnight bay belongs to another trade.** In a hypothetical shared hall, a buyer selects a convenient corner without booking it. The cleaning contractor occupies that area after the installation shift, leaving less time than the offered charging arrangement requires. Prevent this with a reservation covering access, the charging interval and the next departure. If the facility cannot provide that window, the rechargeable proposal does not fit the stated programme; change the programme or the offered arrangement.

<!-- audit-section: project-checklist -->
## Attach these eight items to the RFQ

- Ceiling task, work height measured from the setup floor, and material dimensions carried for the task.
- Shift calendar with work periods, bay-release times and required next-shift readiness.
- Parking-bay plan with measured boundaries, floor records and the facility manager's allocation.
- Return-route drawing with door dimensions, floor slope, overhead geometry and current access photos.
- Electrical schedule identifying the proposed outlet, supply characteristics and site electrical lead.
- Required battery and charger scope, with purchaser-supplied equipment and installation responsibilities listed.
- Destination country, site delivery access and transport or container constraints for the complete package.
- Facility charging requirements, environmental restrictions and the named documentation and handover reviewers.
