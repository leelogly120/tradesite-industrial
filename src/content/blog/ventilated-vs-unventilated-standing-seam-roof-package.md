Choose a ventilated standing-seam zinc assembly when the designer and roofing system supplier accept the proposed build-up and can trace a continuous ventilation route through the actual roof geometry. Price an unventilated warm assembly when they accept that complete system, including its substrate, continuous control layers and junctions. If the offers use different assemblies, compare the cost of completing each roof before awarding the sheet package.

A roof contractor can receive two apparently identical prices for standing-seam pans while one bidder has allowed for ventilation openings and the other expects a fully prepared warm substrate. The decisive drawing is a section through the entire roof, supplemented by details at the awkward places. Get those drawings into the enquiry before comparing coil or roll-forming prices.

## Follow the route at the difficult roof detail

Select the area that is hardest to build as drawn. A plain slope offers little challenge for a tender comparison. A valley, change of level, interruption around plant or junction with an adjacent wall reveals missing parts and unclear responsibility. Ask the designer to take the chosen detail through each permitted roof alternative, including how the assembly continues across that feature.

In its UK guidance, VMZINC describes cold roof assemblies with ventilation space beneath the substrate supporting the zinc, and warm assemblies without that space, with the structure on the warm side of the insulation. It specifies particular substrates, zinc products, membranes and other components for its arrangements. Those references belong to the manufacturer's systems and market. Use the chosen system supplier's detail revision for the project. [VMZINC UK roof build-ups](https://www.vmzinc.com/en-gb/technical-support/general-technical-recommendations/roof-and-wall-build-ups) and [standing-seam substrates](https://www.vmzinc.com/en-gb/substrates)

For the ventilated option, find the stated air inlet and outlet for the bay on the released details. Record the path between them, the trades providing the space and openings, and any treatment needed around an interruption. Price the supporting layers, roof-edge details and inspection before concealment. If a parapet change removes the designed outlet, return the detail to the design team. A generic ventilation allowance on a cost sheet buys little until the revised route has a construction detail.

## What replaces that ventilated space in the warm bid?

The unventilated route calls for the supplier's complete warm assembly. Identify the approved zinc material and its underside treatment, supporting substrate, membrane, insulation, vapour-control provision, clips and junction details. Different approved systems arrange these components differently. Trace the specified continuous control layer at a penetration and at the wall junction. Give each connection a named trade and put protection of the work between visits into the programme.

Ask which materials must arrive dry, when the substrate and concealed layers will be inspected, and what will qualify an area for the metal roofer to start. For either route, record the party carrying the cost of reopening an area that was closed before the specified inspection. This matters commercially when one company installs the supporting assembly and another forms and lays the zinc.

Require both bidders to mark substitutions against the same system drawing. A change from an identified substrate, membrane or underside finish alters the offered assembly. Give the proposed change to the responsible designer and system supplier for a documented decision before pricing it as an equivalent. The fastest apparent saving often comes from quietly moving an expensive transition to another trade.

When both assemblies are accepted for the same area, compare their installed totals on the same programme. Include specialist junctions, substrate work, openings or control-layer transitions, protection between shifts and inspections before concealment. Choose the lower complete cost if both meet the owner's dates. If the cheaper option misses an agreed enclosure date, price the resulting temporary works or delay against the alternative before award.

## Determine what remains for the forming line

Take the zinc supplier's approved specification and the released panel drawing to the forming-equipment bidder. Name the face condition, back coating where specified, seam geometry, mating details, lengths and sample acceptance. Ask the bidder to identify the offered tooling and proposed trial with representative material. Include handling and protection of the accepted faces between the outlet and installation.

A roofer buying factory-finished pans needs a price for the roof assembly and installation. A roofer retaining pan manufacture needs an additional order for a machine capable of making the accepted section from the designated zinc. Count only those field sheets, with the actual repeat orders, in the line investment. The clips, insulation, substrate and specialist junctions remain separate purchases unless specifically included by the respective suppliers.

## Two mistakes in apparently comparable roof prices

**Mistake one.** Suppose a bidder prices a ventilated plain slope across an entire roof. A later parapet detail interrupts the air route, and the budget has no allowance for the designed outlet. Require a priced, accepted junction for the obstructed zone before extending the plain-slope rate to it.

**Mistake two.** A warm-roof bid includes an outer zinc product but leaves continuity at an upstand to the next trade. The supporting layers are covered before that trade’s work is inspected. Put the transition, temporary protection and inspection release into a named work package before placing the pan order.

## RFQ for two complete zinc assemblies

- Attach the roof sections, inclinations and difficult junction detail revisions.
- State the building use and the designer's moisture-related design brief.
- List the accepted system and material references for each roof zone.
- Mark the complete ventilation route and responsibility for its terminals.
- Detail the warm assembly's continuous layers and adjoining trade interfaces.
- Identify the inspection and temporary weather-protection owner at each release.
- Supply the zinc specification, seam drawing, pan lengths and mating sample.
- Separate bought-in pans or accessories from the retained forming quantities.
