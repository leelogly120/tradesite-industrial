Choose a perforated-liner roof assembly when a new building needs room absorption across the roof underside and the accepted acoustic design uses that complete construction. Keep a solid liner and buy separate absorbers when treatment must follow selected work areas, remain independently replaceable or fit a later refurbishment. Give the roof contractor and acoustic specialist the same occupied-room brief before releasing the weather-sheet order. For an existing sound roof with a retained solid liner, favour separate treatment when it meets the accepted room brief without replacing that liner.

A large metal roof creates two purchasing opportunities: the external weather sheet and the internal acoustic construction. Decide the latter from the room's intended use. A production machine belongs in the weather-sheet package only after the required metal product and repeat workload are defined.

## Start with the room result

List the activities that need the acoustic result: speech, assembly work, equipment operation or another specified use. The appointed acoustic specialist sets the room condition, spaces, measurement method and acceptance target used by both bidders. An empty-hall requirement and an occupied-room requirement need clearly identified assumptions. Put the accepted occupancy, equipment and furnishing condition in both quotations.

[Tata Steel's Trisobuild acoustic explanation](https://www.tatasteeluk.com/construction/blogs-news/blogs/acoustic-design-and-performance-using-trisobuild-systems) distinguishes sound absorption from sound reduction and describes perforated liners with specified backing within its tested systems. The useful procurement distinction is the complete assembly. A hole pattern or insulation product alone does not define the room result.

## Integrated absorption changes the roof contract

With an integrated liner, the roofer installs a specified interior face and its associated acoustic layers as part of the roof assembly. Price the identified liner, backing, supports, joints, closures and installation. Coordinate the construction's other duties with the responsible designers, including environmental control, structure and the specified internal finish.

Choose this route where the planned roof underside is the appropriate treatment surface and the complete accepted assembly fits the programme. It gives procurement one defined roof construction to release. The order must retain the actual configuration supporting the acoustic proposal, including designated solid areas and junctions.

An integrated liner is a weak choice when the building owner expects frequent changes that would obstruct or disturb its treatment surface. Draw future mezzanines, process enclosures and service zones on the room plan. If the useful treatment area disappears behind later work, evaluate a different arrangement before ordering the liner.

## Separate absorbers create a second delivery package

Separate treatment follows the specialist's defined locations rather than the full roof area. Obtain a layout identifying panels, baffles or other selected products, suspension or mounting, surrounding clearances and the access needed for replacement. Select the actual products for the building's cleaning, impact and contamination conditions.

The solid roof liner still has its own specified duties. Price it as a completed roof component. Add the treatment system's design, supports, installation, coordination and acceptance to the separate package. Comparing a perforated-liner assembly with the price of loose absorbers leaves essential work outside the estimate.

Separate treatment makes sense where it can be installed after wet or dusty work, follows selected occupied zones or needs independent replacement. Reserve the installation window and access route. A low material price loses its advantage if the accepted treatment cannot be installed after machinery or racking occupies the floor.

Complete the record using actual locations. A treatment area stated only as a percentage of roof area is insufficient when services occupy the most useful surfaces. Have the specialist compare the coordinated layouts before the buyer selects either package.

## Two avoidable scope errors

**Hypothetical mistake: an absorption class becomes the entire acoustic brief.** A contractor buys a perforated liner for a new assembly hall from a product label, then substitutes an ordinary backing and omits the selected junction details. The delivered roof differs from the accepted proposal. Order the complete named construction and review any substitution through the acoustic and roof designers.

**Hypothetical mistake: separate absorbers arrive after their access disappears.** A warehouse estimate assumes treatment will be fitted after occupation, but the suspended units lie above fixed racking. Move installation ahead of racking or price a deliverable later access package before award. Retain that work window in the main programme.
