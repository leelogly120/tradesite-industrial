An importer's supply package is three lists: what leaves the upstream factory gate as equipment, what you add in your market, and what the contractor is told. A catalog family name does not write those lists. This page is not a distributor agreement, a territory, or a margin.

**Contents**

- What the downstream buyer must receive
- What you add locally
- What you are allowed to claim
- Gaps that should stop the order
- How to enquire

## Start from the contractor's handover

### The machine they think they bought
Write the sentence your customer will read: lift only, lift plus former, or lift plus former plus chassis. If you cannot write it, you are not ready to order upstream. [ARC-T25](/products/arc-t25-truck-mounted-roll-forming-lift/) and [ARC-C25](/products/arc-c25-crawler-roll-forming-lift/) are catalog models you can name. They are not a private-label contract.

### Documents that must travel with the serial
Outline, interface, and the list of what is unknown until the quotation. Your customer will ask for a load. You should be able to say "that number is in the signed schedule, not on the website." If your sales sheet copies a number from this site, stop. This site does not publish platform loads.

### Language and the nameplate
Say which language the contractor reads and which name stays on the machine. Do not promise a certification mark the destination has not asked for. If a local engineer must sign the method, that engineer is part of your package, not part of the catalog photo.

## Split the work on purpose

### Upstream scope
Equipment modules, drawings for those modules, and the packing list for those modules. Former tooling stays with [ARC-RF8](/products/arc-rf8-roll-forming-machine/) if it is in the buy. Chassis included yes, no, or unsure, written on the order, not implied by the word truck.

### Your scope
Local chassis, local trailer, registration, assembly yard, training, and spares you choose to stock. Each of those can be "not us". Blank is better than a brochure that says "full solution".

### The contractor's scope
Site power, slabs, road permits, and the crew. You can help them ask the questions. You cannot absorb their roof.

## The trade-off is a complete story against a flexible stock unit

A fully described package is easier to resell and harder to hold for the next contractor, because the chassis and the former are job-specific. A bare lift module is easier to ship and harder to explain at the counter. The constraint is the interface you are willing to own. Pick it before you print a leaflet.

## This package may not fit

It may not fit when you need an exclusive territory this page cannot grant, when you need a price list for machines that are quoted per roof, or when your customer wants a basket and you are about to sell them a former lift. It may not fit when you cannot name who registers the vehicle. That blank will become the contractor's complaint.

## Package RFQ

- Work height and outreach to the outlet you marked
- Roof geometry, slope, and obstructions
- Profile, material, thickness, and coil data
- Wind exposure and the stop rule
- Ground or floor under the setup
- Access route into the site
- Outrigger or track footprint
- Destination country and port
- Chassis or carrier included, local, or unsure
- Transport or container assumption, if any
- Voltage and who owns the controls
- Documents the destination will ask to see

<!-- audit-section: cta-editorial-note -->
## Enquire as a supply package

Send the work height, the site, the destination, the transport assumption, the work zone, and the floor or ground note. The signed technical schedule is project-specific. Editorial images do not set a price, a load, or a lead time. Use [request a quote](/contact/?request=quote) and state that you resell. The <a href="https://www.cbp.gov/trade/basic-import-export" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">CBP import and export overview</a> is a United States customs doorway, not a classification of ARCLIFT equipment.

## FAQ

#### Will this page appoint a distributor?
No. It tells you how to describe a package. Commercial terms belong in a separate conversation.

#### Can I publish the catalog height class?
Yes, if you call it a catalog class and you do not add a load or a price the site does not state.

#### Who answers the contractor's technical question?
Agree that in the package. Upstream can review an RFQ. Your local engineer may still own the method.

#### What if the chassis is always local?
Write "chassis included: no" on every order so the module and the vehicle never get mixed in the invoice.
