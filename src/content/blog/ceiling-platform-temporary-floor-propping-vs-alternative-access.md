**Fund temporary propping for a ceiling platform when an engineered scheme releases every required travel and work position and the building operator can give the contractor the supporting space below for the full period. Buy an alternative access package when it completes the same ceiling tasks within accepted structural limits at lower disruption or cost.** Where neither route has an accepted support arrangement, commission a revised access design before reserving equipment.

A floor assessment can rule out an unmodified platform position without settling how to complete the job. Procurement now faces a different decision. One offer buys access equipment plus temporary structural works, occupation of lower floors and their eventual return. The other changes the equipment, work positions or access direction. Compare both routes through completion of the ceiling work, rather than comparing the price of prop hire with the day rate for another machine.

## Start below the proposed platform

On a section through the building, mark the ceiling task, the intended platform level and the spaces below. Identify which areas are in use, who controls them and which routes must remain open. Give the engineer the actual proposed machine configuration, route and reactions for each relevant state. Include travel, turns and set-up as well as the working position. A structural release covering a work bay is of little use if the machine has to pass through an unassessed transfer corridor.

IPAF’s guidance on ground conditions and supporting structures covers mobile elevating work platforms on suspended floors. It addresses the ability of the structure to carry the forces from the machine and the importance of support in the relevant configurations. Procure the building-specific investigation and design from the competent project parties. Their decision should identify the route and the limits of the release, in terms the site manager can match to the actual job. [IPAF guidance](https://www.ipaf.org/en-us/resource-library/assessment-ground-conditions-and-supporting-structures-safe-use-mewps)

## Buy the structural works as a complete temporary installation

The propping bid should identify its design boundary, the people responsible for checking and accepting the scheme, the levels and positions it occupies, and the beginning and end of that occupation. Include installation access, disruption to tenants, protection of equipment, inspections, removal and reinstatement. Identify the work needed to verify that the building records match the site. Include the price of gaining access for that verification.

Temporary support below heavy plant is a real specialist service. Legend Hire describes a designed and installed back-propping system for a mobile crane on a suspended slab. That case concerns a particular crane and building. A platform project requires its own machine information, structural assessment and scheme. Use the example to understand the scope of the service to purchase. [Legend Hire project account](https://legendhire.com.au/case-study/temporary-crane-back-propping-system-suspended-slab-application-sydney-project/)

Ask the building operator to price lost use downstairs against the actual dates. A loading area can be available for a weekend survey and unavailable during the following month’s delivery programme. A tenant might release one room while retaining an adjoining corridor required for servicing the temporary works. Price the entire occupation from installation through removal, including the consequences of a delayed ceiling handover.

## Make the competing access bid reach the same work

An alternative machine is a credible bid when the engineer accepts its actual loads at the proposed positions and the access planner can demonstrate the working envelope at each task. Enter the crew, tools, panel or component handling by the assigned separate provider, horizontal offset and obstruction for each representative work front. A lighter base that cannot reach an obstructed run leaves a subcontract still to buy. An offer working from another level needs its own structural and access assessment.

If an alternative needs several moves to cover the area served by one large deck, cost the change in installation hours and return visits. Equally, give that route full credit for lower-floor space it genuinely releases. If one method completes most tasks and another is needed for a difficult bay, price the split explicitly. The decision can be to support a single bay temporarily while completing the remaining areas by a different accepted method.

## Two hypothetical purchasing mistakes

**The props release the destination but leave no way in.** The temporary-works quotation covers the platform’s final bay. Delivery requires crossing a suspended passage outside the marked scheme. The machine remains at the unloading point. Price a reviewed access route or a different entry and work package before committing the asset.

**The lower floor is promised twice.** Procurement compares a low propping hire price with a higher specialist-access bid. The tenant has a firm dispatch commitment during the propping period, so its loading lane must move off site. Include relocation, return and programme exposure in the propped option. Review the competing access bid against that revised total.
