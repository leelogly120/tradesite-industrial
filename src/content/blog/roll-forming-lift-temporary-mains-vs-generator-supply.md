**Choose temporary mains when the complete connection is ready before power is needed for readiness checks and production, its assessed supply covers the machine duty, and its programme cost is lower. Choose a qualified generator package when it meets the full duty and site conditions but the mains route misses that window or lacks the required available supply. Where both qualify, award the lower complete total over the same production period.** Obtain dated offers against one equipment-and-load schedule before releasing the power purchase.

This decision starts with an identified electrically supplied former, lift and any powered entry or receiving equipment. Their internal drives remain fixed. The purchase is the external source that serves their required production states, together with the work and attendance needed to keep that source available.

## A connection date belongs on the production programme

Begin at the first date accepted panels must leave the line. Work backwards through the actual equipment arrival, assembly and readiness checks. The required power window includes those activities as well as normal forming. An energy tariff for a connection that starts later addresses a different period.

[UK Power Networks’ temporary connection process](https://www.ukpowernetworks.co.uk/new-electricity-connections/connect-1-4-properties-domestic-small-business/temporary-electricity-connection-what-happens-after-i-apply) illustrates separate survey, offer, preparation, network work and electricity-supplier meter attendance. That network example explains why a connection offer and usable supply are different milestones. Obtain the actual sequence and dates from the providers serving this site.

If the connection arrives after power is first required for readiness or production, either change the accepted programme or price a qualified interim source for the uncovered period. Keep a later mains changeover as a separate planned event, with its own attendance and permitted interruption. Calling the generator temporary does not fund that event.

## A generator rating answers only part of the question

[Caterpillar’s commercial generator sizing guidance](https://www.cat.com/en_US/by-industry/electric-power/electric-power-resources/genset-sizing.html) uses starting and running loads, operating steps, electrical characteristics and site conditions. Its discussion also explains the dependence of a result on supplied inputs. A headline rating alone leaves the particular application undefined.

Have the machine providers identify each supplied subsystem and its electrical requirements. The responsible electrical designer and power provider assess the intended combination, including loads sharing the source and the actual starting arrangements. A bare sum of brochure figures cannot express that whole operating pattern.

Specify a production duty to the generator provider. A standby description and an attractive rental rate are insufficient grounds for buying ordinary shift production. Give the provider the actual shift calendar, load pattern and required service windows. Obtain its proposed configuration, applicable duty basis, site-condition assessment and support schedule for that workload. Detailed source selection, distribution, protection and electrical verification belong to the appointed qualified providers.

Mains needs its own availability assessment. Record the supply allocation agreed for the forming operation, the other users affecting it and the periods for which that allocation is available. An existing board on the site plan is useful only when its available supply and access window serve the intended equipment.

## Price the same power window

For mains, include the actual connection work, customer preparation, distribution boundary, energy charges and agreed removal or reinstatement obligations. For a generator, include the delivered set, external distribution, fuel provision, required service attendance, relocation if priced, and final collection. Identify owner-provided resources beside each offer. Obtain the energy or fuel cost basis for the actual workload; comparing unit rates alone leaves consumption and attendance unpriced.

Use one project period and one agreed production calendar. Keep common distribution work common, so it is neither omitted from both offers nor charged twice to one route. For an owned generator, state the buyer’s ownership period and residual-value assumption separately from the cost of serving this one project.

If both routes meet the same programme and accepted duty, choose the lower complete total. If a source misses a required release, first price an accepted revised programme or a complete bridge arrangement. An unavailable cheap source is not an equivalent production offer.

## Two mistakes in hypothetical power purchases

**Mistake: the meter date falls after the panel release.** A buyer compares a low connection-and-energy offer with a generator covering the whole programme. The mains meter attendance is scheduled after the first roof zone requires panels. Correct the comparison by including an accepted interim supply, its mobilisation/collection and the appointed changeover attendance, or obtain an approved revised release programme before ranking the routes.

**Mistake: the rental rate becomes the total.** Another buyer compares a bare generator rental against a mains offer that includes distribution. Fuel delivery, servicing and final collection remain owner costs, and the equipment’s actual starting states were never submitted. Reissue the complete operating schedule, obtain an accepted production proposal and reconcile both offers at the same distribution endpoint.
