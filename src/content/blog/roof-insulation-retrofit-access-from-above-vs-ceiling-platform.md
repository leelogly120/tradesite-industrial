**Use a ceiling-platform package for a designer-accepted insulation retrofit installed from below when the existing weathering roof can remain and the interior provides usable work zones. Combine insulation with exterior roof renewal when the accepted assembly requires access from above or the roof already needs replacement. Choose the retrofit assembly first; then compare complete access and production packages for the side from which the work must happen.**

An interior project preserves a different set of building conditions from an exterior project. It occupies floor space beneath the roof. An exterior package occupies the roof and its material supply route. Neither route wins from insulation area alone, and a platform purchase cannot settle moisture, fire, structural or thermal design requirements.

## Separate the building improvement from the equipment purchase

The building owner and appointed envelope designer first define the required improvement and acceptable construction. Their input includes the existing roof build-up, its condition, services, moisture findings and the proposed finished assembly. The installer then identifies the work that the approved scheme requires.

A manufacturer example shows why that sequence matters. [Silvercote's Energy Saver Retro Roof instructions](https://www.silvercote.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/SIL400_005-Energy-Saver-Retro-Roof-Install-Instructions.pdf) describe a retrofit around the purlin cavity and explicitly distinguish the case with existing insulation above the purlins. That qualification belongs to that named system. It demonstrates why an empty underside photograph is insufficient to select a retrofit assembly; it does not authorize the same solution for another building.

Keep the design decision with the responsible specialist. The equipment buyer needs the released installation faces, material objects and task sequence. Those inputs support an access quotation without turning the equipment supplier into the roof designer.

## What remains above and what must be reached below?

For a retained roof, identify the underside work at purlins, perimeter junctions, rooflights and services. Record which items remain in place and which have a separately approved alteration scope. Do not assume that every apparent gap is an available platform position.

For roof renewal, identify the accepted new assembly and panel supply route. A forming lift belongs in the comparison only when a compatible former is to produce metal panels near roof level. It is separate from personnel access, insulation handling and roof installation. A project buying delivered roof panels has no automatic need to buy forming equipment.

The roof-edge photograph illustrates the physical distinction: metal sheeting and an open framing strip occupy different areas. An insulation quotation must identify the layers and interfaces that its actual design retains or replaces.

## Compare two complete retrofit routes

Use the same accepted building improvement as the commercial endpoint. If the designer accepts only one route, price that route and its access alternatives instead of inventing an equivalent assembly.

| Decision input | Interior retrofit package | Exterior renewal package | Owner, datum and decision use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Existing roof | Retained layers and condition acceptance: ___ | Removed and retained layers: ___ | Envelope designer; issues the build-up and fixes eligible work scope |
| Task geometry | Purlin, liner and junction work positions: ___ | New roof and insulation installation positions: ___ | Surveyor; references levels to marked building datum and local supporting floor |
| Interior occupation | Cleared bays, remaining machinery and release windows: ___ | Any internal protection and interruption still required: ___ | Facility manager; determines the usable production programme |
| Components | Insulation packs, liner or facing objects and accessories: ___ | Insulation objects, roof panels and other components: ___ | System supplier; supplies dimensions and masses for handling proposals |
| Access arrangement | Floor route and candidate platform positions: ___ | Roof access and external supply positions: ___ | Site team and equipment provider; define complete access scope for review |
| Weathering work | Retained roof repairs included: ___ | Removal, new weathering and temporary provisions included: ___ | Roofing contractor; sets the finished-roof responsibility |
| Completion | Installation, inspections, removal and bay release: ___ | Roof completion, inspections and removal: ___ | Programme manager; gives both quotations an explicit endpoint |

The task geometry row needs more than ridge height. A junction above a deep member and a clear purlin bay are different work positions. Show them on the same section with the existing services. The platform supplier's proposed arrangement then goes to the site engineer with the issued support reactions and floor information.

## The interior route purchases temporary use of the factory

An owner who wants to preserve the existing roof still has to release working space below it. Include equipment entry, material staging, platform setup, approved protection of retained assets, inspections and final removal. Price these activities even when the building remains generally operational.

Use bay releases that the facility can actually deliver. If production machinery or stock prevents a broad deck from serving the required faces, compare a different documented access method or an exterior proposal accepted by the designer. Do not solve an unavailable floor with an unsupported claim about platform outreach.

The ground-route photograph shows a construction-stage example with wet patches and stones. An operating factory presents different obstacles, but the purchase principle is the same: survey the actual delivery and setup route rather than quoting from the roof drawing alone.

## Two hypothetical savings that disappear

**Failure case: an insulation-only quote omits the interior release.** A contractor compares its material and platform cost with a complete exterior renewal price. Clearing machinery, protecting retained assets and returning each bay are absent from its column. Obtain the facility's actual release scope and price those activities before selecting the interior route.

**Failure case: a roof-forming lift is ordered for the underside task.** Another buyer reads the building height and requests a machine intended to raise a roof-panel former. Its contract actually requires a crew to fit an interior insulation assembly. Shortlist a ceiling/wall work platform for the personnel-access duty; add forming equipment only for a separately defined panel-production requirement.

2. Condition findings and the specified retained or renewed components.
3. Sections locating underside tasks and roof interfaces from named datums.
4. Insulation, liner and accessory dimensions, masses and delivery packs.
5. Service locations, machinery and facility-approved work-zone releases.
6. Floor records, entry route and proposed platform setup positions.
7. Panel/former data and receiving layout if elevated production is included.
8. Programme, inspections, destination, transport and final reinstatement scope.
