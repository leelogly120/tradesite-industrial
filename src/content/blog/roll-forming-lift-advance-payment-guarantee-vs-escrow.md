Choose an advance-payment guarantee when the supplier needs usable funds to build the identified forming-lift package and the accepted bank undertaking protects the agreed outstanding advance. Choose escrow when the supplier can fund the work while money is held, or the agreed releases fund each required stage. A cheaper arrangement has no value if it leaves the build unfunded or the advance outside the required protection. Before paying, obtain the actual bank or escrow terms and a dated manufacturing-funds plan. When both routes meet the same protection and funding requirements, compare their complete cost through the agreed endpoint.

## Follow the advance from the buyer to the build

Fix the equipment order before comparing security. Identify the former, lifting assembly, tooling and auxiliaries. Keep a locally purchased chassis or separately contracted integration outside the overseas seller's advance unless that seller actually supplies it. The advance must belong to a named contract and legal payee.

With an advance-payment guarantee, the buyer pays the supplier and holds a separate bank undertaking for payment under its stated demand conditions. With escrow, an independent agent holds funds under an agreed release arrangement. The useful first comparison is where the cash sits and when the supplier can use it.

A wrapped machine still needs an itemized scope. For the actual order, connect the assembly, tooling and accessories in the packing schedule to the advance-funded manufacturing work.

## A bank undertaking must leave usable manufacturing funds

[ICBC's advance-payment guarantee description](https://www.icbc.com.cn/ICBC/EN/CorporateFinance/GlobalService/FXGuarantee/AdvancePaymentGuarantee/) connects the instrument with an exporter's advance for goods or materials. Its application conditions include an approved credit line and a bank-required margin. Obtain the actual financing requirements with the proposed undertaking.

Put materials, bought-in components and contracted manufacturing services on their required payment dates. The supplier's finance lead should identify the resources available on those dates after meeting its bank's margin and other funding requirements. Compare the resulting usable funds with the work required before the next receipt. Accept the bank-backed route only when that actual plan funds the ordered build.

[HSBC explains an advance-payment guarantee and a performance bond](https://www.business.hsbc.uk/en-gb/products/international-business-guarantees). The protected obligation, beneficiary, amount and duration matter more than the heading. Require the bank's actual wording to address the agreed advance exposure. Keep any separately required performance or quality obligation identified in its own contract scope.

The [ICC demand-guarantee form](https://library.iccwbo.org/content/tfb/pdf/Model%20forms%20758.pdf) provides fields for the parties, underlying relationship, amount, presentation, expiry and charges. Its optional clauses address when an advance-related undertaking becomes available and how its amount changes. Reconcile the effective event, reduction record and expiry with the amount the buyer would still need to recover. An instrument issued before payment can specify a later effectiveness event; link that event to the actual remittance.

## Escrow has to support the production start

[Citi's business-escrow explanation](https://www.citi.com/banking/small-business-guide/banking/what-is-a-business-escrow-account) describes a neutral party holding funds until agreed conditions are met. Its account agreement covers release, duties, fees and dispute handling. Obtain a provider's accepted arrangement for the actual parties, currencies and transaction before treating escrow as an available purchase route.

If the supplier finances manufacture itself, retaining the buyer's money until the agreed release can be workable. If the supplier needs that money to procure parts, a completion-only release creates an unfunded build. Price an expressly agreed earlier-release arrangement or another funded security structure.

Record each release separately from the balance still held. Once money is paid out, exclude it from the funds still protected by escrow custody. If the buyer requires advance-refund protection after that release, include the expressly agreed separate cover for that amount. Retain cancellation and dispute instructions beside the normal release conditions.

Revise the advance or supply terms when neither route meets both the agreed protection requirement and the manufacturing-funds plan. Where both qualify, compare quoted issuance, amendment, account and release charges, together with the cost of committed funds over the same period.

## Keep protection aligned when the package changes

The loose shafts and sleeves below show why tooling needs its own identity within the purchase. An advance for a complete line can cover several assemblies with different supply responsibilities. Carry their identities into the order before assigning financial reductions to delivered scope.

![Upright roller shafts, blue bearing housings and loose sleeves stand beside grey machine frames in a workshop.](/images/equipment/buyer-20261101-roller-components.webp)
*Link each included tool and auxiliary to the order's agreed advance-reduction record.*

Have the commercial owner reconcile any changed amount, equipment scope or programme with the bank or escrow provider before accepting the financial amendment. A revised machine quotation does not itself revise a separate undertaking or account agreement.

Where [URDG 758](https://library.iccwbo.org/content/tfb/RULES/tfb-urdg758-rules.htm?AGENT=ICC_QAT+title%3D) is incorporated, its undertaking operates independently of the supply contract and deals with documents. A recovery demand must meet the applicable terms. Keep equipment acceptance with the technical contract and its authorized parties.

## Two hypothetical mistakes before an advance

**Mistake (hypothetical): escrow blocks the money required to start.** An importer funds escrow with release only after factory completion. The supplier's purchase plan requires that money for tooling and components. Reconcile the usable funds and required dates before payment. Choose a funded release arrangement, a workable bank-backed advance or revised order terms.

**Mistake (hypothetical): the bond title replaces a coverage review.** Another buyer accepts a performance-bond document and assumes its entire advance is covered. The document ends at factory dispatch, while the agreed order leaves part of the advance unapplied until destination integration. Match the protected obligation and remaining balance to the bank's terms before paying and whenever the order changes.
