Choose aluminium for a coastal industrial roof when the specified alloy, finish and complete assembly have written acceptance for the actual exposure and the production route can deliver them. Choose coated steel when its identified coating system and accessories satisfy the same project brief. Start with an exposure-based roof specification, then price the complete accepted material package and its manufacture. If only one offered material system has project-specific exposure acceptance, retain that eligible specification while resolving the other offer rather than awarding an unevaluated cheaper metal.

There is no useful universal rule that every coastal roof needs the same metal. A warehouse beside exposed surf, a sheltered harbour building and a roof affected by industrial emissions present different procurement inputs. Obtain a site-specific material selection from the responsible roof-product parties before ordering coil.

## Locate the exposure, including sheltered surfaces

Record the site location, marine setting, surrounding industrial sources and surfaces sheltered from normal rain washing. Give the material provider the building orientation, roof geometry and planned maintenance access. Avoid reducing the brief to a guessed straight-line distance from the sea.

[BlueScope's steel-roofing selection bulletin](https://cdn.dcs.bluescope.com.au/download/tb-01a) identifies location and local environmental conditions as material-selection factors. Its product recommendations concern its own named steel products and stated context. Use the principle of exposure-specific selection without adopting an Australian distance table as a global rule.

## Aluminium is a defined material system

Specify alloy, temper, thickness basis and finish with the roof designer and material provider. Include the selected fasteners, clips, flashings, gutters and other interfaces. The accepted roof system determines the material and connection requirements rather than a general preference for lighter-looking sheets.

[Kalzip's roof-system FAQ](https://www.kalzip.com/faq/) discusses corrosion evidence for identified Alclad roofs. Treat that evidence as belonging to the documented material and roof combination. For the proposed coastal roof, obtain acceptance for its exact nominated alloy, finish and assembly before selecting the production material.

For a contractor making its own sheets, qualify the actual material and profile on the offered forming configuration. Include the required surface condition, cutting and completed geometry in acceptance. A line that has produced a steel panel of a similar shape has not thereby completed the aluminium product review.

Buy finished aluminium panels when the project's product requirements exceed the contractor's demonstrated manufacturing scope or the isolated order does not justify qualification. The roof contract can still include those panels without the contractor owning their production equipment.

## Coated steel is more than one grade of painted coil

Name the substrate, metallic coating and paint system in the material schedule. Obtain the material provider's project recommendation for the site and the roof product provider's accepted assembly. Preserve both references when comparing quotations. Replacing a named severe-exposure product with ordinary painted steel changes the offer even if the colours match.

Coordinate accessories at the same time. Direct contact and water flowing from one material onto another need the relevant providers' accepted compatibility details. Include exposed fasteners and concealed clips, not just visible flashings. Assign the roof detailer responsibility for the complete material schedule.

Avoid using a warranty headline as the entire selection basis. Record the written product scope, covered application, maintenance duties and exclusions relevant to the actual proposal. Price the maintenance access that the owner can deliver rather than an assumed cleaning regime that has no budget or route.

Keep offered material thickness conventions explicit. The buyer should not compare figures referring to different measurement bases. Request the product provider's specification and use that same basis in coil purchasing and the machinery enquiry.

## Compare delivered roofs and retained production capability

For bought panels, include the accepted material, finish, lengths, packing, freight and replacement-piece route. For own production, add qualifying material, tooling, handling, inspection, start-up losses and the resources needed to protect the surface. Use actual accepted panel quantities rather than nominal coil width to compare delivered output.

**Mistake (hypothetical): ordinary steel replaces the accepted exposure product.** A distributor supplies ordinary painted coil for a harbour warehouse because its colour matches the accepted exposure product, while the coating specification differs. The roof no longer matches the material recommendation. Restore the accepted material or obtain a fully revised product decision before manufacturing.

**Mistake (hypothetical): aluminium panels inherit steel accessories.** A contractor changes the main sheets while retaining an unreviewed accessory order. Reissue the complete material schedule, obtain the providers' accepted contact and runoff details, and price the actual revised package before awarding it.
