**Choose siphonic roof drainage when a qualified drainage designer can deliver the required roof duty and a coordinated, supported collector route offers a material advantage inside the hall. Choose conventional gravity drainage when its graded routes and downpipes already fit the building and offer a better complete installation and maintenance price.** Compare accepted designs for the same roof, discharge points and overflow strategy before ordering a platform.

One proposal can protect valuable production space by moving downpipes. Another can follow a short gravity route to convenient connections at the building edge. Pipe length alone captures neither benefit. Obtain a plan that follows water from each roof outlet to the accepted downstream connection, and a separate sheet showing where people will stand to install and service the system.

The wide hall photograph shows an elevated working deck, floor space and roof framing. Imagine the same view after cranes, stock and partitions arrive. A clear approach for fitting a collector during construction can disappear before the first maintenance visit. Mark the workface and the future approach on the same building section.

## What changes inside the building?

[Geberit's description of Pluvia siphonic roof drainage](https://www.geberit.co.uk/sanitary-piping-systems/water-drainage-systems/geberit-pluvia/) explains how its system uses roof outlets and a planned pipe network and can run horizontal collector pipes without a conventional gradient. Those characteristics belong to an engineered system. The project designer supplies the hydraulic calculations, compatible outlets, required support method, operating conditions and transition to conventional drainage. A contractor cannot obtain a siphonic design by drawing an ordinary collector level.

Ask the conventional designer to show the fall needed along each branch and the elevation where it crosses the most congested bay. Check the effect on loading doors, production equipment and other services with the building coordinator. Where existing gravity connections already serve the outlets efficiently, a new specialist system has to justify its extra design, supply and commissioning scope. Where proposed downpipes occupy a process line, price the real cost of relocating or protecting them.

Keep emergency or secondary drainage within the responsible designer's brief for both alternatives. Obtain the roof designer's outlet detail and the receiving drainage authority's accepted connection conditions. A roof outlet inspection from above and a collector support inspection from below require different equipment, permissions and work windows.

## Put a price on the last inspection visit

On the installation estimate, separate outlet placement, waterproofing, collector support, pipe assembly, specialist checks and project handover. Agree who supplies lifting equipment for long pipework. The personnel platform is priced for the people, tools and permitted task materials in the offered arrangement. A broad deck does not give the contractor a separate pipe-hoisting service.

On the maintenance estimate, start with the access required by the proposed system provider and the facility plan. Identify which tasks occur at roof level and which require a return beneath the collector. For each interior task, record the room or bay, closure permission, ground support and route through the completed building. Compare the cost of repeat visits over the same owner-selected period without assigning unsubstantiated maintenance intervals to either system.

## Two drainage-estimate mistakes

**Mistake one — crediting floor space twice.** A siphonic proposal removes a downpipe from one bay but introduces its transition and a protected drop in the next. The buyer credits all old positions as available production space. Compare the net usable floor areas on the coordinated plans and value only the released area the owner can use.

**Mistake two — buying one access visit for both sides of the roof.** An interior deck is booked for pipe supports, while the roof outlet and waterproofing interface require separate authorised access above. Assign the roof package to its trade, name the inspection point and include both visits in the handover programme.
