Choose a polycarbonate-sheet extrusion business when supported customers require that defined translucent product and the complete polymer-processing, quality and delivery operation is funded. Choose metal roll forming when the supported orders specify compatible metal sheets and the complete coil-to-panel business earns its cost. Buy occasional complementary rooflights from a specialist rather than treating one production line as a route to both materials.

These businesses sell different products and require different manufacturing proposals. A roof with both opaque sheets and daylighting zones can support a coordinated supply offer, while its two product families still come from separate production processes.

## Start with the customer product, not a similar roof profile

“Polycarbonate roofing” is too broad for a plant quotation. Name the intended sheet construction, geometry, dimensions, surface system, colour or light-transmission requirement and the application for which the product will be supplied. The customer's accepted roof assembly and the product provider's evidence determine that application.

For metal sheets, identify the grade, finish, controlled profile, length range and specified fastening or seam interfaces. Keep product appearance separate from the assembly evidence. Similar corrugations across two materials do not establish the same structural, movement, fire, weathering or installation performance.

[Brett Martin describes thermoplastic extrusion among its manufacturing competencies](https://www.brettmartin.com/our-operations), with flat, corrugated and structured plastic sheets among the products it makes. Its [Marlon FSX page](https://www.brettmartin.com/plastic-sheets/our-products/product/marlon-fsx) identifies an extruded solid polycarbonate sheet used in applications including rooflights. Those examples establish an actual plastics-manufacturing category. The buyer still needs a proposal for the particular product and its intended markets.

A distributor should first decide whether it wants to manufacture that product or simply supply it. A successful combined roofing order can be filled with owned metal production and specialist rooflights, or with purchased metal sheets and owned polymer production. Owning both plants is a separate investment case.

## Specify a whole operating plant for each route

For polymer manufacture, request an integrated proposal from a specialist process provider covering the defined feedstock, preparation, extrusion and downstream operations through inspected, protected and packed sheets. Have the provider identify the utilities, environmental provisions, staffing, process control and product-development work required by its configuration.

For metal manufacture, price coil handling, forming tooling, cutting, output support, inspection and dispatch for the defined sheet. Add the actual building, utilities, staff and working capital. A bare machine quotation and a complete installed plant offer are not comparable investment totals.

The acceptance plan must connect the plant to the product sold. Establish which samples, measurements and evidence release output for the intended customer application. The product specialist owns those criteria; the production provider identifies how its proposed process supplies the required output and records.

Do not use a metal former's rated speed as a proxy for either business's saleable throughput. Budget from accepted output of the actual product mix, including changeovers, quality checks, packing and rejects. Use quoted trial and operating evidence rather than an assumed conversion between the two processes.

## A product-to-plant investment worksheet

Fill this out for the first product family in each proposed business. Attach actual customer requirements and provider quotations behind the entries.

| Investment field | Buyer entry to complete | Responsible owner | Decision use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| First sold product | Enter sheet construction, geometry, material and finish ___ | Product lead | Defines what the plant must make |
| Accepted applications | Record customer assemblies, market requirements and supporting evidence ___ | Customer designer and product specialist | Establishes the eligible sales scope |
| Supported demand | Enter products, quantities, order status and dates ___ | Sales lead | Separates committed work from a broad forecast |
| Process boundary | List feedstock preparation through protected packed output ___ | Specialist plant provider | Prices a complete manufacturing route |
| Factory resources | Enter space, utilities, staffing and environmental provisions ___ | Factory project team | Tests the operating location |
| Output release | Record samples, measurements and release records ___ | Quality lead | Defines saleable product acceptance |
| Cash and stock | Enter plant cost, raw material, finished stock and payment dates ___ | Finance lead | Tests funding through delivery |
| Complementary supply | Name bought products, suppliers, interfaces and quoted lead times ___ | Procurement lead | Tests partnership against owning a second plant |

Where the proposed roof product is new to the business, include development and customer acceptance before counting full commercial output. A plant trial and an accepted application are different milestones. The investment schedule should name the owner and funding for both.

## A mixed roof order does not require a mixed-material factory

The [rooflight-compatible panel guide](/blog/rooflight-compatible-roof-panel-roll-forming-package/) addresses coordination between metal sheets and rooflights. That remains necessary even when both are bought from established suppliers. Product interfaces, components and installation responsibilities belong in the accepted roof package.

Use external supply for the smaller or less predictable product family when its complete delivered price is preferable to a dedicated plant. The owned business can then concentrate on supported demand, stable production and customer service in its primary family.

If repeated complementary orders later justify another process, create a separate investment proposal. Identify the actual shared resources and the resources that remain independent. A common sales team and warehouse do not make polymer process knowledge, metal tooling or product release requirements interchangeable.

## Two hypothetical factory mistakes

**Failure case: a metal profile is treated as an extrusion specification.** A distributor wants translucent sheets matching a metal roof and asks only for the same rib outline. Its proposal omits the polymer product construction, surface requirements and application evidence. Define the complete translucent product with the specialist before purchasing its manufacturing process.

**Failure case: occasional rooflights justify a second plant.** Another metal-sheet producer counts a few combined roof orders as a continuing plastics workload. The factory proposal then adds development, staff and utilities beyond the forecast. Purchase the rooflights externally and revisit ownership when qualifying repeat demand supports the complete operation.
