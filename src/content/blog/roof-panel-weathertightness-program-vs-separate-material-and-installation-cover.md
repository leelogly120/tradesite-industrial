Choose the roof manufacturer's weathertightness program when the owner's contract requires that undertaking and the specified system, producer, installer and review stages qualify. Buy separate product and installation undertakings only where the owner accepts their written scope and the responsible parties can deliver the agreed remedy.

## Ask what event the owner wants covered

Start with the contract and proposed documents. A coating issue on a panel, a defect in the metal substrate and water entry at a roof detail are different events. Identify which party answers each claim, what it undertakes to provide, what is excluded and what the owner has to do. A long term in the heading can describe a narrow finish obligation. It does not define who investigates a leak at an interface with another trade. Have the owner and its advisers review the actual terms before treating two offers as equivalent.

MBCI publishes different warranty resources for its products and roofs. Its information illustrates why material, finish and roof-system obligations should be compared separately. Obtain the terms for the precise system, location, owner and project. Ask the program issuer which proposals, documents, installer qualifications and observations it requires, and when each has to be submitted. Include these requirements in the production schedule. Terms issued by one roof manufacturer apply to that issuer's program. [MBCI warranty resources](https://www.mbci.com/resource-center/resources/warranties/)

## Follow a disputed leak through both offers

Give the bidders an example that crosses an interface, such as water appearing near equipment installed after completion. Ask who receives the notice, what information is required, who investigates, which roof components and trades fall within its commitment and what work the owner must arrange separately. For an installation defect, identify the installer or issuer responsible under the offered terms. For a later trade's alteration, identify the exclusion or obligation that governs investigation and repair. Record that allocation in the commercial comparison without assuming an automatic outcome. A single point for initial notification can be valuable even when some remedial work belongs to a different contract.

Request two complete prices when the specification allows an alternative. In the manufacturer-program bid include authorized supply, installer requirements, review attendance, documents, fees and required closeout. In the separate-undertaking bid include the installer and manufacturer's actual commitments, an agreed coordination arrangement and any other acceptance or support the owner requires. Compare the same roof and contractual event, not simply the longest number of years printed on either document. Ask what happens if the manufacturer changes a detail or the owner adds rooftop plant later.

## Protect the forming investment from ineligible sales

Separate the orders the proposed line can serve under the named program from the ones it can serve only under separate undertakings. A physically matching profile does not itself make a producer an authorized source. If a program calls for named material or an approved location, give the machine supplier that requirement before tooling is ordered. Conversely, a customer accepting a separately documented roof can create legitimate work outside a particular program. Record its written acceptance instead of assuming that all local projects require the same purchase.

**Mistake: A finish document is priced as leakage cover.** A bidder attaches a long-term coating document to a roof tender that asks who will respond to water entry. The purchaser treats the lower bid as complete. Ask for a response against the specified leakage obligation and obtain owner acceptance of any alternative before awarding or calculating savings.

**Mistake: Machinery is bought for orders outside the program.** A fabricator sees a pipeline of specified roofs and orders matching profile tools. The program holder later identifies another authorized production source for those jobs. Exclude the affected sales from the line case and obtain a separate written authorization or pursue orders with an accepted alternate package.

Award the program route when it meets the written owner requirement and its delivery conditions fit the project. Choose separate undertakings only with a documented division of responsibility acceptable to the customer. Retain copies of the final documents, responsible contacts, inspection records and owner maintenance obligations with the handover. Give the forming-line purchase team the resulting eligible product list, rather than a sales forecast based on the entire market for metal roofs.

## Eight RFQ inputs for cover and production eligibility

1. Attach the owner's performance and contractual cover requirements.
2. Identify the exact system, panel, detail revision and project location.
3. Request the issuer's terms, remedies, exclusions and notice route.
4. Name the approved producer, forming location and installation party.
5. List required submittals, review attendance and closeout evidence.
6. Provide separate product, finish and installer undertaking documents.
7. State owner duties for maintenance, later works and records.
8. Request project prices and confirmed eligible future panel orders.
