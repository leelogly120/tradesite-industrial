**Buy a pneumatic ETFE cushion system when its designed envelope performance is required and the owner accepts the inflation equipment and long-term service responsibilities. Choose a mechanically tensioned single-layer foil system when the designer confirms that one layer meets the actual enclosure requirement and its supporting geometry suits the façade.** Price how installers reach the perimeter, how replacement foil is handled and who restores the finished system. The appearance of the foil alone tells a buyer little about those costs.

ETFE is a material. The commercial purchase is an installed envelope with a support structure, edge details and a defined service life strategy. A covered outdoor circulation space and a conditioned building can call for different thermal performance. Establish the boundary of the enclosed space before treating two translucent elevations as equivalent products.

## Understand what holds the foil in shape

[Architen Landrell's ETFE design guide](https://www.architen.com/articles/etfe-foil-a-guide-to-design/) describes both single-layer systems and inflated cushions. In a cushion assembly a dedicated air supply maintains the form of the foil elements. A single-layer solution uses a mechanically tensioned arrangement designed for its geometry. The envelope specialist specifies the actual supporting elements, edge restraints and connections. The building engineer accepts the forces transferred to the primary structure. Two attractive renderings can represent materially different support purchases.

Ask the design team for the project requirements for thermal performance, daylight, environmental exposure and other relevant building duties. Compare calculations and evidence for each proposed system in its proposed orientation. A designer's choice of a cushion to meet an insulation requirement cannot be replaced by one sheet merely because its support detail looks simpler. Conversely, an open canopy with an accepted one-layer design has no reason to carry a permanent inflation plant unless another project requirement justifies it.

For a cushion proposal, identify the plant location, power supply, distribution route and points the operator must inspect or maintain. The specialist supplies the actual monitoring, service and response plan. Ask the owner who receives a system alert, who has access to the plant and how the provider reaches an affected cushion. For the tensioned alternative, ask what the specialist inspects at perimeter restraints or supporting elements and how the element can be released and reinstated. Each system needs a credible replacement method.

## Follow one damaged field from discovery to handback

Select a representative high field at a corner or return. Have the specialist identify its boundaries on an elevation and describe the repair or replacement offer for that particular design. State which neighbouring fields, edge sections, plant or distribution equipment are affected and how the facility accepts the finished result. The buyer needs an allocation of responsibilities, material handling and access, rather than a do-it-yourself membrane procedure.

Use the same project period and service outcome for the financial comparison. Price the accepted envelope, its permanent infrastructure, commissioning, scheduled visits and the defined representative intervention. Include a contribution for owner-provided energy on the cushion option using the actual supplier data. Keep a speculative damage event separate from routine maintenance so the buyer can see what drives the result. A single unpriced repair allowance obscures the practical difference between a routine plant visit and replacement of a large foil field.

Request a scaled section showing the machine, operator position, membrane surface and separate replacement-handling path at the most difficult edge.

## Two procurement mistakes in a foil envelope comparison

**Mistake one — evaluating membrane price per area as the finished cost.** A hypothetical cushion bid excludes inflation supply, controls and service access. The apparently cheaper system leaves permanent infrastructure unfunded. Obtain a complete, accepted envelope package and identify all owner-provided items.

**Mistake two — reserving a platform beside the middle of the field.** The crew can reach the visible surface, but the replacement work is at a perimeter behind a projecting return. Have the specialist draw each required working side and secure an accepted approach to that edge before ordering equipment.
