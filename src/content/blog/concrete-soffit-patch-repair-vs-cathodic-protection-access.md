**Price a local patch-repair programme when the structural engineer's investigation identifies bounded concrete defects and accepts local repair as the required treatment. Price cathodic protection with its specified concrete repairs, commissioning and monitoring when the corrosion specialist calls for an area-wide protection system.** Choose personnel access against those accepted work packages. A photograph of a damaged soffit cannot decide which treatment the structure needs.

A car park owner sees loose material at a handful of parking bays. A concrete repair contractor sees a proposed quantity of overhead patches. Where the investigation identifies wider corrosion exposure, the engineer defines the structural zone requiring assessment. Treat the diagnosis, treatment decision and purchase of working access as different responsibilities. Buyers need enough preliminary investigation to obtain comparable offers before booking a deck for production work.

A car park, loading bay or plant undercroft can offer much less headroom and a less forgiving floor arrangement. Start with ceiling and beam sections, ramps, floor construction and each proposed work position before accepting any machine into the estimate.

## Commission the repair scope before asking for a rate

Request a condition survey and the engineer's explanation of the deterioration mechanism, limits of investigation and proposed acceptance criteria. Ask the engineer to distinguish unsound material requiring repair from adjacent areas requiring treatment, monitoring or no intervention. Where further investigation is required before a final decision, buy a defined survey stage and cost its access separately. Give an allowance for unforeseen damage a commercial basis, such as agreed measurement and rates for genuinely opened-up quantities.

The US Federal Highway Administration's [corrosion-control guidance for concrete structures](https://highways.fhwa.dot.gov/sites/fhwa.dot.gov/files/FHWA-HRT-24-127.pdf) discusses corrosion mitigation, including cathodic protection. Its context is highway structures. For a particular building, the owner's structural and corrosion specialists select and design the treatment. Cathodic protection addresses corrosion of reinforcing steel. The engineer must still specify the removal or repair of defective concrete and any necessary structural work.

A local repair quote should state how repaired areas will be measured, how overhead working areas will be released and who accepts the prepared and completed repair. A protection-system quote should define the treatment zone, specialist design, electrical and monitoring equipment specific to the proposed system, commissioning and future inspection. Distinguish components that require powered control from those that belong to another proposed design. Obtain an actual maintenance plan from the selected specialist.

## Put survey, production and return access on separate lines

Use the investigation to identify which bays can be visited in one release and which need separate traffic control or building closures. Ask the repair provider for task-specific handling, debris containment and exclusion arrangements. A crew platform provides personnel access within its allowed duty. It is a separate purchase from debris-catching arrangements, material hoisting and temporary support of structural elements.

Compare two proposals against the same required service outcome and owner-selected time horizon. If only one treatment is accepted by the engineer, compare delivery methods within that treatment. A cheaper patch-only bid has no useful price advantage over a specified protection system if it leaves the diagnosed mechanism untreated. Conversely, a broad treatment proposed without a building-specific basis needs technical justification before the owner funds permanent controls and monitoring.

## Two repair-estimate mistakes

**Mistake one — multiplying photographed patches by one access rate.** A hypothetical buyer counts visible damage from floor level and commits a fixed-price crew. The survey later identifies a larger treated zone with separate access and monitoring requirements. Obtain the accepted condition scope and a mechanism for measured changes before awarding the work.

**Mistake two — buying protection installation without future access.** Monitoring connections appear on the specialist drawing but sit above a permanently allocated storage bay. Allocate a service route and access window during design. Include commissioning and repeat checks that the actual proposal requires.
