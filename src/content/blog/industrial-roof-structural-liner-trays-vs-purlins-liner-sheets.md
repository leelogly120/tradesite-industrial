Buy structural liner trays when the engineer selects their load-bearing arrangement and the complete roof build-up suits the building. Buy purlins with specified liner sheets when the accepted secondary-steel design uses that architecture. Compare structure, roof layers, installation and building interfaces together. Release the weather-sheet profile only after the supporting roof arrangement is fixed. If the main-frame and purlin design is already released, price a tray substitution as a complete revised structural roof package before removing the purlins from the order.

The inner metal face is not one interchangeable commodity. A structural tray has an assigned supporting role. A liner sheet in a purlin-supported assembly has the role given to it by that system's designer. Their visual similarity does not justify substituting one into the other's quotation.

## Read the section from the frame outward

Obtain a roof section showing the main frame, any secondary supports, inner face, other layers and external covering. Name which product spans between which supports. Ask the structural designer to identify the design basis and connections for each eligible alternative.

[Tata Steel's structural liner-tray page](https://www.tatasteeluk.com/construction/products/roof/profiles/roofdek-liner-tray) provides identified tray products with load/span information and design resources. Its [RoofDek manual](https://www.tatasteeluk.com/sites/default/files/RoofDek%20manual.pdf) includes a liner-tray arrangement at the main frame and separate deck-on-purlin details. These examples establish distinct roof architectures; their published values belong to those specified products.

The sheet at the machine outlet below demonstrates why the production drawing needs a function as well as a section. Specify the actual weather sheet for an ordinary roof-panel forming enquiry. Procure structural trays or secondary steel under their own defined product and engineering scopes.

## Tray route: price an engineered supporting product

Obtain the structural designer's accepted tray layout and connections alongside the roof designer's issued layers, openings and weathering interfaces. Include the interfaces at ridges, eaves, walls and structural movement joints. Name the provider responsible for the tray product information and the designer accepting its use in the building.

A proposed tray roof needs its own delivery and installation plan. Record finished component lengths, packing groups, lifting arrangements and installation access. Obtain the contractor's actual sequence and temporary construction requirements from its competent designers. Include every remaining support, connection and installation item in the accepted tray arrangement before crediting the omitted purlins.

Compare this route where its accepted arrangement serves the building and its complete supply and installation proposal is deliverable. Keep optional acoustic or interior-finish configurations identified separately. A different perforation, finish or roof layer is a product choice that belongs in the approved design.

## Purlin route: retain every layer in the estimate

For purlins and liner sheets, obtain the secondary-steel arrangement and its connections to the main frame. Identify the liner specification, supports, fasteners, joints and assigned duties in the complete roof. Include spacers or other components actually required by the selected assembly.

This route is a strong choice where the released structure and roof package already suit the building and the installer can deliver it efficiently. A competing tray alternative needs a genuine whole-building advantage, rather than a comparison with only the purlin material cost.

Price delivery and installation of the secondary steel as well as the liner and outer sheets. Coordinate service supports and openings with the responsible designers. Show where separate services connect to the building rather than assuming any inner metal face is a general-purpose fixing surface.

Enter dimensions from the designer's stated support datums. Do not treat a clear building span, grid spacing and tray bearing dimension as the same measurement. Give the supplier the issued drawing rather than a single isolated span figure.

## Sequence the inner structure and outer-sheet work

The selected inner construction determines which parts must be installed before later roof layers. Put material delivery, installation access, inspection and release of the outer roof into the programme. Identify any temporary support or protection scope assigned by the construction design, and price it under the correct provider.

The low-position forming arrangement below serves a different duty from the installed roof structure. A forming lift positions a compatible panel former. It is not a proposal to support the building's roof. Keep the production and structural packages separately identifiable even if they share a site window.

*Coordinate sheet production with the roof erection programme while retaining the distinct equipment and building duties.*

**Mistake (hypothetical): a structural tray is replaced by a similar liner.** A purchaser replaces the specified main-frame-spanning tray with a shallow liner offer selected from rib depth and appearance, without a revised supporting design. The product lacks the supporting role specified by the engineer. Restore the named tray or obtain a complete designer-issued alternative before changing the order.

**Mistake (hypothetical): only the omitted purlins are counted.** A tray bid appears cheaper because the buyer subtracts secondary steel but omits revised connections, handling and erection work. Complete the alternative's component and construction schedule, then compare both installed roofs.
