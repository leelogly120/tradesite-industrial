**Choose an OEM-approved platform-rack package when its documented combination accommodates the actual stock, people and tools throughout the installation. Buy separate material lifting when the object, height or lifting/holding duty falls outside that carrying arrangement. Shortlist ARC-F25 for the personnel workface when its offered configuration fits the located tasks and site. Mark where each object changes support. When both packages meet the same finished-installation requirement and dates, choose the lower complete cost.**

A pipe rack and a material lift buy different functions. Stock carried beside a crew is not automatically a load hoisted from the floor, held at a fixing point or supported during assembly. The commercial mistake is to leave one of those functions between two quotations.

## Follow one real pipe length to its fixing position

Record its delivered condition, individual length and mass, destination and required presentation. Distinguish receiving a bundle, separating stock, carrying a length with the crew, presenting it to the work and supporting the assembly. Allocate each retained duty to a named resource; keep the installation method with the responsible contractor.

A rack offer needs the exact platform and attachment combination. [Skyjack's heavy-duty pipe-rack literature](https://www.skyjack.com/sites/default/files/literature-library/NA_ENG_RT_HEAVY_DUTY_PIPE_RACK.pdf) lists eligible models and distinguishes rack load from total platform load including the rack load. That is a useful purchasing boundary: an attachment headline is not extra capacity added to a personnel platform. Its figures belong to the listed Skyjack configurations, not ARC-F25.

The deck-level photograph makes this a layout question as well as a total-mass question. Crew positions, tools and stock compete for actual working space. A long object also has a swept envelope beyond the patch of deck occupied by its supports.

## Purchase a carrying configuration or a separate lifting resource

Use the rack route where the accepted configuration supplies the required carrying/support duty without leaving another machine to be hired later. Ask for the permitted object envelope, combined loading conditions and applicable machine documents as one configuration response. A generic bracket quotation does not identify the completed machine. Admit the rack route only when both its own permitted load/object conditions and the complete platform loading conditions cover the proposed task state.

For independent lifting, buy the actual load-handling function and reach. [Genie's SLC-24 material-lift page](https://www.genielift.com/en-gb/aerial-lift-products/push-around-material-lifts/slc-24) describes industrial and HVAC material handling, offers different attachments and publishes a finite lift height. A material-lift model is not suitable merely because the personnel platform reaches the ceiling. Match the object's required support position to that exact handling machine's documented scope. State whether the purchase ends at stock presentation or includes holding during installation; do not leave that duty to an assumed function of the personnel deck.

Two resources also require two physical places. Draw the material machine, its supporting footprint, stock receiving area and personnel-platform position together. Identify who supplies the handling equipment, its people and the coordination needed at the common workface. If the joint arrangement blocks the platform's required approach, revise the package before comparing purchase amounts.

## Compare completed installation, including the second footprint

The rack price belongs beside its actual carrying restrictions, deck configuration and the remaining stock-to-workface resources. The separate-handler price includes delivery, setup resources, people, occupation, removal and any personnel-access changes caused by its footprint. Use the same completed lengths, zones and acceptance endpoint for both.

Keep access and lifting eligibility ahead of cost. A cheap handler that stops below the actual presentation point does not complete the package. Neither does an attractive personnel deck with an attachment that its manufacturer has not accepted. Once both routes serve the same work and programme, compare their complete cost through installation handback.

![Raised red broad-deck equipment inside a tall hall above rough ground, with a large cylindrical structure nearby.](/images/equipment/buyer-20261029-deck-hall.webp)
*Reserve actual support and approach positions alongside other installed objects, using the site engineer's ground assessment.*

The layout must survive construction progress. Record the last position from which equipment leaves after pipework, partitions and stored stock occupy the hall. If the last bays leave room for the personnel platform but not the independent handler, price the separately accepted arrangement that completes those bays. Conversely, if their stock lies outside the rack's permitted object envelope, retain the material resource for those positions instead of claiming one rack purchase completes every bay.

## Two purchase mistakes to avoid

**Mistake (hypothetical): the rack allowance is added to deck capacity.** A buyer totals the crew and tools against the personnel-platform headline, then treats the rack headline as a separate extra allowance. The quoted combined configuration no longer matches that load schedule. Correct the comparison with one simultaneous load plan and the manufacturer's applicable combined limits before accepting the rack route.

**Mistake (hypothetical): the material lift is priced without a usable workface.** A contractor orders a compact material lift for a high services run because its load figure covers the pipe. Its actual reach does not reach the required holding point, and its footprint prevents the deck approaching the fixing region. Replace the incomplete offer with a documented handling duty and a joint-position drawing; retain both costs in the decision.

## Eight RFQ attachments for the personnel-platform offer

1. Issued ceiling-services plan and sections showing actual personnel fixing positions, offsets and supporting-floor datums.
2. Pipe/conduit schedule with product, individual dimensions and mass, delivered packaging and required presentation positions.
3. Simultaneous crew, tools and materials schedule, with their proposed positions on the offered personnel deck.
4. Exact rack/platform combination and applicable configuration documents for any proposed carrying route.
5. Separate material-provider offer stating lifting/holding duty, equipment/attachment identity, reach and retained resources.
6. Combined work-zone drawing showing both equipment footprints, stock receiving, entrances, turns and clear corridors.
7. Supporting-floor or ground records and the engineer receiving the actual offered support reactions.
8. Destination, site power, work calendar and transport boundary, requesting the offered deck layout, simultaneous task/load limits, support reactions and acceptance records.
