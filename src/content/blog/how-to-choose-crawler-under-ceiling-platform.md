## Introduction

Choosing the right aerial platform for ceiling work can mean the difference between a project completed in weeks versus one that drags on for months. Whether you are installing HVAC systems in a new airport terminal, mounting lighting rigs in a sports stadium, or replacing ceiling panels in a warehouse, the access equipment you select determines your crew's daily productivity, your project's safety record, and ultimately your bottom line.

For decades, the construction industry relied on two options: **scaffolding** (slow to erect, immovable once built) or **spider lifts** (compact but limited in platform size and unable to work while moving). Today, a third option has emerged — the **crawler under-ceiling platform** with pick & carry capability — that fundamentally changes the productivity equation for large-scale ceiling projects.

But with four models ranging from 20m to 35m working height, how do you select the right one? This guide walks you through the key decision factors — working height, load capacity, site conditions, and power requirements — to help you match the right machine to your specific project.

## Understanding Crawler Under-ceiling Platforms

### What Makes Them Different

A crawler under-ceiling platform is a self-propelled aerial work platform mounted on a tracked crawler chassis. Unlike spider lifts that must deploy outriggers before every lift and retract them before every move, a crawler platform with **pick & carry** capability can drive while the platform is fully elevated and loaded with workers and materials.

This single capability — continuous movement at working height — is what separates crawler platforms from every other access method for ceiling work. Where a spider lift crew completes 8-10 positions per shift (each with 30-45 minutes of outrigger setup), a pick & carry crew covers the same area in a single uninterrupted pass.

### Key Advantage: Pick & Carry Workflow

The pick & carry workflow delivers three measurable benefits:

1. **Time savings** — 30-40% faster completion on typical ceiling installation projects compared to scaffolding, and 20-25% faster than spider lifts
2. **Labor efficiency** — the large platform (12m×8m = 96m²) keeps 6-10 workers productive simultaneously, versus 2-3 workers on a spider lift basket
3. **Continuous workflow** — no stop-start cycle of setup, work, teardown, reposition, repeat

### Typical Applications

Crawler under-ceiling platforms excel in any large-venue environment where:
- Ceiling heights exceed 15m
- Work areas span thousands of square meters
- Schedule pressure demands maximum daily productivity
- Floor surfaces must be protected from concentrated outrigger loads
- Zero-emission operation is required in enclosed spaces

Common project types include airport terminals, sports stadiums, convention centers, exhibition halls, manufacturing facilities, and aircraft hangars.

## Key Selection Criteria

### 3.1 Working Height Requirements

The first and most critical decision factor is your project's ceiling height. Measure from the finished floor to the lowest ceiling element you need to access — and add 1.5-2m for comfortable working clearance above the platform.

**Important consideration:** The working height is the platform height plus approximately 2m for a worker's reach above the platform railing. So a 25m platform height gives you practical access to ceilings at approximately 27m.

### 3.2 Load Capacity Needs

Calculate your required platform load by adding:
- **Workers** — average 80kg per person (with PPE and tools)
- **Materials** — ceiling panels, ductwork sections, lighting fixtures
- **Equipment** — power tools, welding sets, measurement instruments

**Standard models (FDDPT-20M, 25M, 31M):**
- Safe Working Load: 0.3-0.5 tonnes (300-500 kg)
- Maximum workers: 6-10 (depending on tools and materials on platform)
- Best for: ceiling panel installation, lighting, HVAC, fire protection systems

**Heavy-duty model (FDDPT-35M):**
- Safe Working Load: 1.2-3.0 tonnes (1,200-3,000 kg)
- Maximum workers: 6-10 plus heavy equipment
- Best for: heavy mechanical installation, large lighting arrays, structural steel connection, equipment maintenance

**When to choose heavy-duty:** If your ceiling work involves individual components weighing more than 200kg, or if you need to position heavy equipment (generators, large HVAC units, industrial fans) at ceiling height, the FDDPT-35M is the only model that handles these loads on the platform.

### 3.3 Site Access and Ground Conditions

Evaluate your project site for these factors:

**Floor surface type:**
- **Polished concrete / tile / marble** — all FDDPT models feature wide crawler tracks that distribute weight across a large contact area, producing ground pressure compatible with commercial flooring. Calculate specific ground pressure: machine weight × 9.81 ÷ track contact area.
- **Raised access flooring** — verify the raised floor's design load rating against the machine's ground pressure
- **Unfinished concrete / compacted earth** — crawler chassis handles rough terrain with 30% gradeability

**Access dimensions:**
- All FDDPT models share the same 2.95m overall width — verify doorway and corridor clearances
- Overall height with rails folded: 2.7m (20M) to 3.17m (35M) — check overhead clearances on the route to the work area
- Minimum turning radius: 16m — ensure adequate maneuvering space

**Indoor vs. outdoor operation:**
- All models offer dual power (diesel + electric) for seamless indoor/outdoor use
- Verify that electric power delivery at the site can support the twin electric motors
- For outdoor-only construction sites, diesel-only operation is available on all models

### 3.4 Power System Requirements

| Power Mode | When to Use | Available On |
|-----------|-------------|-------------|
| Diesel only | Outdoor construction, rough terrain, heavy lifting | All models (150hp / 160hp on 35M) |
| Electric only | Indoor facilities, zero-emission requirements, noise-sensitive areas | All models (twin electric motors) |
| Dual power | Projects spanning both outdoor and indoor phases | All models (standard) |

**Key question:** Will any portion of your project require work inside an occupied or enclosed building? If yes, dual power is essential — and it comes standard on every FDDPT model.

**Challenge:** A major international airport needed to replace 12,000m² of ceiling panels at 28m height in an active terminal. Work could only occur during overnight closures (11pm-5am), and the terminal could not be disrupted by scaffolding barriers during operating hours.

**Solution:** Two [FDDPT-31M](/products/fddpt-31m-crawler-ceiling-platform/) units operating in electric mode from 11pm to 5am each night. The pick & carry workflow allowed continuous ceiling panel removal and installation along 200m departure halls without overnight scaffolding erection and morning teardown.

**Result:** Project completed in 45 nights versus the 72 nights originally estimated with scaffolding. Zero passenger disruption during terminal operating hours. 38% schedule savings translated into approximately $200,000 in avoided liquidated damages.

### Example 2: Sports Stadium Construction — FDDPT-35M

**Challenge:** A new 65,000-seat stadium required installation of 340 LED lighting fixtures (each weighing 180kg) at 33m ceiling height, plus 2,400m² of acoustic ceiling panels. The compressed construction schedule allowed only 6 weeks for ceiling work.

**Solution:** One [FDDPT-35M](/products/fddpt-35m-crawler-ceiling-platform/) heavy-duty model, leveraging its 3T platform load capacity to carry multiple lighting fixtures per trip plus installation crews. The 8T integrated crane lifted heavy mechanical equipment to ceiling height without a separate tower crane.

**Result:** Ceiling installation completed in 5 weeks — one week ahead of schedule. The FDDPT-35M's heavy-load capacity eliminated 200+ individual crane lifts that would have been required with a lighter platform, saving approximately 1,500 crane-hours and $180,000 in crane rental costs.

### Example 3: Warehouse Lighting Upgrade — FDDPT-20M

**Challenge:** A logistics company needed to upgrade 8,000m² of warehouse lighting at 18m ceiling height, replacing metal halide fixtures with LED panels. Operations had to continue in adjacent warehouse sections during the work.

**Solution:** One [FDDPT-20M](/products/fddpt-20m-crawler-ceiling-platform/) operating during day shifts in electric mode. The compact 20m model navigated between storage racks while the 12m×8m platform spanned multiple lighting rows simultaneously.

**Result:** Lighting upgrade completed in 12 working days versus the 20 days estimated with spider lifts. Minimal floor disruption — no outrigger pads on the warehouse floor, no scaffolding blocking forklift traffic. The logistics company estimated $45,000 in avoided operational disruption costs.

### Initial Investment Comparison

### Where Pick & Carry Wins Financially

The higher initial investment of a crawler platform pays back through:

1. **Labor productivity** — 6-10 workers productive simultaneously vs. 2-3 on a spider lift. On a 10,000m² ceiling project, this typically saves 2,000-4,000 labor-hours.

2. **Schedule compression** — 30-40% faster completion means earlier project handover and revenue generation. For airport and stadium projects, schedule delays carry liquidated damages of $10,000-$50,000 per day.

3. **Eliminated repositioning waste** — every spider lift repositioning costs 30-45 minutes of non-productive time. On a project requiring 200 positions, that's 100-150 hours of pure waste eliminated.

4. **Rental utilization** — for equipment rental companies, the pick & carry platform's higher daily output justifies premium rental rates and delivers faster ROI (typically 12-18 months).

### ROI Timeline

For **contractors** using the platform on 3-4 projects per year: 12-18 months payback.
For **rental companies**: 18-24 months payback based on utilization rates of 60-70%.
For **facility owners** (airports, stadiums) maintaining their own equipment: payback within the first major renovation project.
