**Choose an eligible continuous process when its complete cost is lower than the accepted press route for the same orders, or it meets a required dispatch programme the press route cannot serve. Choose eligible discontinuous press production when it wins that same complete comparison.** Buy exceptional panels externally when neither supported internal route is justified. Both proposals must serve the accepted facing, core and format requirements. Product eligibility and a successful sample are prerequisites; the investment also needs a funded operating case.

This comparison starts inside an already justified composite-panel business. The separate [sheet-former versus sandwich-panel factory guide](/blog/sandwich-panel-production-line-vs-roof-sheet-roll-former/) decides which finished-product business to enter. Here the buyer has released insulated roof-panel requirements and must choose the architecture of the complete manufacturing operation.

## Two owned processes need the same accepted panel brief

Issue the panel family's facing materials, core, joint, dimensions and required product evidence through the responsible product and design parties. Group orders by accepted product revision. Keep enquiries for other cores, joints or applications separate until their production and qualification routes are established.

An eligible continuous proposal and an eligible press proposal must both address those actual products. A quotation for a different core or joint is a different manufactured product, even when it uses the same sales description. Retain the customer's thermal, fire, structural and other product requirements in the acceptance brief; the equipment buyer should not redesign the panel to make an equipment comparison convenient.

[Hennecke's continuous sandwich-panel information](https://www.hennecke.com/en/loesungen/nach-maschine-anlage/sandwichpanel-anlagen/kontinuierliche-sandwichpanel-anlagen) describes integrated production from material preparation through packaging for metal-faced panels. Its [discontinuous range](https://www.hennecke.com/en/loesungen/nach-maschine-anlage/sandwichpanel-anlagen/diskontinuierliche-sandwichpanel-anlagen) describes metering-and-press equipment for specified facings, formats and PUR/PIR products. These establish process categories. Each proposed roof-panel family still needs its own supported manufacturing and product-release route.

## Campaign mix matters more than a headline machine rate

Use the actual order book to describe repeated products and required changes. Record the panel family, quantity, delivery date and the features that change between campaigns. Include facing profile and finish, core specification, joint details and format where those affect the offered process.

A repeat metal-faced programme deserves a complete continuous-line proposal. Smaller or custom-format work deserves comparison with an eligible discontinuous proposal rather than automatic rejection on a headline output figure. Test each against the actual required dispatch schedule and accepted product, including changeovers and remaining tasks.

Obtain representative order evidence from the actual process provider. Record input materials, produced products, accepted quantities, required resources and the complete observation boundary. Distinguish an individual panel trial from evidence supporting the proposed campaign programme. Assess the whole factory's economics alongside that product-feasibility evidence.

## Price every resource between raw input and packed product

A continuous offer needs its complete material-to-output scope and installed layout. A discontinuous offer needs its actual facing preparation, metering, press and mould resources, handling and finishing scope. Obtain the process provider's complete equipment and operating requirements rather than treating a press or a conveyor as the whole factory.

Keep raw-material supply and process readiness with the appointed qualified parties. The buyer needs priced utilities, premises work, materials management, staffing, inspection and service arrangements. Technical process settings and chemical handling belong to those specialists' controlled systems, outside this purchase comparison.

Include all required auxiliary and quality resources in both cases. If one provider's quoted boundary ends before trimming, packing or product release, obtain the missing scope before ranking it against a complete line. Likewise, define whether facing preparation is internal, separately purchased or supplied within the offered plant.

## A programme-and-process investment worksheet

Use the same planning period and accepted dispatch boundary. Enter the actual proposal reference in each row so a changed product or excluded operation remains visible.

| Investment input | Fillable project record | Responsible owner and reference | Decision use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Accepted products | Facing, core, joint, format and product revision ___ | Product authority; released panel drawings/specification | Establishes eligible manufacturing proposals |
| Supported demand | Orders by panel family, quantity and dispatch date ___ | Commercial lead; committed order schedule | Separates repeat campaigns from exceptional products |
| Process boundary | Every operation from received materials to packed panel ___ | Process provider; issued flow diagram | Exposes missing stations and outside services |
| Change programme | Required product transitions and representative order evidence ___ | Production lead; actual campaign schedule | Tests complete output against the delivery requirement |
| Press/line resources | Offered equipment, moulds, auxiliaries and attendance ___ | Process provider; identified equipment schedule | Establishes each complete operating package |
| Factory fit | Measured zones, utility connection points and material routes ___ | Facilities engineer; building-grid and floor-plan datums | Prices installation and occupied space |
| Product release | Required checks, records, qualification and release owner ___ | Quality lead; destination/customer acceptance plan | Funds accepted saleable output |
| Complete economics | Installed cost and operating costs through accepted dispatch ___ | Buyer and finance lead; same orders and period | Selects the supported investment or outside supply |

For mixed demand, compare a defined internal repeat range with purchased exceptional panels. A plant does not need to serve every possible enquiry to justify its accepted workload. Conversely, count only products that the offered process and qualification plan actually address when calculating the internal production case.

## Two hypothetical investment failures from partial evidence

**Hypothetical failure case: a successful panel sample becomes the full campaign forecast.** A buyer accepts one product trial and assumes the proposed factory will meet a changing weekly order mix. Required transitions and their resources remain outside the evidence. Obtain a representative programme comparison and complete cost before choosing the architecture.

**Hypothetical failure case: a press amount is compared with a complete factory.** Another buyer ranks a discontinuous press quotation below an integrated line while leaving facing preparation, material resources and product-release work unpriced. Complete both boundaries through packed, accepted output before comparing capital and operating commitments.
