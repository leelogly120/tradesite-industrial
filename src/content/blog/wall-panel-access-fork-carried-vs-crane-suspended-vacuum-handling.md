Buy a documented fork-carried vacuum package when its actual carrier and adapter present every nominated panel and remain available for the required slots. Choose an accepted crane-suspended package where its geometry serves positions the fork route excludes or competing carrier work makes that route incomplete. When both complete the same wall on time, choose the lower complete handling and personnel-access cost, including exceptions and returns. Shortlist ARC-F25 for separate repeated fixing and sealing positions served by its configuration. First put panel, device, carrier and people positions on one wall section and floor plan, then request both complete offers.

## Start after the panel and vacuum method are accepted

Fix the panel product, delivered state, dimensions, mass and installation orientations. The panel maker and handling specialist establish whether the actual device and contact arrangement suit it. This comparison starts after that acceptance; changing the panel surface or assuming every sandwich panel accepts vacuum lifting creates a different purchase.

A real dual-carrier example is [Wirth's Oktopus PANEL-Jack GL-E](https://www.wirth-gmbh.com/en/construction-zone/sandwich-devices/gl-e-crane-horizontal). The manufacturer identifies crane-eye and forklift-adapter forms for wall-panel work. Its information separately records their configuration mass, controls and surface conditions. Those distinctions make the carrier decision a complete-equipment purchase rather than an alternative way to hang an identical-priced tool. Wirth excludes protective film on the suction areas for this device. Resolve a film-on delivery state with the panel maker and handling provider before treating that package as eligible; do not infer acceptance from a similar bare panel.

The stated forklift category does not establish compatibility with an arbitrary telescopic handler. Require the actual carrier, adapter and device identification and the responsible provider's accepted combined response. Both alternatives remain material-handling equipment. Workers retain their own access machine.

## A fork-carried device changes the offered carrier

Obtain the complete assembly the handling provider proposes: carrier configuration, adapter, vacuum device and the actual panel. Identify the matching chart and configuration documents in its response. A bare-fork rating does not describe the offered wall-panel presentation arrangement.

Read the adapter and device data together. [Wirth's corresponding fork-carried GL-E page](https://www.wirth-gmbh.com/en/construction-zone/sandwich-devices/gl-e-forklift-vertical) identifies the adapter and a side-shift option. The buyer needs the actual included configuration and its delivered mass, rather than combining the crane-form device data with a fork-carrier price.

Follow the accepted panel positions through the building. The carrier needs the actual approach and working space assigned by the handling plan, while the fixing crew needs an independent position. Check the complete presented object on the wall section, including the device and the space behind it. A quoted lift height alone leaves that geometry unanswered.

Reserve the carrier through the actual panel slots. If it also unloads deliveries or serves another trade, show those bookings in the same programme. Price an agreed replacement or changed delivery arrangement for each conflict. Owning a suitable carrier does not make it available for two simultaneous duties.

## A suspended package has its own geometry and attendance

For the crane route, obtain the actual crane and suspended-device arrangement serving the same panel-position schedule. The crane provider and handling specialist supply the accepted response for the lifting and presentation duty. Include the device, its suspension and actual panel geometry in that response, rather than crediting the crane with the panel location alone.

Identify the crane's accepted standing and working areas, the required receiving space and the controlled area associated with the handling proposal. Put those beside the personnel platform's base and supports. Resolve their interaction through the responsible specialists before treating two individually acceptable machines as one deliverable installation.

Read the control and attendance scope. The [GL-E manufacturer datasheet](https://www.wirth-gmbh.com/downloads/datasheet/panel-jack/panel-jack_GL-E.pdf) distinguishes the control arrangements for its two forms. Obtain the actual provider's operator and control-position commitments for the ordered equipment. Price the actual operator, material-handling and fixing duties with the offered control arrangement.

That datasheet is an example revision, rather than the current project lifting plan. Use the actual offered documents for the unit being ordered. Procurement records the accepted equipment and resources; it does not calculate lifting capacity, select suction positions or prescribe rigging.

## Keep the personnel purchase in both totals

Quote the repeated fixing and sealing work from its own task inventory. A broad shared deck warrants comparison where several adjacent positions need crew and tools together. Isolated corners, returns or obstructed approaches need a separately defined equipment response. Panel presentation and crew access must both reach their respective accepted positions.

![A red multi-level personnel-access structure stands in a narrow corridor between industrial buildings.](/images/equipment/buyer-20261029-wall-corridor.webp)

*A visible wall face still needs a workable joint layout for people and the separate panel handler.*

Compare the actual shared ground and airspace, not just two machine outlines in separate drawings. Include staging, support footprints, access routes and required work periods. Do not assign the panel to the personnel deck, guardrail or visible auxiliary hook to remove a missing handling resource from the budget.

For each qualifying route, total carrier and device supply, adapters, transport, specialist attendance, people access, agreed waiting and exceptional work. Use the same accepted wall quantities and dates. Where only one package serves a required position, price its complete resource; where both qualify, award the lower complete installed-wall cost.

## Two hypothetical carrier-purchase mistakes

**Mistake (hypothetical): the fork chart omits the offered attachment.** A buyer accepts a carrier's bare-fork rating and budgets a vacuum device using the other carrier form's mass. Obtain the accepted combined configuration and matching project response before ranking the complete fork-carried quotation.

**Mistake (hypothetical): separate plans claim the same standing area.** A crane reaches the panel on its own section, while the fixing platform occupies its only accepted working area. Resolve the combined section and floor plan through the specialists, then price the eligible handling or personnel alternative for the conflicting locations.

## Eight RFQ attachments for separate personnel access

1. Accepted panel identity, orientations and handling requirements with actual dimensions and masses.
2. Measured fixing, sealing and inspection sections with heights/offsets from supporting-floor datums.
3. Concurrent people, tools, permitted objects and task positions with dimensions and masses.
4. Entry, turning, transport and setup survey reserving receiving and panel-presentation areas separately.
5. Floor/support records and requested configuration-specific personnel-equipment reactions and footprint.
6. Both actual carrier/device/adapter packages with current model/serial/revision records and accepted geometry, including exceptional positions.
7. Panel slots, operator/control positions, fixing handovers and priced carrier conflicts or returns.
8. Destination, site power, transport/storage scope and requested platform geometry, task limits and handover records.
