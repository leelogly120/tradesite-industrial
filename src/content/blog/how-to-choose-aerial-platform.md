# How to Choose the Right Aerial Platform: A Practical Guide

I've watched too many contractors waste money on the wrong equipment. They either over-spec (paying for capacity they don't need) or under-spec (causing delays and safety issues). Here's the simple framework I use.

## The 5-Question Decision Matrix

Before looking at any equipment, answer these 5 questions:

### Question 1: What's Your Working Height?

**Measure from ground to highest work point, then add 2m safety margin.**

| Actual Height | Platform Needed | Why |
|---------------|-----------------|-----|
| <12m | 15m | Safety margin |
| 12-18m | 20m | Standard coverage |
| 18-25m | 25-30m | Full reach |
| 25-35m | 35-40m | Maximum height |

### Question 2: What's Your Coverage Area?

| Area Size | Platform Type | Reason |
|-----------|--------------|--------|
| <1,000 sqm | Scissor lift | Sufficient, low cost |
| 1,000-5,000 sqm | Boom lift | Good reach, reasonable cost |
| 5,000-15,000 sqm | Pick & carry | Fast coverage, efficient |
| >15,000 sqm | Pick & carry | Essential for speed |

### Question 3: What's Your Floor Type?

| Floor Type | Ground Pressure Limit | Required Equipment |
|------------|----------------------|-------------------|
| Reinforced concrete | >2.0 MPa | Any crawler |
| Polished concrete | 1.5 MPa | Low pressure crawler |
| Epoxy coating | 1.0 MPa | Ultra-low pressure |
| Synthetic turf | 0.5 MPa | Ultra-low pressure |
| Marble/granite | 0.5 MPa | Ultra-low pressure |

### Question 4: Indoor or Outdoor?

| Environment | Power Source | Reason |
|-------------|-------------|--------|
| Indoor only | Electric | Zero emissions, quiet |
| Outdoor only | Diesel | Full power, extended runtime |
| Both | Dual power | Maximum flexibility |

### Question 5: What's Your Budget?

| Budget Range | Recommendation |
|--------------|----------------|
| <$30,000 | Rent standard equipment |
| $30,000-80,000 | Rent specialized equipment |
| $80,000-150,000 | Purchase or long-term lease |
| >$150,000 | Purchase with service contract |

## The Decision Matrix

Rate your project on each factor (1-5):

| Factor | Weight | Score |
|--------|--------|-------|
| Height requirement | 25% | ? |
| Coverage area | 25% | ? |
| Floor sensitivity | 20% | ? |
| Indoor/outdoor | 15% | ? |
| Budget | 15% | ? |

**Score 1-2:** Standard scissor lift
**Score 3-4:** Boom lift or basic crawler
**Score 5-6:** Specialized crawler (pick & carry)
**Score 7+:** Premium crawler with all features

## Common Mistakes

### Mistake 1: Over-Specifying

**Example:** Using a 35m lift for 15m work

**Cost impact:** 40% higher rental cost
**Solution:** Match equipment to actual requirements

### Mistake 2: Under-Specifying

**Example:** Using an 18m lift for 25m work

**Cost impact:** Project delays, safety risks
**Solution:** Add 20% margin to height requirement

### Mistake 3: Ignoring Floor Protection

**Example:** Using high ground pressure equipment on epoxy floors

**Cost impact:** $50,000+ in floor repairs
**Solution:** Always assess ground pressure requirements

### Mistake 4: Forgetting Indoor/Outdoor

**Example:** Using diesel-only equipment indoors

**Cost impact:** Emission violations, health risks
**Solution:** Use dual power for mixed environments

## Quick Reference Guide

| Need | Recommendation |
|------|---------------|
| Large area, sensitive floor | Pick & carry crawler |
| High reach, rough terrain | Boom lift |
| Tight spaces | Spider lift |
| Indoor work | Electric crawler |
| Outdoor work | Diesel crawler |
| Budget conscious | Standard scissor lift |
