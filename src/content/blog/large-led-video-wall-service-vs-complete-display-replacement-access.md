**Buy a bounded service campaign when the actual display provider accepts the retained system and identifies the required replacement parts and work positions. Buy complete renewal when the accepted project changes the display, its support arrangement or its service geometry beyond that retained scope.** Price personnel access, specialist handling and the completed display handback separately. ARC-F25 is a personnel-access candidate, not a display-lifting tool.

A large screen can look like a repeated field of interchangeable modules while its service requirements belong to one identified product and installation. The buyer needs the real asset records and a competent provider's scope before treating a module price as a complete maintenance purchase.

## Establish the retained display and support boundary

Obtain the actual product references, installation records, condition findings and owner's required outcome. Identify modules and other equipment covered by the proposed service, with separately defined support or building work. A similar image on another screen is insufficient to establish a replacement part or service route.

[Daktronics' module-handling guidance](https://www.daktronics.com/en-us/support/kb/000020638) refers to front and rear access and directs users to display-specific service information where components obstruct access. Its [site-configuration information](https://www.daktronics.com/en-us/support/kb/000027523) also distinguishes access arrangements. Those examples show why the actual product and service side matter; they are not a maintenance procedure or compatibility decision for your display.

For complete renewal, obtain the selected display's support, service-space and building-interface requirements from the responsible specialists. Identify what remains and what changes. A new electronic product can create construction and access duties outside the price of the display cabinets.

## Front-face appearance can hide a rear-service purchase

Have the nominated specialist mark the actual work faces and required positions for the accepted tasks. Include access behind or beside the display where the product and installation need it. A platform proposed against the visible front does not answer a different rear-side task.

Keep service access distinct from the movement of new or removed components. The appointed display and handling specialists identify those objects, their requirements and the accepted material equipment. The personnel platform supplies only the functions supported by its actual configuration and agreed scope.

## Compare the complete accepted display outcome

Use this worksheet for a specific asset or replacement project. Keep the electronic product, building support, personnel access and material services as visible purchase lines.

| Purchase field | Fillable project entry | Responsible owner and datum | Decision use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Owner's requirement | Retained service or new-system outcome ___ | Asset owner; accepted project brief | Defines the comparable result |
| Existing system | Product IDs ___; condition/support records ___ | Display and building specialists; located survey | Establishes the retained boundary |
| Service package | Actual parts ___; accepted compatibility ___ | Nominated display provider; written asset-specific offer | Releases the bounded service route |
| Renewal package | New display/support/service-space scope ___ | Responsible designers; issued installation documents | Defines construction beyond product supply |
| Personnel faces | Front/rear/side tasks ___; positions ___ | Specialist installer; local floor/building datums | Tests actual access coverage |
| Material services | Objects ___; dimensions/masses ___; handler ___ | Display and handling providers; actual component schedule | Prices separate removal and presentation |
| Acceptance | Checks, integration and handback scope ___ | Display specialist and owner; project requirements | Establishes returns and completion duties |
| Complete total | Product ___; construction ___; handling ___; access ___ | Buyer; same accepted handback | Supports the renewal investment |

When only the retained service route is technically available, compare actual provider packages for that route. When the owner selects a new system, replace the old work-position and material assumptions with its real requirements. A generic alternative is not useful simply because it fills the other column in a budget.

## Price occupation around the actual venue

For an occupied concourse, hall or production space, the operator identifies the released areas and work periods. Include delivery, setup, specialist work, checks, removal and reinstatement. A short component task can still require a significant venue possession once its complete access and protection scope is counted.

For a replacement project, reserve staging and separately defined material equipment. Coordinate those resources with the personnel platform on one layout. The building reviewer needs the actual equipment reactions and positions, while the display specialist owns its support and handling requirements.

The low deck in the photograph remains a complete machine in the room. Use the offered configuration's dimensions for arrival, waiting and exit, including the building state after the display is installed.

## Two hypothetical video-wall orders with missing functions

**Failure case: module dimensions become service compatibility.** A buyer purchases similar-looking replacement parts and prices a front-side visit, while the actual asset and required service face remain unassessed. Obtain the display provider's asset-specific service proposal and required positions before buying components or access.

**Failure case: a broad deck becomes the assumed display hoist.** Another project budgets a personnel platform but assigns cabinet support and replacement movement to it without an applicable material arrangement. Buy the specialist handling scope and coordinate it with access before committing the installation programme.
