# Low Ground Pressure Aerial Lifts: Protecting Your Floors

A client called me last month. "We have a problem," they said. "Your competitor's lift just ruined our $75,000 epoxy floor."

That's a phone call I never want to receive. And it's why ground pressure matters more than most people realize.

## The $75,000 Mistake

### What Happened

A contractor used a spider lift with 1.8 MPa ground pressure on a newly installed epoxy floor. The tracks left permanent grooves that couldn't be repaired—only replaced.

### The Numbers

| Cost Item | Amount |
|-----------|--------|
| Floor removal | $15,000 |
| New floor installation | $50,000 |
| Project delay (2 weeks) | $10,000 |
| **Total** | **$75,000** |

### The Cause

No ground pressure assessment was done. The contractor assumed "it's a crawler, it should be fine."

**It wasn't.**

## Understanding Ground Pressure

### What is Ground Pressure?

Ground pressure = Equipment weight ÷ Contact area

**Example:**
- Equipment weight: 5,000 kg
- Track contact area: 2.5 sqm
- Ground pressure: 2.0 MPa

### Why It Matters

Different floor surfaces have different load-bearing capacities:

| Floor Type | Maximum Safe Pressure | Damage Risk |
|------------|----------------------|-------------|
| Reinforced concrete | >3.0 MPa | Low |
| Polished concrete | 1.5-2.0 MPa | Medium |
| Epoxy coating | 0.8-1.2 MPa | High |
| Synthetic turf | 0.5-0.8 MPa | Very high |
| Marble/granite | 0.5-1.0 MPa | High |

## Floor Damage Prevention

### Before Starting Work

1. **Identify floor type** — What's the surface material?
2. **Determine pressure limit** — What's the maximum safe pressure?
3. **Select equipment** — Choose equipment with appropriate ground pressure
4. **Test if uncertain** — Small test area first

### During Work

1. **Monitor ground conditions** — Watch for soft spots
2. **Avoid sudden turns** — Reduces shear forces
3. **Use protective mats** — Extra protection for sensitive areas
4. **Clean tracks** — Prevent debris from scratching floors

### After Work

1. **Inspect floor** — Check for any damage
2. **Document condition** — Photos and notes
3. **Report issues** — Immediate notification
