Buy a designed roof-diaphragm package when the structural engineer accepts the exact sheet, connections, openings and load path, and the panel business can fund their controlled manufacture and handover. Retain the engineer-accepted separately braced roof scheme when that supported diaphragm route is absent. Compare complete engineer-accepted schemes for the same building before committing forming tools. First obtain the issued stability drawings and identify the roof-sheet duties in each offer.

This decision concerns the product your panel business promises to supply. A sheet acting in an engineered diaphragm has an assigned role in transferring in-plane forces through its connections. In the separately braced option, obtain the sheet duties from that scheme's designer; do not assume that separate bracing removes every structural duty from the cladding. Neither an attractive sample nor a familiar rib outline determines the assigned structural role.

The loose ribbed samples below show the manufacturing component to identify. Put its controlled section beside the designer's connection and roof-zone drawings before attaching a structural sales description.

## Cost the complete stability schemes

[ArcelorMittal's stressed-skin introduction](https://constructalia.arcelormittal.com/en/sica-steel-academy/applications-steel-solutions/thin-walled-construction/diaphragm-effect-stressed-skin-design) explains the contribution of profiled sheeting and its positive connections to a building's resistance and stiffness. It identifies connections at sheets, supports and panel edges as parts of the force-transfer arrangement. Its discussion is a technical explanation, not evidence that any unnamed roof panel qualifies.

The [Swedish steel institute's publication description](https://www.sbi.se/produkt/stabilisation-by-stressed-skin-diaphragm-action/) describes sheeting, beams and rafters acting together to transfer wind forces to wall bracing, with sheet and fastener drawings forming part of the documentation. A diaphragm offer therefore still needs the rest of its designated structural route. Removing a roof-bracing line from a bill of quantities does not produce a complete alternative scheme.

Have the structural engineer identify both eligible arrangements and the connection, member and installation scope assigned to each. The commercial comparison then includes the panels, retained steelwork, required connection components, engineering, delivery, installation and records. Include changes to the surrounding roof package that the designer requires for either option.

Keep the weathering and building-envelope requirements in both bids. A proposal that serves the structural role still has to deliver the selected roof assembly. The roof designer must coordinate its actual layers and details with the structural scheme before the product buyer releases the manufacturing drawings.

## Make the qualified product visible in the line purchase

The product definition should name the sheet section, material, thickness basis, geometry, finish and drawing revision accepted for its assigned use. Connect those records to the forming proposal and trial plan. Require actual measurements against the released drawing, with the agreed sampling and disposition of rejected output. Select a representative order with its material identity and length mix so the forming trial produces the same sheet revision offered to the customer.

Then list the specified sheet-to-support, sheet-to-sheet and edge connection components using the designer's references. Procurement buys those references and the installation scope allocated to them. The structural designer supplies the connection arrangement and any revised bracing requirements. Buy the parts and installation defined in those issued drawings.

A producer entering diaphragm-panel work must also fund the evidence and product-control organisation needed for that range. Name the evidence owner and the engineer accepting its application. Record the represented construction and permitted production revisions. A machine trial establishes its agreed manufacturing output, while the appointed structural parties accept the building application.

Use the table to identify the resulting purchase boundary. Complete it separately for each proposed scheme and retain the same building-plan references.

Choose component-only supply where the business has an accepted sheet-manufacturing duty and another named party supplies the engineered roof system. Choose the coordinated diaphragm-product offer only when its engineering, component sources, quality work and customer handover are funded. Price the engineering, evidence, purchased connection components and product-control work against qualifying diaphragm orders. Retain ordinary sheet orders in their own forecast so the added product organisation earns its cost on the work it can actually serve.

## Openings and later changes belong in the award

Ask the building team to issue the coordinated roof plan, including rooflights, plant penetrations and future service zones. The structural engineer identifies their consequences for the eligible schemes. Obtain that decision before changing the sheet layout or pricing a replacement field. Include the engineer's review, revised components and defined installation work in each alternative's allowance for the scheduled building changes.

The roof photograph below shows long sheet runs and joints. Their appearance alone does not identify an engineered diaphragm; use the issued product and connection drawings to assign the role. Keep the actual panel arrangement in the owner handover so a future contractor knows which design information controls changes.

ArcelorMittal's introduction also explains that a stressed-skin sheet field requires structural consideration before removal and that owners need information about its role. Allocate the required building records and change-review responsibility in the contract. This is a real continuing product boundary to price when comparing the two schemes.

**Mistake (hypothetical): a different sheet keeps the original structural label.** A purchaser substitutes a visually similar profile after the diaphragm scheme is accepted. Its material and connection interface differ from the released construction, so the supplied product no longer matches the scheme purchased. Return that proposed substitution to the structural and product authorities before ordering; use only the construction their revised decision accepts.

**Mistake (hypothetical): future plant openings disappear from the comparison.** A roof buyer prices a continuous diaphragm field while the services plan already shows later equipment penetrations. The bids describe different buildings and different retained work. Issue the coordinated openings to the engineer and obtain complete revised scheme prices before award.
