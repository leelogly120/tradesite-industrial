## Fix what must remain after the dirt is removed

The purchase concerns grime removal from existing overhead surfaces. Identify the actual finish, dirt and accepted cleaned condition. Keep paint removal, substrate profiling and replacement of damaged material outside that comparison unless the owner expressly changes the work. The cleaning specialist and surface owner release the method for those materials and contaminants.

[Cold Jet identifies ceilings, rafters and overhead framework](https://coldjet.com/dry-ice-blasting/applications/facility-maintenance/) among dry-ice applications. That establishes a real equipment category, rather than acceptance for every painted ceiling or electrical component. An attractive demonstration on one beam does not establish the required result on every retained face.

Have the trial record the actual presented condition, treated locations, retained-finish acceptance and any excluded surfaces. Give inaccessible backs, narrow junctions and hand-finished details their own task entries. Both quotations must include the work needed to reach the agreed cleanliness across the priced area.

## Dry ice buys a supply and gas-management system

Identify the offered dry-ice equipment before budgeting deliveries. [Kärcher's L2P explanation](https://www.karcher.com/us/en/commercial/dry-ice-blaster-ib-10-8-l2p) distinguishes liquid-CO2 equipment producing pellets on demand from the conventional delivered-pellet supply chain. These create different receiving, storage and replenishment purchases. A single allowance called “dry ice delivery” does not describe both.

For a delivered-pellet offer, obtain the actual supply, receiving and storage commitments. For a liquid-CO2 offer, obtain the corresponding cylinder supply and handling arrangements. Add the proposed air and electrical supply, hoses and floor-plant positions. Price the real providers and attendance rather than assuming existing plant services are available at every cleaning zone.

The [Kärcher IB10/8 L2P manual](https://s1.kaercher-media.com/documents/manuals/html/BTA-5778340-000-04/EN.html) identifies site risk assessment and air-exchange requirements and excludes potentially explosive atmospheres for that equipment. The facility and cleaning specialists must supply the accepted environmental package for the actual work. Obtain the facility's acceptance of the actual cleaning package, including its supplies and personnel equipment.

Keep the removed dirt in the contract. The manual describes dirt being blown away during cleaning. The CO2 medium turns into gas, but the removed material still needs the contractor's accepted collection, protection and handback scope. Include the consequences for machinery, stock and other surfaces below the work.

## Wet washing buys recovery and a released condition

A wet quotation needs the actual water supply, cleaning products where specified, plant, hoses and collection arrangements. Name the provider responsible for contaminated water, its receiving destination and the facility condition required before handback. Water use alone is not a complete washing contract.

[Nilfisk FOOD's Griff datasheet](https://nilfiskfood.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/06/Griff-wall.pdf) provides a real wall-and-ceiling cleaning example with water-pump, compressor and utility information. Its named application and supply data do not establish coverage for a high-bay project. Obtain the specialist's response for the actual workface, supply route and operating duty instead of extending a product description to any hose length or elevation.

Keep cleaning separate from any required microbiological treatment. If the owner requires additional disinfection, testing or a later production release, identify those as distinct included duties or separate purchases. Do not credit a dry grime-removal demonstration with the result of a wet cleaning-and-sanitising contract.

Where the facility requires a dry condition, book the equipment and area occupation through that release. Use the real contractor programme and acceptance record; an assumed drying interval has no place in the equipment comparison.

## Give the platform supplier the real cleaning task

A broad deck is a candidate where crew and tools repeatedly occupy adjacent overhead faces. Fragmented details require a separate coverage response. Identify the work positions on sections, measured from the actual supporting floor, alongside the cleaning-plant footprint and hose routes.

![A raised red personnel deck stands beneath white hall framing with rough wet ground and neighboring equipment visible.](/images/equipment/buyer-20261029-deck-hall.webp)

*Reserve the full personnel-equipment footprint beside the cleaning plant and its supply routes.*

List actual tool and hose interactions, not just the mass of people and containers. Request the access provider's equipment-specific response to the proposed task and environment. Include the cleaning provider's declared tool-reaction and connected-hose demands; a mass-only schedule leaves these task effects outside the review. The personnel-equipment provider must accept the actual cleaning task and environment for its proposed configuration. Procure an appropriate different access package for excluded positions or conditions.

## Two hypothetical cleaning-purchase mistakes

**Mistake (hypothetical): disappearing media deletes the dirt-collection budget.** A buyer removes all waste-handling work from the dry-ice offer because the media becomes gas. Greasy flakes and released dirt still reach protected production areas. Retain the actual soil collection, affected-area protection and facility handback in the specialist quotation.

**Mistake (hypothetical): a washing rate beats a complete dry package.** One wet offer excludes capture, contaminated-water disposal and the required dry release. Those costs remain with the factory while the other bidder includes handback. Complete both resource contracts and their actual access occupation before comparing totals.
