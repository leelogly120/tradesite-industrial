**Choose an offered lithium-ion system when its documented duty covers the ceiling-work cycle, its charging arrangement fits the real breaks, and destination support makes the complete package worth buying. Choose lead-acid when the required work fits its documented duty and the owner can provide its charging and care resources. Separate flooded batteries from AGM before comparing prices.** First send the work sequence and available charging windows to the equipment maker; compare complete platform proposals against that same schedule. When both complete offers meet it, select the lower supported ownership total over the same buyer-approved period.

The purchase is an integrated battery, charger and machine-control arrangement. It comes after choosing a platform that reaches the workfaces with an accepted deck, floor route and support arrangement. An attractive battery offer cannot compensate for a machine that cannot serve the building. Keep ceiling access and energy provision as two connected decisions.

The raised deck below illustrates the work to describe: repeated elevated tasks, relocation between released positions, and periods when the crew is working without demanding another machine movement. Record those activities separately. An elapsed shift and a continuous lifting test are different evidence.

*Map lifting, repositioning and waiting periods to the actual ceiling workfaces before comparing battery duty.*

## Three proposals sit behind the two chemistry names

Flooded lead-acid, AGM lead-acid and lithium-ion should occupy separate quotation lines. [Genie's announcement of its next-generation slab scissors](https://www.genielift.com/docs/default-source/press-releases/en/2024/genie-launches-future-ready-scissor-lifts.pdf) distinguishes FLA, AGM and lithium-ion arrangements for its named product family. Genie’s named designs illustrate three quotation categories; obtain the actual proposed platform’s battery schedule before assigning any of them to the purchase.

For flooded batteries, include the actual care tasks, access and responsible staff. [Trojan's maintenance guidance](https://www.trojanbattery.com/resources/battery-maintenance) identifies watering for flooded batteries and distinguishes their care from AGM. Buy the owner's ability to perform the specified programme alongside the cells. A low purchase amount is weak value if the required work has no assigned resource.

AGM belongs in the comparison when a documented lead-acid system meets the duty and the owner wants that distinct care arrangement. Do not charge lithium with the cost of watering an AGM alternative. Equally, a maintenance-free battery description does not remove the platform's inspection, electrical connection and charger-care obligations.

For lithium, obtain the identified pack, battery-management system, matched charger, supported machine controls and replacement package as one offer. The relevant comparison is the supplied system's task capability and ownership cost. An advertised cell-cycle count from another application is not a fleet replacement forecast.

## The recharge opportunity must exist on the programme

Have the work planner mark the start and finish of each required task period. Include travel in the permitted configuration, repeated raising, stationary work, waiting and withdrawal. The maker then identifies which offered arrangement meets that sequence and the conditions behind its evidence. Require both alternatives to cover the same crew, permitted equipment and working positions.

The site electrical owner records where charging is accepted, when supply is available and the actual electrical interface. A lunch break spent moving the machine away from occupied work is not wholly available for charging. Include the movement and connection arrangements in the provider's programme, without inventing a universal charging duration.

Record operating, charging and storage temperatures separately against the offered system's documents. A battery's operating range cannot be substituted for its charging range. Price any specified temperature-control or storage provision with the associated platform offer. If the site cannot supply an essential condition, remove that offer from the feasible comparison.

## Buy a replacement route, not a promise about cells

Genie's [scissor maintenance manual](https://manuals.genielift.com/Parts%20And%20Service%20Manuals/data/Service/Scissors/1305686.pdf) requires the proper machine software configuration when upgrading from standard batteries. The purchasing lesson is concrete: battery changes belong to the equipment maker's controlled configuration, rather than a later cell substitution by the importer.

Obtain the offered replacement assembly, associated configuration work, service provider and destination quotation. Include fault information and the support needed to distinguish a battery problem from a charger or machine problem. Credit a local source only for the identified compatible package it actually offers.

The yard photograph shows the separate equipment occupation involved in service and delivery. Ask for the real battery-service access and material-handling requirements on the offered machine, then reserve them within the owner's maintenance arrangement. A broad deck and a parked crawler still occupy floor space during that work.

## Two hypothetical battery purchases that disappoint

**Mistake (hypothetical): flooded care is charged to the AGM bid.** A contractor compares lithium with a lead-acid price, adds watering labour to that column, and calls the lithium premium recovered. The actual alternative is AGM. Correct the comparison to the exact battery constructions and their documented care tasks before awarding the energy package.

**Mistake (hypothetical): the break is sold twice.** A planner allocates the same lunch interval to charging and to withdrawing the platform from a shared hall. The site electrical owner’s accepted charging location is outside that work area. The required recharge window is shorter than assumed. Rebuild the programme with the real withdrawal and charging arrangements, then choose a system whose documented duty covers the revised sequence.

<!-- audit-section: project-checklist -->
## Eight RFQ attachments for the complete platform offer

1. Ceiling plans, sections and workface heights from finished-floor datum.
2. Crew, tools and permitted material distribution by task.
3. Required lift, move, wait and withdrawal sequence.
4. Charging locations, usable windows and actual site-supply records.
5. Operating, charging and storage environmental conditions.
6. Exact battery, charger, controls and replacement-system proposal.
7. Owner's maintenance resources and destination service quotations.
8. Common ownership period and itemised complete-package cost comparison.
