Choose a diesel-equipped forming-lift package only when the importer has a valid applicable EPA entry basis for its actual engine and the required supporting records. Compare an actual engine-free offer when it meets the same machine duty with a complete, available site-power arrangement. If neither proposal reaches lawful entry and usable production, hold the purchase instead of ranking the two incomplete prices. Before paying, have the importer accept the engine-entry basis and the site electrical owner accept the alternative's utility basis. Both decisions must refer to the same shipped subsystem inventory and required forming duty.

Engine-free means the identified shipped scope contains no internal-combustion engine, including separate auxiliaries. It is a purchase boundary to establish from the real offer. The ARC catalogue identifies forming equipment families; the actual quotation must identify its offered power configuration.

## Start with every shipped power source

Separate the forming drive, lifting power arrangement, crawler or other carrier, vehicle chassis and loose auxiliary equipment. Record whether each contains an internal-combustion engine. Give every engine its identity and its place in the importer's entry review, even when it arrives inside a larger machine.

## The entry question follows the engine inside the machine

The [current EPA forms page](https://www.epa.gov/importing-vehicles-and-engines/publications-and-forms-importing-vehicles-and-engines) identifies Form 3520-21 for relevant heavy-duty highway and nonroad engines, including engines already installed in equipment. Its scope includes gasoline as well as diesel categories. Changing the fuel name is not the same as removing an engine from the shipment.

[EPA Form 3520-21 and its instructions](https://www.epa.gov/system/files/documents/2024-08/form3520-21-2024-08-secured-enabled.pdf) identify the engine categories and entry provisions. The certified-engine route calls for a valid applicable EPA certificate and an emission-control label in English. The form also contains specific exceptions and exemptions; it does not assign one universal code to every machine.

Have the importer match the actual engine family, physical identity and build information to the records supporting the selected lawful basis. Keep readable label images beside the identified engine and shipment entry, rather than an unattached example from another machine. Where an exception or exemption is proposed, have the responsible party document why that actual import meets it and which further conditions apply. A general brochure statement is insufficient for that decision.

The form includes a limited exception for certain new products imported by their original manufacturer with the specified certificate and label. A contractor or distributor importing through a supply partner should establish its own actual status before relying on it. The equipment quotation and the party making the declaration need consistent identities.

## Compare complete usable alternatives after the entry gate

An eligible diesel proposal still needs the fuel, service and site arrangements for its intended work. Its entry evidence answers an import question. The production brief separately establishes the accepted profile, former-and-lift interfaces, work locations and operating conditions.

For an engine-free proposal, obtain the actual offered drive schedule and utility requirements for every function. Have the site electrical owner identify the accepted supply at the intended connection point and the date it becomes available. Include the power equipment and integration work needed to perform the same duty. Compare delivered usable equipment, rather than an engine with an incomplete electrical alternative.

EPA's [nonroad-engine terminology](https://sor.epa.gov/sor_internet/registry/termreg/searchandretrieve/termsandacronyms/search.do?checkedAcronym=true&checkedTerm=true&hasDefinitions=false&matchCriteria=Contains&search=&term=Nonroad+engine) describes an internal-combustion-engine category. An ordinary electric motor is a different device. That distinction helps inventory the package; the actual engine classification and import requirements still follow the applicable current rules.

A separately shipped generator belongs on the engine inventory even if the forming machine itself uses an electric drive. If a different party supplies that generator locally, identify that separate supply and its responsibilities in the complete project comparison. The machine price should not appear lower because the necessary power source disappeared from the cost sheet.

## Two import mistakes to catch before dispatch

**Mistake (hypothetical): the former's motor stands for the whole package.** A buyer calls an offer electric after reviewing only the roll-forming drive. The hypothetical lifting offer includes a separate engine-driven power unit, but its identity is absent from the importer's engine list. Reconcile the entire subsystem inventory and establish that engine's applicable entry basis before accepting the order.

**Mistake (hypothetical): a gasoline generator is labelled an engine-free alternative.** A revised bid removes one diesel engine but adds a loose generator for the site duty. The revised packing scope lists that generator separately, but the purchaser compares only the main machine description. Include the generator and its engine in the import and cost review, or select a real alternative with the required accepted utilities already provided.

Used engines, foreign labels and proposed later conversions still require an applicable documented entry basis for the actual shipment before dispatch. Separate other customs, vehicle, electrical and destination requirements from the engine question instead of treating an engine-free inventory as complete equipment approval.

## RFQ attachments for a US-bound equipment proposal

1. Actual shipment subsystem list and complete engine inventory.
2. Installed engine manufacturer, family, model year and serial identity.
3. Readable emission-control label and applicable certificate records.
4. Importer's selected entry provision and supporting responsibility record.
5. Actual engine-free alternative quotation when one is offered.
6. Same forming duty, accepted utility data and separate power-equipment schedule.
7. Vehicle, upper-equipment and auxiliary scope with named responsible parties.
8. Packing identity, release hold point and complete delivered-cost comparison.
