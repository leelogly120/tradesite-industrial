Choose an actually offered compact feeder-straightener when it meets the accepted intermittent roof-panel feed duty and surface requirements at the lower complete installed and supported total. Choose separate straightening, buffering and feeding when the compact offer fails the required cycle or finish, or the qualifying separate package costs less over the same period. Give both bidders the same pre-punch/feed sequence, material programme, finished-panel acceptance and ownership period.

This decision begins after owned straightening and intermittent feeding have been selected. It concerns the complete entry cell: reel interface, straightening, feed, buffer, controls and site works. A simple continuous panel line without that selected duty belongs to a different comparison. Buying a press-feeding package simply because it fits the coil width is the wrong starting point.

## The compact label hides different material paths

A combined feeder-straightener brings functions together. A separate layout places distinct straightening and feeding equipment around a defined strip buffer. The production question is what moves or stops when the downstream feed cycle pauses, and how the proposed arrangement treats the accepted material throughout those states.

[Dimeco's press-feeding range](https://www.dimeco.com/en/press-feeding-solutions/) identifies uncoilers, straighteners and feeders as modules, alongside compact straightener-feeders. [COE's SpaceMaster range](https://info.cpec.com/coe-spacemaster) combines unwinding, straightening and feeding for stated progressive-die applications. These establish real equipment categories. Their press applications are separate from the actual roof-panel process being purchased here.

The pictured entry rollers and forming stands below help locate the handover into the forming machine. Put that handover on the offered layout with the upstream preparation and downstream feed operation. Identify which provider accepts their combined result.

## Floor space is an installed package

Measure the complete cell from the building's finished-floor datum. Include coil-loading access, guards, service positions, cabinets and movement around the line. A short equipment bed with an unpriced loading aisle is not a complete compact layout. Equally, separate machine lengths do not describe the required buffer and its controlled space.

Where a separate layout uses a pit, the civil proposal belongs in the investment. The building owner and responsible designer must address the actual slab, concealed services, permitted excavation and future reinstatement. An existing hole elsewhere in the factory is not evidence that a new pit is suitable. Where excavation is unavailable, compare only offered above-floor or compact arrangements accepted for the required duty.

In its [straightener discussion](https://www.formtekgroup.com/formtek-talks/what-to-consider-before-adding-a-straightener-to-your-operation/), Formtek distinguishes intermittent pull-through contact from driven straightening, including the implications for surface-critical material. The same discussion separates a feed buffer from accumulation intended to sustain a coil change. Keep those functions distinct in the quotations. This purchase fixes the feed-cycle duty; coil-change continuity is outside its acceptance endpoint.

A compact unit with driven rolls also needs its actual sequence identified. Mark which rolls contact the inspected face and which sections stop during the selected feed cycle on the provider's process description. Do not apply the pull-through example to every combined machine. Qualify the documented configuration through accepted surface condition and complete-order output.

Complete this table before comparing equipment totals. Each entry should identify the document or observed condition as well as its owner.

Run the comparison through the normal production sequence, including the actual stop/start pattern and return to accepted output. Tie the surface assessment to the faces and condition specified for shipment. A flat strip that looks satisfactory before the press or former is only an intermediate result. Preserve its identity through the accepted panel so later marks are assigned to the correct stage.

A separate layout spreads the feed interfaces and buffer responsibility across distinct units. A compact proposal combines functions within its offered assembly. Compare how each actual package provides maintenance access, replacement assemblies and competent support. Price the production cover needed during an identified repair, rather than treating local spare stock as a complete support plan. Include these resources over the same ownership period.

## Two purchases that lose their apparent saving

**Mistake (hypothetical): a short-feed demonstration becomes full coverage.** A shop accepts a compact trial using short feed increments on plain stock, while its normal programme contains longer feed increments on painted coil. The purchase omits a trial of the inspected painted face through repeated production pauses. Run that actual order through accepted finished panels. Select another qualifying layout or fund an accepted route for the excluded orders if the compact proposal fails.

**Mistake (hypothetical): the pit is outside the cheap quotation.** The separate equipment wins on purchase price, while the leased factory requires service diversions and later floor reinstatement. Add the accepted civil works and reinstatement to that offer. If the building scope is unavailable, remove that layout and rank the qualifying alternatives instead.

## Eight attachments for a complete entry-cell RFQ

1. **Panel definition:** controlled profile, feature positions, finished lengths and dispatch acceptance.
2. **Material programme:** grades, thicknesses, widths, lot identities, coil mass/inside/outside diameters and inspected-face requirements.
3. **Feed duty:** accepted intermittent pre-punch sequence, representative feed lengths and order quantities.
4. **Straightening basis:** actual incoming condition and agreed prepared-material result.
5. **Entry layouts:** complete offered compact/separate equipment, buffer, loading and service envelopes.
6. **Building record:** finished-floor datum, slab/services information, openings and pit/civil permissions.
7. **Acceptance responsibilities:** interface owners, coordinated trial scope and finished-panel evidence.
8. **Supply endpoint:** RF8/former identity, destination power, installation, civil exclusions and same-period support total.
