Choose a vertical fabric-lifting hangar door when the accepted design meets the required clear opening and operating duty and accommodates its upper storage, supports and service positions. Choose a horizontal sliding door when its accepted design accommodates the lateral travel and storage arrangement without conflicting with hangar operations. When both qualify, compare complete installed and commissioned openings, including personnel access and separate component handling. Select the lower complete cost that meets the required operating brief and handover date.

This purchase starts with a new door architecture, not an operator upgrade to an existing door. The buyer must coordinate where the door goes when open, the building it connects to, and the equipment needed to install and maintain it. The widest advertised opening does not answer those questions.

## Draw the open door as carefully as the closed door

[ASSA ABLOY's large-door overview](https://www.assaabloy.com/us/en/campaigns/megadoor-solutions-from-assa-abloy-entrance-systems) presents vertical fabric and horizontal rolling systems as different offerings for hangars and other large industrial openings. Its [horizontal-door page](https://www.assaabloy.com/us/en/solutions/products/hangar-and-large-application-doors/horizontal-rolling-doors) describes an engineered building-specific product. Those examples establish the categories, without transferring a manufacturer's dimensions, performance or supply services to ARCLIFT.

Give the door designer the aircraft or vehicle movement envelope issued by the operator, together with the required clear opening and building sections. Require the proposed door's open, moving and closed geometry on those drawings. Mark every storage and travel area belonging to that actual proposal.

## Upper storage and lateral storage create different interfaces

For a vertical fabric proposal, obtain the offered upper assembly, stored configuration and building connections. Identify where installers work during support preparation, assembly, specialist adjustment and acceptance. The nominated door and building specialists determine the system and installation method.

For a horizontal proposal, obtain the offered travel and parked-panel arrangement, track/support details and threshold interfaces. Identify which elements are part of the building work and which the door supplier includes. A door-leaf price does not complete the supporting construction, travel path or finished threshold.

Do not assume that every vertical door uses the same storage geometry or every sliding door needs the same external side pocket. Require the actual proposal's layout. The relevant design question is whether that arrangement preserves the operator's accepted opening, circulation and usable hangar space.

Compare both at the same operating endpoint. Include the required controls, specialist checks, commissioning, user handover and service documentation. Keep these duties separate from personnel-platform supply so they remain visible in the purchase orders.

## Complete an opening-to-access worksheet

Fill this from the operator's requirements, measured building and actual system proposals. Use the same reference level and opening coordinate system for every bidder.

| Purchase field | Entry to complete | Responsible source | What the entry changes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Usable opening | Clear width/height and movement envelope ___ | Operator and designer; issued opening datum ___ | Rejects systems that reduce the required operating envelope. |
| Open and moving geometry | Upper or lateral storage, travel limits and reserved zones ___ | Nominated door designer; plan/section revision ___ | Tests the complete architecture against the hangar layout. |
| Building interfaces | Upper/side supports, tracks, threshold and connections ___ | Structural and door designers; issued details ___ | Defines construction outside the door-component price. |
| Personnel tasks | Faces, offsets and stage-by-stage working positions ___ | Installer and surveyor; actual supporting-floor datum ___ | Defines the offered access envelope. |
| Component presentation | Object dimensions/masses and separate handling resources ___ | Door and handling specialists; delivered-component schedule ___ | Keeps door lifting outside an unqualified personnel-deck assumption. |
| Ground occupation | Approach, setup, traffic and occupied aircraft areas ___ | Facility planner; installation-stage plan ___ | Tests whether equipment can stand where the tasks require. |
| Completion | Checks, commissioning, handover visits and retained zones ___ | Door provider and programme lead; accepted completion schedule ___ | Prices access through the real handover. |
| Continuing service | Required service points, routes and replacement tasks ___ | Provider and asset owner; maintenance brief ___ | Completes the ownership comparison. |

An accepted system can still need a different personnel-access configuration at separate stages. Price those actual tasks. Do not reserve one large deck for an entire project merely because the door itself is large; buy the configurations that serve the defined workfaces.

## Keep component handling out of the personnel shortcut

The door supplier provides actual component information and the nominated specialists determine how those components reach their positions. A shared working deck is a candidate for people, tools and the specifically offered task load. Its presence in an estimate does not supply a method for holding or raising door assemblies.

Coordinate personnel positions with the separate material route. Include ground staging and the space needed by the handling equipment. Then assess both against the opening and the installation-stage building condition, including unfinished thresholds or areas reserved for other contractors.

## Two hypothetical door investments with missing space

**Mistake: only closed-door dimensions enter the tender.** A buyer chooses an opening size but omits the stored and moving door geometry. The offered arrangement later conflicts with hangar equipment or aircraft circulation. Require open, moving and closed layouts before choosing the architecture.

**Mistake: installation access ends before commissioning.** A contractor budgets personnel access for fixing components, then blocks the specialist's later service positions with fit-out and stored equipment. Include the actual commissioning visits and retained zones in the programme and access order.
