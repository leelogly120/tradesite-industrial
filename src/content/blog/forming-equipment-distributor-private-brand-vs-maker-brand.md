Retain the manufacturer's name when your distribution business cannot support the manufacturer role behind an own-brand machine. Choose own-brand equipment only with an accepted upstream agreement, assured technical evidence and resources to discharge that role for every supplied configuration. Customer recognition alone does not justify the extra obligation. Before placing the order, compare both arrangements using the same equipment, destination and sales programme, with the responsible entity named beside each duty. If both are workable, choose own branding only when its supported benefit justifies the extra continuing cost; otherwise retain the manufacturer's identity.

## The name belongs to a machine and a responsible entity

This decision concerns the forming machinery itself. It does not choose the brand on the roof panels it produces. Distribution is already the business model: the question is whether to sell the identified machine under its actual manufacturer's identity or under your own name or trademark with the resulting responsibilities.

Start with the exact sale presentation. Put the quotation, intended machine marking, declaration and instructions beside the proposed customer-facing name. A distributor's contact sticker and a machine placed on the market under that distributor's own brand need different analysis. Have the responsible adviser establish the role from the complete arrangement before the artwork is released.

The roller train below gives that branded promise a physical boundary. The controlled equipment record needs the actual forming configuration and tooling, including which provider approves a change. A common range name must not conceal different machine revisions.

## Use the law that applies to the actual placement

For an EU placement in November 2026, examine the [Machinery Directive 2006/42/EC](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX:02006L0042-20260530). Article 2(i) connects manufacturer status with design or manufacture, responsibility for conformity and placement under that person's name or trademark, or for its own use. Article 5 assigns pre-placement duties covering essential requirements, technical-file availability, instructions, conformity assessment, the declaration and CE marking. Its paragraph 3 requires the necessary means, or access to them, for the conformity process.

The [Machinery Regulation (EU) 2023/1230](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2023/1230/2026-07-27/eng) generally applies from 20 January 2027. Article 17 then expressly places manufacturer obligations on an importer or distributor putting an in-scope product on the market under its name or trademark. Do not treat that future article as already generally applicable in November 2026. Establish the relevant placement event and rules for the actual order with competent advice.

The purchase choice follows that role analysis. An upstream contract can fund and allocate work between businesses; it is not a way to erase the responsible entity's statutory duties. Retaining the manufacturer's identity also leaves the distributor's actual destination obligations to be addressed.

## Price access to evidence, changes and continuing action

An own-brand quotation needs more than permission to print a name. Agree the evidence access and technical resources needed by the responsible manufacturer, including a workable response when authorities request applicable records. Preserve legitimate confidentiality through agreed channels. Buying an own-brand machine does not make all upstream intellectual property public.

Have the technical owner identify who evaluates a proposed substitute motor, control component, tooling revision or changed combined assembly. Require a change record connecting the physical unit to the affected documentation and approval. Commercial approval to use a logo is separate from authority to release a technical change.

Use this worksheet for one defined opening range. Enter current document references and actual provider costs rather than assuming that every unit in a broad catalogue shares the same evidence.

| Purchase input and datum | Entry for both branding offers | Responsible owner | Branding decision |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Market and placement event | Destination ___; event/date ___; applicable regime ___ | Destination adviser and importer | Establishes the duties being priced |
| Manufacturer identity | Legal entity ___; proposed marking/declaration names ___ | Commercial and compliance leads | Tests whether the presentation matches the accepted role |
| Machine population | Former/lift/tool IDs ___; serial/configuration register ___ | Product technical lead | Defines which physical units the agreement covers |
| Evidence availability | Record/version ___; holder/access route ___; required response event ___ | Responsible manufacturer and upstream issuer | Rejects an own-brand role without workable evidence access |
| Instructions and declaration | Issuer ___; applicable version/language ___; delivery owner ___ | Documentation lead | Allocates the records accompanying the actual machine |
| Technical changes | Proposed change ___; reviewing party ___; release record ___ | Design authority and product lead | Prevents substitutions bypassing the accepted process |
| Continuing resources | Unit/customer register ___; correction scope/provider ___; accessible funding ___ | Service and finance leads | Tests whether continuing obligations have real resources |
| Complete commercial case | Range benefit ___; evidence/staff/service costs ___; period ___ | Management and finance | Approves the branding route only after duties are funded |

Record the proposed benefit as supported orders, retained customer relationships or another identified commercial result. Compare the cost over the same range and period. If the own-brand case depends on a future compliance team or evidence rights that have not been agreed, keep the manufacturer's identified presentation and rework the distribution offer.

## Dispatch must preserve the same identity

At dispatch, a complete machine needs the same identity in the buyer's records and the physical shipment. The wrapped line below makes that handover tangible. Connect the order's unit identity to the packing list and accompanying documentation. Agree who hands those records to the onward customer and retains the controlled version after dispatch.

![A blue machine line partly wrapped in clear film stands on a workshop floor with diagonal stripes at its near end.](/images/equipment/buyer-20261102-wrapped-former.webp)
*Carry the same unit and document identities from the purchase order into the onward equipment sale.*

Plan for the upstream relationship ending. The distributor's customer commitments and the responsible manufacturer's obligations do not disappear with an expired marketing agreement. Price continuity of required records, change information and customer communication in the opening transaction. Keep private customer and technical records in the agreed business channel.

## Two hypothetical branding mistakes

**Mistake (hypothetical): the own-brand order buys artwork but no evidence route.** A distributor approves its name on a forming line and markets itself as the responsible manufacturer. Its upstream purchase includes only a brochure, leaving no agreed route to the technical records its role requires. Keep the order at the identified manufacturer's arrangement until competent review and a funded evidence agreement make the chosen role workable.

**Mistake (hypothetical): a replacement component disappears from the branded record.** The supplier substitutes the specified motor after the distributor releases its document set. Sales continues using the old configuration identity. Hold the affected release, obtain the responsible technical review and update the linked unit records before selling the changed configuration.
