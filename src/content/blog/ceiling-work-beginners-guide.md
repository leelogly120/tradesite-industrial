# Ceiling Work for Beginners: Everything You Need to Know

Starting in ceiling work can feel overwhelming. Equipment options, safety requirements, project management—there's a lot to learn. This guide covers everything you need to know to get started.

## What is Ceiling Work?

Ceiling work involves installing, maintaining, and repairing ceiling systems in buildings. This includes:

- Acoustic panels
- Lighting systems
- HVAC components
- Fire suppression systems
- Structural elements
- Decorative finishes

## Essential Equipment

### Aerial Platforms

**Types:**

| Type | Best For | Cost Range |
|------|----------|------------|
| Scissor lift | Low height, small areas | $100-200/day |
| Boom lift | Medium height, moderate areas | $200-400/day |
| Pick & carry | Large areas, efficiency | $250-400/day |
| Spider lift | Tight spaces, high reach | $300-500/day |

**Recommendation for beginners:**
Start with [pick & carry platforms](/products/) for large area coverage—they're faster and safer than alternatives.

### Hand Tools

**Essential toolkit:**
- Cordless drill
- Impact driver
- Level (2m and 4m)
- Tape measure
- Chalk line
- Safety knife
- Pliers and wrenches

### Safety Equipment

**Required PPE:**
- Hard hat
- Safety glasses
- High-vis vest
- Safety boots
- Gloves
- Harness (when required)

## Safety Basics

### The Golden Rules

1. **Never work without training**
2. **Always inspect equipment before use**
3. **Wear all required PPE**
4. **Tether all tools at height**
5. **Communicate with your team**

### Common Hazards

| Hazard | Prevention |
|--------|------------|
| Falls | Guardrails, harnesses |
| Dropped objects | Tool tethering |
| Electrical | Lockout/tagout |
| Struck-by | Barriers, communication |
| Material handling | Proper techniques |

## Project Planning

### Before Starting

1. **Assess the site** — What are the requirements?
2. **Select equipment** — Match to your needs
3. **Plan the work** — Sequence and schedule
4. **Order materials** — Ensure availability
5. **Brief the team** — Safety and procedures

### During the Project

1. **Daily planning** — What's happening today?
2. **Progress tracking** — Are we on schedule?
3. **Quality checks** — Is the work meeting standards?
4. **Safety monitoring** — Any issues or concerns?
5. **Communication** — Keep everyone informed
