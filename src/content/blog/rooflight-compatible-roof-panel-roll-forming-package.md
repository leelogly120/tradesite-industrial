Buy a coordinated metal-panel and specialist rooflight package when the project combines profiled metal sheets with in-plane rooflights. Release the metal-panel tooling against the agreed interface drawings and matched samples. Keep rooflight manufacture with the identified specialist supplier. A roof-panel roll former is a metal-sheet production purchase, not a substitute for a translucent rooflight manufacturing system.

If the designed rooflight sits on a separate kerb, coordinate the opening and surrounding metal details against that arrangement. Do not force an in-plane profile-matching solution onto a kerb-mounted design. The roof designer selects the assembly; the equipment buyer uses that decision to define the metal product and production schedule.

## Start with the rooflight arrangement, not a matching colour

Mark the rooflights on the roof plan and identify the relevant assembly type. For in-plane profiled rooflights, collect the metal section, rooflight section, lap direction, support arrangement and the specialist's joint details. For a kerb arrangement, collect the opening, kerb and flashing details that define the surrounding metal sheets.

[Brett Martin's site-assembled rooflight range](https://www.brettmartin.com/daylight-systems/our-products/product/site-assembled-rooflight-systems) describes profiled GRP products supplied to match metal cladding. That is a separate specialist product category. Its catalogue does not establish compatibility with a proposed new ARCLIFT metal-panel section; the selected products need their own matched documentation.

## Give the metal line one controlled product definition

The former proposal needs a metal-panel drawing rather than an instruction to “match a rooflight.” Freeze the critical section dimensions, cover-width datum, lap geometry, material specification, coating and acceptance method. Name the party controlling each drawing and the document that resolves conflicts between the metal and rooflight suppliers.

When a specialist rooflight product is already specified, use its controlled interface information during the metal tooling review. When the metal section is fixed first, select a specialist supply route with documented compatibility. If the two selected products cannot be coordinated, change the design or product selection before ordering tooling. Do not leave the mismatch for the installation crew.

The coordination decision does not replace the building's structural, fire, thermal or weathering design. Obtain the assembly requirements from the responsible project designer. The manufacturing brief should reproduce the relevant product and interface requirements without inventing a universal rooflight spacing or performance class.

## Keep the interface register beside the panel schedule

| Required input | Fillable project record and datum | Owner | Production or supply decision |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Assembly type | In-plane/kerb arrangement ___; roof detail revision ___ | Roof designer | Determines whether section matching or opening/flashing coordination controls |
| Metal section | Drawing ___; cover datum ___; side-lap orientation ___ | Metal product designer | Defines the roll-forming tooling target |
| Specialist product | Rooflight supplier/product ___; controlled section revision ___ | Rooflight supplier | Identifies the actual mating product |
| Joint detail | Side/end joint references ___; nominated support lines ___ | Roof system designer | Fixes metal-panel ends and adjoining details |
| Panel schedule | Roof zone ___; piece mark ___; ordered length datum ___ | Roofing contractor | Converts the roof layout into labelled metal production orders |
| Samples | Metal ID ___; rooflight ID ___; comparison record ___ | Quality lead with both suppliers | Links the interface decision to identifiable physical products |
| Release timing | Design release ___; metal release ___; specialist delivery ___ | Project planner | Prevents metal production running ahead of an unresolved assembly change |
| Site handover | Receiving area ___; packaging groups ___; replacement-piece route ___ | Installation and logistics leads | Keeps mixed products together by installation zone |

Record dimensions from the drawing's stated datum. For example, a cut length taken from a piece schedule and a support-to-support distance are not automatically the same quantity. Resolve the schedule with the designer, then send the same definition to the former supplier and the installation team.

## Produce by roof zone and preserve piece identity

A rooflight layout can split an otherwise repetitive metal-sheet order into several lengths and joint positions. Use piece marks tied to the roof plan. Identify which panels stop at openings, which continue through a zone, and which require separate bought-in details. Release manufacturing only against the current approved schedule.

Keep that distinction in the offer: ARCLIFT's forming enquiry concerns the metal line and any nominated lifting arrangement; the rooflight product and its performance documentation belong to the specialist supply scope.

If elevated forming is selected, coordinate the metal output sequence with the contractor's receiving and installation plan. Separate production equipment from roof access and specialist handling. The [roof-panel handover-zone guide](/blog/elevated-roll-forming-roof-panel-handover-zones/) helps define that site interface without treating the forming lift as personnel access.

## Avoid two expensive coordination failures

**Hypothetical failure case: the metal profile is changed after the rooflights are ordered.** The buyer substitutes a visually similar roll-former section to reduce equipment cost. Existing rooflight documents refer to the earlier section, so the previously agreed joint arrangement is no longer the ordered assembly. Run the product substitution through the roof designer and both supply scopes before changing tooling.

**Hypothetical failure case: a roof opening becomes a production instruction without piece marks.** A planner tells the factory to supply “shorter sheets near the lights” while the cutting list still shows continuous lengths. Wrong pieces arrive together and the installation crew must sort a design conflict on site. Issue a controlled zone-and-piece schedule with one length datum and a named release owner.

## Quote the defined metal scope and the coordinated interfaces

Attach these eight documents to the metal-line RFQ:

1. The roof plan identifying rooflights and metal production zones.
2. The controlled metal profile and material specification.
3. The nominated rooflight product and specialist interface drawings.
4. Joint, support, opening and flashing details relevant to the metal supply.
5. The marked panel cutting and labelling schedule.
6. The identified sample and interface acceptance record.
7. The production location, receiving route and project release sequence.
8. A supply matrix naming the metal, rooflight, design and installation parties.
