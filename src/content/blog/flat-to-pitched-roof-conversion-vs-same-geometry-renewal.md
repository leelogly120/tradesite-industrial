Choose a flat-to-pitched metal conversion when the owner wants a different roof form or drainage arrangement and the design team has a viable support, discharge and building-services solution. Renew on the existing geometry when it can meet the owner's brief with the specified repairs and covering. Buy forming equipment against the metal panels you will actually supply on the selected route.

An estimator looking at a low-slope building can price replacement covering from its plan area. That measure misses what a pitch conversion buys. A new ridge changes the direction of water, the height of the roof against adjacent walls and the lengths of the sheets. The contractor may also take on new framing and alterations to rooftop plant. An owner comparing only the price per unit of covering is comparing different completed buildings.

## Follow one drop of water and one piece of plant

Start at the existing high point and follow the designer's proposed water path to its discharge. Identify the receiving gutter, outlet or other drainage connection and the trade that changes it. An owner seeking to move water away from a sensitive interior drain may value that change even if it needs external works. Where an existing drainage layout can be renewed in place, a change in roof form needs its own justification.

Now pick a piece of equipment that must stay in service. Show its curb, replacement access and discharge or intake clearances on both roof sections. A proposal that puts a new framing zone around the plant must name who designs, alters and returns those connections to service. If access to the equipment is part of the owner's continuing obligation, include that access in the comparison.

*Give each proposed panel run an origin and a destination. A new ridge can divide a former run or send its water to a different edge.*

## The price of changing planes

Manufacturers offer engineered retrofit framing that creates a new metal roof plane over an existing building. McElroy calls for inspection of the existing deck and structural supports ahead of its framing design. MBCI also describes its framing, roof system, insulation and ventilation as related design choices. Use these sources to identify separate tender responsibilities. The design team chooses the system for the building in front of it. [McElroy retrofit framing](https://www.mcelroymetal.com/metal-roofing/retrofit-and-recover/low-slope-to-steep-slope) and [MBCI NuRoof](https://www.mbci.com/products/nuroof/)

For the conversion offer, request a complete section from the existing structure to the new weather surface. Ask for the interfaces at high walls, penetrations, eaves, changes of plane and adjoining roofs. Identify existing materials retained, materials removed and the owner of any repairs discovered during opening. The engineer's work on the supports and the roofer's work on the weathering plane should meet at an identified drawing revision.

For same-geometry renewal, keep the required owner outcome visible. Price surveys, local substrate work, new weathering, drainage repairs, plant interfaces and interruptions. If a designer specifies a membrane assembly, the roofing contractor's metal-forming purchase can fall to trims or disappear. If the same-geometry option uses a compatible metal system, price those exact runs. A metal sheet quotation for every square metre on both options can steer the award toward an irrelevant machine.

## Use the panel takeoff to size the machine booking

A new pitch can change the number of separate slopes even where the building footprint stays the same. Price full runs, short infill pieces and bought flashings separately. A small group of long panels can justify a production position for one elevation while the rest arrive finished. Match the panel supplier's delivery batches to the framing handovers. Long runs released late in the job do little to justify booking a machine throughout the earlier structural work.

## Two illustrative purchasing mistakes

**Mistake 1 — Choosing a new ridge on sheet price alone.** A bidder prices the weathering skin and leaves the services subcontractor to find that an intake sits in the space assigned to new framing. Obtain a coordinated section and an alteration price. Carry that figure into the roof award before placing the panel order.

**Mistake 2 — Booking a lift from the old eaves measurement.** A contractor measures the present roof edge from the pavement, then awards a forming lift for a conversion with a new receiving edge behind an extended gutter. Ask the surveyor for the new edge and offset from the actual support pad. Price the alternative position or a different sheet-reception package if the original position falls outside the offered arrangement.

## RFQ package after the owner selects the geometry

- Existing survey with edges, falls, adjacent buildings and drainage destinations.
- Owner's brief for retained operations and the requested change in drainage or roof form.
- Both design sections with structural review scope and construction-stage support requirements.
- Plant, access and penetration schedule showing items to retain, alter or relocate.
- Accepted roof-system drawing with panel material, profile, lengths and quantities.
- Programme separating framing work, release of each roof plane and roofing handover.
- Surveyed lift positions, ground support arrangements and proposed receiving points.
- Scope schedule separating structure, services, sheet supply, forming and installation.
