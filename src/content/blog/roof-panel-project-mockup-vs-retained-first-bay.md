Order a separate installed roof mockup when the contract requires it, specified testing requires disturbing the assembly, or the owner needs a reference before the permanent roof is available. Use a retained first bay only when the contract permits that approach, it represents the required details and formal acceptance precedes repetitive production.

## Distinguish a sample, a production trial and an installed benchmark

A short panel sample can demonstrate an appearance or section. A machine trial can establish whether particular tooling produces the specified piece. A project mockup can demonstrate workmanship, coordination, assembly of agreed details and the appearance of the finished roof. These deliverables answer different questions. Read the mockup clause, drawings, testing section and acceptance instructions together. Record what the owner expects to see, whether any testing is required and whether the contract permits retaining the assembly as permanent work.

*Roof sheets meet uncovered framing. The project reviewer should specify the real junctions that the installed benchmark must represent.*

For example, Delaware's transportation department issued a project specification requiring a roof and eave mockup with several related components. That specification allowed approved mockups to become part of the completed work subject to conditions. Such contract wording supports an in-place option on that project. It does not authorize substituting a first production bay on another contract. Obtain the client's written selection, location, acceptance criteria and permitted final disposition for this project. ## Price the same representative details under both routes

Make a list of the features that influence approval: roof section, support, underlayment, clips, joints, eave, fascia, interfaces and appearance where specified. An independent mockup requires support, materials, installation, access for review, retention for comparison and eventual removal or agreed transfer. A retained bay can reduce duplicate materials, but only if the permanent structure is ready, contains the relevant details and satisfies all ordinary roof requirements. Exclude a plain field bay if the contract requires the benchmark to include an eave and a change of plane.

Work backwards from the first serial panel release. A separate mockup can often start before the full roof workface becomes available if its location and materials are ready. It can also require an additional drawing release and procurement of small quantities that differ from the main order. A retained bay saves rebuilding only after it has passed the required review and remains undisturbed in the condition the contract accepts. Price correction, repeat review, temporary protection and restoration if the first attempt needs changes.

## Decide who can release the rest of the order

Ask the buyer's commercial team to record the exact written decision that allows production to resume. If the reviewer accepts workmanship only, refer design deviations to the authorized designer. If the owner signs off a colour sample only, keep the installation detail review open. Separate completed observations, agreed corrections, deviations requiring design authorization and the final release for the repeated work. If materials for the whole roof must be ordered before appearance approval, ask whether unformed coil or another reversible commitment can preserve supply while limiting the cost of a change.

**Mistake: The first bay lacks the detail under review.** A contractor proposes retaining a plain permanent bay. The specification calls for a roof edge, fascia and associated attachments in the benchmark. The bay is complete but the review cannot close. Locate a representative permanent section with written approval or commission the specified separate mockup before releasing the field order.

**Mistake: Installation continues while acceptance is still open.** A supplier assumes the architect's site visit amounted to release and forms every remaining panel. The approved record later requires a revised detail. Set a named hold point, reserve time for review and correction, and restrict advance production to the agreed quantities until the documented release.

Select the independent assembly where early review, required testing, repeat access or the contract makes it necessary. Retain the first bay when it represents the real details, can remain compliant and the contract accepts that disposition. Count saved materials only once and keep ordinary permanent roofing work in both prices. The best offer is the one that obtains a usable decision on the production roof before irreversible commitments accumulate.

## Eight entries for the installed roof mockup RFQ

1. Attach the contract's mockup, test and retention clauses.
2. Identify roof section, detail drawings, components and revisions.
3. Mark the separate location and any eligible permanent bay.
4. List materials, support, adjoining work and access suppliers.
5. State review criteria, witness parties and written release roles.
6. Provide testing, correction, reinspection and protection requirements.
7. Give serial production dates and the quantity held for approval.
8. Request separate prices for removal, retention and final handover.
