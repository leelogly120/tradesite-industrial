A project-nominated former is a requirement to answer, not simply a suggestion for the bidder's catalogue search. If a supplier proposes another machine, procurement needs a separate alternative response showing what changes. The useful output is a comparison against the buyer's actual brief, with equipment, material and local integration assumptions kept visible. A lower price for another former should not silently replace the nominated purchase.

This differs from the [owned-former retrofit guide](/blog/existing-roof-panel-former-lift-retrofit-purchase/), which asks which assets an owner wants to retain. Here the project may nominate equipment before any purchase. ## State what nominated means in this enquiry

### Identify the machine with the records available

Give the nominated former's actual identifier and the records the project can provide. If it is not yet supplied, distinguish proposed documentation from an inspected asset. A model name alone may leave the assembly, cutter, cabinet or auxiliary equipment unclear. Ask the buyer's requirement owner what configuration is intended and which information remains unavailable. The lifting enquiry needs to concern an identifiable arrangement rather than a machine name that different bidders interpret differently. Keep the record status clear without manufacturing missing dimensions, mass information or interface details.

### State whether alternatives are permitted

Ask the project owner to say whether the nominated machine is mandatory, a reference for comparison or a base requirement with separately permitted alternatives. Procurement should not decide that meaning from the presence of a brand or model in a drawing note. The actual tender or enquiry owner must define the requested response. This article supplies no public-procurement rule or legal interpretation. Its practical purpose is to prevent an alternative from being mixed into the base answer when the buyer expected a clear response against the nominated equipment.

### Keep the required panel task constant

Identify the profile, material and output requirements that the nomination is intended to address. A supplier proposing another machine should respond to that same product basis or mark its departures. If the nomination itself has unresolved product scope, make the gap explicit. A machine alternative can be commercially interesting without being established as technically equivalent. The [line-specification guide](/blog/roll-forming-line-specification-long-span-roof-panels/) develops the product and assembly brief; this tender comparison uses that common basis to expose exactly what a bidder proposes to change.

## Ask bidders to separate the base response and alternative

### Request a direct answer about the nominated assembly

Ask the bidder to identify the supply it proposes around the nominated former, the information it needs and any scope it excludes. An answer may remain preliminary when the machine records are incomplete. Keep that qualification visible rather than forcing a compatibility statement. The buyer should know whether the bidder has addressed the base request, declined it or offered only an alternative. Those are different responses. A catalogue recommendation for another line should not be presented as a complete answer to a lift and integration enquiry around the nominated equipment.

### Require an alternative to name its actual differences

For any permitted alternative, request its equipment identity and a marked comparison against the common product and assembly brief. Ask which included cutting, feed or control functions differ and which information is still proposed. Do not assume that two machines making a similar panel create the same lift interface. Their actual physical records belong with the equipment reviewers. Procurement needs the changed boundary and responsible response, not a homemade judgement that one set of dimensions or a similar photograph proves interchangeability.

### Make local-work assumptions explicit

HSE's <a href="https://www.hse.gov.uk/work-equipment-machinery/are-you.htm" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">equipment supply-role guidance</a> distinguishes manufacturers, suppliers, importers and users in Great Britain. That context supports asking who supplies the actual machine information and connecting scope in each route. It does not determine tender compliance, legal roles at another destination or technical equivalence between the nominated and alternative former.

A bidder may assume that another party supplies connecting items, destination work or a separate integration service. Ask it to identify those assumptions for both routes. The alternative may move work outside the equipment offer rather than remove it. Keep the same commercial completion boundary in view so the buyer can see which party must provide the remaining function. This is useful even when one coordinator presents both offers: a common quotation does not eliminate the technical and commercial interfaces inside it or establish that every site activity is included.

## Compare changed assumptions before prices

### Work through a hypothetical alternative bid

Imagine the project nominates Former A with an identified product brief. Bidder One responds to A but requires additional machine-side records. Bidder Two offers Former B and excludes the destination integration assumed in its summary price. These are hypothetical replies, not ARCLIFT offers. The first has a specific information dependency; the second proposes a different asset and completion boundary. The buyer should obtain the missing records and the alternative's changed-scope response before ranking prices. Neither incomplete documentation nor a lower alternative price determines the technical or commercial answer by itself.

### Use a base-versus-alternative worksheet

| Comparison item | Nominated-machine response | Separate alternative response |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Equipment identity | Actual nominated assembly addressed | Proposed machine and included assemblies |
| Product requirement | Common profile and material basis | Marked departures or same stated basis |
| Physical interface | Records available and missing | Alternative's own geometry and support records |
| Supplied integration | Included items and services | Changed included and excluded work |
| Destination resources | Explicit buyer or local-provider assumptions | Differences from the base response |
| Remaining decisions | Named information or project dependency | Permission to consider and assess the alternative |

The trade-off concerns changed equipment and retained obligations. A comparison that keeps only equipment prices hides the reason the offers differ. Unknown costs should remain unknown rather than become invented adjustment figures.

### Distinguish permission to consider from acceptance

Allowing alternatives opens a procurement discussion; it does not accept the proposed machine or its product. Ask the responsible project party to decide the stated departures and ask the equipment parties to address the actual configuration. Keep any later decision connected to the marked alternative and revision. A general email saying alternative acceptable can otherwise leave the participants unsure which machine, material or local-work assumption was considered. This article does not define tender compliance or contract formation; it helps the buyer request a sufficiently specific response for its own decision process.

## Issue a nominated-machine response sheet

### Include six inputs for a comparable tender reply

- Identify the nominated former, intended assemblies and actual machine records available to bidders.
- State the enquiry owner's meaning of nomination and whether separately identified alternatives are permitted.
- Attach the common profile, material and output requirements and ask for every departure to be marked.
- Request included integration, local-work assumptions and missing information separately for the base and alternative.
- Show outlet height, roof-zone geometry, site access and ground information for the separate lifting assessment.
- Include destination documentation, transport boundary and power/control interfaces for each proposed arrangement.

The return should let a reviewer locate the exact changed purchase without searching through unrelated product brochures. Ask the bidder to preserve the base question even if it can only offer an alternative.

### Carry the decision into the final equipment scope

Once a route is selected for further review, retain its actual machine and product references in the next enquiry. Do not leave the nominated model in the heading while substituting another arrangement in the attachments. Update the scope presented to the lift, material and destination reviewers so they respond to the same proposal. The [price-scope guide](/blog/roll-forming-lift-price-quotation-cost-scope/) supports the commercial comparison once those boundaries are clear. This page's added value is the explicit base-versus-alternative decision, rather than another generic equipment checklist.

<!-- audit-section: limitations-not-fit -->
### Recognise a bid that may not fit the buyer's brief

A response may not fit when alternatives are outside the requested scope, the proposed product differs from a mandatory requirement or important destination work is excluded without disclosure. No technical or legal equivalence follows from this worksheet. It provides no mounting design, machine approval or tender-law conclusion. Ask the actual enquiry owner and responsible specialists to address the identified differences. An attractive machine photograph or a familiar model label cannot supply the missing product and integration answer.

## FAQ

#### Does nominated always mean mandatory?

Ask the actual enquiry owner to define it. The word can leave the expected response unclear. This article supplies no legal interpretation; it recommends making the base requirement and permitted alternative scope explicit.

#### Can a bidder offer another former?

That depends on the actual enquiry. Where alternatives are permitted, request a separate marked response showing equipment, product and local-work differences. Do not mix it silently into the nominated-machine price comparison.

#### Does similar output establish lift compatibility?

No. The actual machine arrangement needs its own interface information and review. Matching a product description is only part of the equipment question and cannot replace geometry, support, service and assembly records.

#### Is this the same as retaining an owned former?

No. Retention starts with an owner's asset preference and inventory. A nomination is a requirement in the project enquiry, which may concern equipment not yet purchased. Keep that different decision visible.
