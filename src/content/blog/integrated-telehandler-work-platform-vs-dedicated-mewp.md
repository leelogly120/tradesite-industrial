**Choose an integrated telehandler personnel platform when the machine manufacturer documents the exact carrier, attachment, controls and operating configuration, and the carrier is free through the required work periods. Buy or hire a dedicated mobile elevating work platform (MEWP) when routine personnel work overlaps material handling or the proposed combination lacks an accepted personnel-lifting configuration.** An inexpensive cage priced without the rest of the system is a poor starting point for a production schedule.

A material-handling fleet owner sees an attractive saving: one engine and chassis can serve two trades. The key figure is the number of shifts when the carrier is actually available to people working at height. Where agreed deliveries occupy that carrier, it is unavailable for the personnel shift. Start with the agreed tasks and their dates before comparing attachment prices.

## What makes the attachment a genuine personnel-work option?

Ask for traceable carrier and attachment designations and their compatible configurations. Obtain the manufacturer's personnel-use instructions, control arrangement, load information and operating envelope for that combination. Identify who supplies, configures and checks the system before personnel use. If an existing telehandler has an unknown serial-specific configuration, budget a documented assessment before assigning work. Stop pricing that branch if the manufacturer or responsible supplier cannot identify a permitted combination.

The UK Health and Safety Executive addresses a different class in its [guidance on non-integrated platforms on forklift trucks](https://www.hse.gov.uk/pubns/pm28.htm). It limits their use to exceptional circumstances in the situation covered by that guidance. An ordinary fork-mounted cage therefore offers no sound baseline for planned repeat ceiling installation. Check the rules governing the actual destination and work, alongside the instructions for the specific equipment. Manufacturer integration and local permission are separate questions.

A dedicated MEWP avoids competing use of the carrier, but it still needs to fit the task. Obtain the crew space, permitted load distribution, support arrangement, travel route, rescue provision and operator requirements for the exact model. A ceiling contractor fitting long runs can value shared deck space. A separate point beyond a fixed obstruction can require a specialist boom proposal. Count work only after reviewing its position.

## Rebuild the saving around a real working week

Take a week that includes both cladding deliveries and ceiling work. Allocate the actual start and finish times to a common carrier calendar. For every clash, identify an accepted response: move a delivery, supply another material handler, hire a personnel unit, or change the installation sequence. Give a rescheduled delivery a cost only if the supplier and receiving crew accept the changed slot. The cheapest dedicated attachment can lose its advantage before anyone accounts for changeovers.

For the dedicated option, price delivery, collection, scheduled attendance and time waiting for a released workface. Apportion shared fixed costs consistently. An owned carrier already committed to a job has an opportunity cost if the attachment removes it from material work. Conversely, do not allocate the full purchase price of a carrier already needed under both plans. Compare additional cash costs and displaced productive work on the same schedule.

## Two mistakes in a combined-carrier quotation

**Mistake one — accepting the bare attachment price.** A quotation names a basket, but the fleet machine lacks the documented configuration needed by that attachment. The owner has budgeted neither an eligible carrier nor commissioning. Obtain a complete traceable offer, or procure the correct personnel machine for these shifts.

**Mistake two — promising the unloading crew the same carrier.** Ceiling installers occupy it at the only accepted delivery slot. Idle installers, waiting freight or emergency hire then consume the apparent saving. Resolve the clash against the actual weekly programme before promising either trade an available machine.
