Choose a conductor-rail package for a crane axis when the crane designer accepts its electrical duty, collector path and environment, especially where a proposed festoon's loops or storage stack cannot fit. Choose a festoon package when the accepted moving cable or hose bundle and its travel, hanging envelope and parked stack fit the installation. Specify different systems on bridge travel and trolley travel when those axes have different requirements. Buy personnel access against the resulting installation positions, not the crane's advertised lifting capacity.

This comparison is about supplying a moving machine. It differs from choosing fixed building busbar trunking instead of a cable-tray distribution route. It also differs from deciding whether a crane repair should happen on site or in a workshop.

## Split the quotation by movement axis

Draw the fixed supply, bridge movement and trolley movement separately. Mark the physical travel limits and where installers must reach supports, feeds and connections. For each axis, the crane designer defines the electrical and mechanical duty; the system supplier identifies the equipment and occupied envelope it is actually offering.

[Conductix-Wampfler's product overview](https://www.conductix.com/products) distinguishes conductor rails from festoon systems, whose trolleys carry cables or hoses on tracks. Its [overhead bridge-crane reference](https://www.conductix.com/reference-reports/overhead-bridge-crane) shows conductor rails for main power combined with a C-rail festoon for the hoist. That is an example of system architecture, not an ARCLIFT installation or a universal selection rule.

A combined architecture requires an interface schedule. Establish which vendor supplies the fixed feed, collector or moving bundle, support brackets and end connections. Allocate design, installation and acceptance rather than leaving each bidder to price a different portion of the same axis.

## Compare envelopes before comparing purchase totals

The festoon alternative needs the proposed cable package, its supported movement and the actual loop and stack envelopes. The conductor-rail alternative needs the actual rail arrangement, collector travel and feed/access positions. Treat both as engineered systems with documents, not interchangeable lengths of material.

Use the following worksheet for each axis. A value measured from a column face is not directly comparable with one measured from the runway centreline; put the common datum on the drawing.

| Axis decision input | Project entry and evidence | Owner and award decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Consumer duty | Power, controls and any hose or other moving services ___ | Crane designer defines what the axis must carry |
| Travel geometry | Axis length, end positions and shared drawing datum ___ | Surveyor and crane designer establish actual movement |
| Festoon package | Cable schedule, loop envelope and parked-stack drawing ___ | Festoon supplier proves the offered package fits |
| Rail package | Rail/collector arrangement, feeds and maintenance positions ___ | Rail supplier proves the offered package fits |
| Environment | Indoor/outdoor conditions, contamination and process restrictions ___ | Plant engineer sets the system's service conditions |
| Crew positions | Installation and later service points relative to the supporting floor ___ | Installation lead defines personnel-access duty |
| Released work window | Isolation authority, crane standstill boundary and bay-release schedule ___ | Plant coordinator defines accessible work periods |
| Complete cost | Systems, interfaces, installation, access and acceptance offers ___ | Buyer compares one commissioned scope per axis |

Remove an alternative that fails its accepted duty or geometric envelope before ranking cost. If both qualify, compare the complete installed and accepted axis. Do not make an unpriced support bracket or a missing termination disappear inside the access-equipment budget.

## Access follows the installation drawing

A broad floor-supported deck belongs on the shortlist when repeated installation points are reachable from an accepted work strip. Where an obstruction prevents that configuration reaching a point, allocate separate suitable access rather than assuming the deck has unquoted outreach. Count those separate positions in the same installation comparison.

Keep personnel access separate from lifting and supporting electrical components. The project plan identifies the material-handling equipment, loads, attachment arrangements and people responsible. Specify the personnel platform's task capacity separately from the crane's rating, and allocate cable reels and suspended rail lengths to the accepted material-handling arrangement.

Electrical isolation and the crane's secured condition are controlled by the responsible project professionals under the applicable site rules. Give each contractor the actual released work window and record the scope it accepts within that period.

## Include the floor route in every access offer

An access machine delivered to the gate still needs a released route and suitable support at the work strip. The hall image below shows rough ground, obstructions and other equipment sharing the building. It illustrates why “indoor installation” is an incomplete equipment brief.

![An unfinished hall has a rough stone-covered route, an orange machine at the left and a distant raised red platform.](/images/equipment/buyer-20261102-hall-ground-route.webp)
*Survey the complete route and supporting locations before committing the electrical contractor's access dates.*

Price receiving, travel between work strips, material staging, installation occupation and collection. Record the plant's actual production restrictions. A cheaper supply system does not remain cheaper if its offered installation arrangement needs a longer shutdown or an additional access package that has been excluded from the comparison.

## Two errors that create incomplete crane packages

**Mistake: one choice is imposed on every axis.** A hypothetical buyer chooses conductor rails for the runway and assumes the same arrangement automatically covers the trolley's moving services. The trolley duty includes a different service bundle. Require an axis-by-axis design and compare the complete accepted combinations before placing the order.

**Mistake: the festoon is priced without its parked stack.** Another quotation allocates cable travel but no space for the collected trolleys at the end position. The apparent material saving is based on an arrangement that does not fit. Obtain the supplier's loop and stack drawing on the actual crane layout and discard the non-fitting offer.
