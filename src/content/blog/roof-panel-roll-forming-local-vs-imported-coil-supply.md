Choose local coil when it meets the released product specification and order dates at a complete route cost no higher than the accepted import alternative. Import when its representative material passes the product trial, the transport and stock plan serves the order dates, and it supplies required material unavailable locally or a documented complete-cost advantage. Include inspection and replacement responsibilities before comparing the two routes.

This choice precedes a useful roll-former quotation. The machine provider needs the nominated material specification and representative samples. Treat a catalogue thickness class as an initial screen, then use the actual profile, material properties and representative trial to define the forming scope.

## Keep material identity the same in both quotations

Issue one controlled requirement covering grade, thickness, coating, width, surface condition and the properties relevant to the specified product. Give the material owner responsibility for accepting proposed alternatives. Do not compare a local quote against one specification with an import quote against a loosely similar commercial description.

Connect the offered material to actual product identity and lot records. [SSAB's GreenCoat Pural BT product page](https://www.ssab.com/en/brands-and-products/greencoat/products/pural-bt) describes product and production-date identification for its own material. This illustrates the traceability principle. Use the actual supplier's documentation for the ordered coil, with the product and lot identities belonging to that delivery.

Keep sourcing and forming decisions separate. If material changes, apply the agreed product and process review before treating the previous line trial as representative. Changing a grade or coating to obtain a cheaper supply route changes the input, even when the coil fits the same stand.

## Build a route cost that ends at accepted production

Use the same order quantity and period for each route. Record the quoted material, transport, handling, inspection, storage, funding and replacement responsibilities as applicable. Have the relevant procurement, logistics and finance specialists establish the actual costs and destination requirements. Use their current destination-specific freight, tax and currency inputs in the comparison.

Calculate the cost against accepted output from the representative order. Keep trial loss, rejected material and usable remnants visible. A lower delivered material invoice does not help when the route repeatedly supplies material outside the accepted requirement or ties up stock the business cannot use.

The comparison also needs an interruption case. Name how an actual order is served if a shipment or lot is unavailable: an approved second source, accepted stock or an identified finished-panel supplier. Select that backup against the same released product and acceptance requirements before treating it as available order cover.

## Let the supply route shape the forming brief

Send the actual input range and representative material to the forming provider. Identify which materials are routine and which are exceptional. Ask for the product trial and technical schedule covering the intended range, rather than assuming one successful sample approves every future supply substitution.

For mobile or elevated forming, define the job material package separately. Its coil presentation, handling and equipment interfaces must match the actual proposed arrangement. A factory's material purchasing unit is not automatically the unit delivered to a site former or lift.

## Two hypothetical sourcing failures

**Failure case: low import price conceals a different input.** The business compares commercial material names but the offered grade or coating differs from the trial material. Issue one specification and obtain an accepted representative trial before committing the material route and forming scope.

**Failure case: a local backup exists only in conversation.** A delayed shipment leaves orders uncovered because the alternative source has no accepted matching material or available quantity. Price and document the actual interruption route before using it to justify the import plan.

## Give the forming provider a real input package

Attach eight items to the RFQ:

1. Controlled panel drawings and acceptance requirements.
2. Complete nominated material specifications and routine/exceptional ranges.
3. Offered producer, product and coil/lot identification documents.
4. Representative samples and existing trial results.
5. Actual order quantities, lengths, colours and release periods.
6. Coil presentation, storage and material-handling scope.
7. Production location and any site/lift interface requirements.
8. Destination utilities, existing equipment and supplied-equipment boundary.
