# Crawler Lift vs Scaffolding: The Real Cost Comparison

I've seen too many contractors choose scaffolding because it's "cheaper." Then they're shocked when the total project cost ends up higher. After analyzing 20 projects, here are the real numbers.

## The False Economy of Scaffolding

### What People Think

" scaffolding is cheap. We can rent it for $2,000/week."

### What Actually Happens

**Hidden costs that add up:**

| Hidden Cost | Amount | Why It Happens |
|-------------|--------|----------------|
| Erection labor | $2,400 | 3 days × 4 workers |
| Dismantling labor | $1,600 | 2 days × 4 workers |
| Material damage | $800 | 10% of materials |
| Storage between projects | $500 | Warehouse space |
| Project delays | $3,000 | Weather, setup issues |
| **Total hidden costs** | **$8,300** | |

**Total scaffolding cost: $10,300 for 4 weeks**

**The "cheap" option costs $2,575/week when you include everything.**

## The Real Cost of Crawler Lifts

### What People Think

"Crawler lifts are expensive. $5,000/week is too much."

### What Actually Happens

| Cost Item | Amount | Why It's Worth It |
|-----------|--------|-------------------|
| Equipment rental | $5,000 | No setup/teardown costs |
| Operator (1 person) | $4,000 | Fewer workers needed |
| Fuel/maintenance | $500 | Efficient operation |
| **Total cost** | **$9,500 for 4 weeks** | |

**The crawler lift costs $2,375/week — actually cheaper than scaffolding.**

## The Break-Even Analysis

### When Scaffolding Wins

Scaffolding becomes cost-effective when:
- Project duration > 3 months
- Multiple trades working simultaneously
- Heavy load requirements (>500 kg/sqm)
- Permanent or semi-permanent installations

### When Crawler Lift Wins

Crawler lifts are better when:
- Project duration < 3 months
- Large area coverage needed
- Floor protection required
- Time is critical
- Safety is priority
