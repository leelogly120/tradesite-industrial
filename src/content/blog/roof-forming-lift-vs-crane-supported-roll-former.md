Choose a dedicated roof-forming lift when repeated projects justify an owned, compatible line-and-lift package and each site can provide its working position. Compare a specialist crane-supported forming system when the roof product supplier offers that complete production method and the project can reserve the required crane throughout its occupation. A crane already on site is useful only if its actual duties, configuration and booking support that production assignment.

The purchase decision is who supplies the complete elevated production arrangement and which project resource it occupies. It is separate from hiring a crane to deliver coils or unload a machine. Keep those shorter material-handling duties outside the continuous production commitment unless the provider expressly includes them.

## Two engineered arrangements, not an interchangeable lifting accessory

A dedicated forming lift supports a compatible roof-panel former on its lifting assembly. Its selection connects the former, material supply, elevated outlet, receiving area and supported ground position. Truck and crawler versions add different vehicle or transport requirements to that package.

A crane-supported production system is a specialist arrangement developed around the identified former and the proposed crane-supported duty. Its provider must define the production configuration, supporting equipment, site interfaces and responsibilities. A transport lifting point, a crane's presence or a workshop photograph is insufficient grounds for converting an ordinary line into suspended production equipment.

BEMO's published site conditions provide a concrete example of this distinction: their roof-level production provisions call for the client to supply a crane for the duration of production, with the crane selection addressed by the crane specialist. This is evidence that crane occupation belongs in that supplier's production scope, rather than a universal method for other formers. ## The crane calendar can decide the shortlist

Start with the crane's committed assignments. Steel erection, mechanical equipment placement and material delivery are separate demands on the same resource. If the crane-supported forming proposal occupies it during the roofing production period, the contractor must reserve that time or price another crane arrangement.

Do not compare the dedicated lift's purchase price with only the crane's lifting time. Include mobilisation, production occupation, agreed interruptions, required attendance and release. Obtain those amounts and periods from actual providers. The lift alternative also needs its transport, assembly, support review, maintenance and production team priced over the same project scope.

If the specialist production supplier provides both the former and its operating team, that proposal purchases more than equipment access. Compare it with the owned package plus the people and production responsibilities that the buyer will supply. A machine-only total cannot answer a complete panel-production service quotation.

Use the worksheet to establish whether both routes can meet the same roof assignment before comparing expenditure.

## Ownership is strongest when the next roofs use the same capability

A dedicated package deserves a purchase enquiry when the contractor has repeated elevated-forming work, an established production team and practical arrangements for storage, maintenance and transfers. Record the known projects and identify any that need a different roof system or outlet arrangement. Ownership is justified by the work the package can actually address.

For one specialist roof product or an isolated project, obtain the production supplier's complete service proposal before purchasing an asset. The service route is attractive when it delivers the required product and the project can provide its crane and site obligations. If the crane occupation conflicts with essential construction work, compare another production arrangement rather than treating the existing crane as free capacity.

The workshop line in the photograph makes the retained manufacturing task visible. Whether supported by a lift or supplied within a specialist service, the actual former still needs the specified tooling, material and panel acceptance basis.

## Two illustrative purchasing mistakes

**Mistake: the crane is booked twice.** The roofing budget counts a site crane that the erection team needs during the same production period. Both packages appear complete until their calendars are combined. Reserve an actual production allocation and price the replacement crane duty before choosing the crane-supported offer.

**Mistake: transport lifting becomes a production proposal.** A buyer has a former with documented transport lifting provisions and assumes that a crane can hold it while panels are made. The missing item is an engineered production system, not another sling quotation. Obtain that complete specialist proposal or retain the dedicated lift route around a reviewed former.
