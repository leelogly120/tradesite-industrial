## The Stadium Ceiling Problem

A 60,000-seat stadium has roughly 25,000 square meters of ceiling surface. The acoustic panels, lighting grids, rigging points, HVAC systems, and fire suppression equipment all need periodic maintenance. Panels get damaged, lights burn out, rigging needs inspection, and HVAC filters need replacement.

Traditionally, this work means erecting scaffolding from the field level — a process that takes days per section, costs hundreds of thousands of dollars, and prevents the venue from hosting events during the work period.

For a stadium that hosts 200+ events per year, even a few days of lost revenue can cost more than the maintenance itself.

## Why Scaffolding Falls Short

Scaffolding for stadium ceiling work has three fundamental problems:

**Time.** Erecting scaffolding to 30m height across a 100m span takes 5–7 days per section. Working at height takes another 3–5 days. Dismantling takes another 2–3 days. For a full stadium ceiling, you're looking at 2–3 months of scaffolding work.

**Cost.** Scaffold rental, erection labor, and dismantling typically cost $15–25 per square meter of coverage. For 25,000 square meters, that's $375,000–$625,000 in scaffolding alone — before you count the labor doing the actual maintenance work.

**Venue disruption.** Scaffolding blocks seating sections, prevents field access, and often requires closing entire stands. A stadium that normally hosts 200 events per year might lose 30–50 events during a ceiling maintenance project.

## How Pick and Carry Changes the Math

A crawler under-ceiling platform like the ARC-F35 approaches the problem differently. Instead of building temporary access from the ground up, the machine drives onto the stadium floor, raises its 12m×8m platform to the working height, and travels along the ceiling line.

**Time.** A single ARC-F35 can cover the same ceiling area in one week that would take scaffolding a month. The 12m×8m platform means 6–10 workers can operate simultaneously, and the pick-and-carry capability means no stop-lower-reposition-raise cycle.

**Cost.** Equipment cost plus labor for a ARC-F-based maintenance project runs about $200K–$400K — significantly less than scaffolding when you factor in the eliminated erection and dismantling costs.

**Minimal disruption.** The machine works from the field level. Seating sections stay clear. The venue can host events in unaffected sections while ceiling work continues in others.

- Labor (20 workers, 90 days): $300,000
- Venue downtime: 45 days × $50,000/day revenue loss = $2,250,000
- **Total: ~$2,950,000**

**ARC-F35 approach (actual):**

- Venue downtime: 0 (work performed during non-event hours)

The ARC-F approach saved over $2.4 million — mostly from eliminated venue downtime. Even without counting lost revenue, the direct cost savings exceeded $330,000.

## Why Stadiums Are Adopting Pick and Carry

Three trends are driving stadium operators toward pick and carry platforms:

**Shorter maintenance windows.** Stadiums host more events than ever. The window for major maintenance work keeps shrinking. Pick and carry's speed advantage is increasingly valuable.

**Higher venue revenue.** A modern stadium generates $50,000–$200,000 per event day. Every day of downtime costs real money. Eliminating scaffolding downtime directly improves the bottom line.

**Safety liability.** Stadium owners face increasing liability for worker safety during maintenance projects. Remote-controlled platforms at ground level are inherently safer than workers on scaffolding at 30m.

## Making the Transition

Switching from scaffolding to pick and carry requires planning:

1. **Site survey:** Verify floor loading capacity for the machine's weight
2. **Access planning:** Confirm the machine can enter and maneuver within the stadium
3. **Power supply:** Plan electric power connections for indoor operation
4. **Training:** Ensure operators are trained on the ARC-F series
5. **Scheduling:** Plan work around the event calendar to minimize disruption

The investment in planning pays off in dramatically faster, cheaper, and safer ceiling maintenance.
