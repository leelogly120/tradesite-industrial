# Industrial Park Construction: 6 Buildings in 6 Months

> "Managing 6 simultaneous roof installations seemed impossible until we found the right equipment." — Liu Jian, Project Director, Henan Industrial Development Zone

## Project Overview

### Client Background

Henan Industrial Development Zone is developing a new industrial park in Xinxiang, consisting of 6 factory buildings ranging from 5,000 to 15,000 square meters each.

### The Challenge

The developer needed to complete all 6 buildings within 6 months to meet tenant move-in deadlines. Traditional methods would have required multiple cranes and extended timelines.

**Key Issues:**
- 6 buildings with different heights and roof designs
- Total roofing area: 55,000 sqm
- Tight 6-month deadline
- Limited budget for equipment and labor

### Previous Solutions Attempted

The developer initially planned to use crane-assisted installation:
- Estimated requirement: 3 mobile cranes
- Estimated timeline: 9-12 months
- High equipment rental costs
- Complex logistics coordination

## The ARCLIFT Solution

### Product Selection

The [ARC-C32 Crawler Roll Forming Lift](/products/arc-c32-crawler-roll-forming-lift/) was selected for its:

| Specification | Value |
|---------------|-------|
| Model | ARC-C32 |
| Working Height | 32m |
| Platform Size | 6m × 2m |
| Roll Forming Speed | 15m/min |
| Ground Pressure | 1.0 MPa |

**Why ARC-C32:**
- Tallest building: 30m (32m provided safety margin)
- Single unit could serve all 6 buildings
- Integrated roll forming eliminated multiple material staging areas
- Self-propelled movement between buildings

### Implementation

**Phase 1: Pilot Building (Months 1-2)**

- Building 1 (15,000 sqm): Completed in 5 weeks
- Operator training: 2 days
- Production rate stabilized at 700 sqm/day
- Lessons learned documented

**Phase 2: Parallel Execution (Months 3-5)**

- Buildings 2-5 executed in sequence
- Average completion: 4 weeks per building
- Crew proficiency improved steadily
- Production rate: 750-850 sqm/day

**Phase 3: Final Building (Month 6)**

- Building 6 completed in 3.5 weeks
- Final production rate: 900 sqm/day
- Project completed on schedule

### Training & Support

- 2-day initial training
- Weekly technical consultations
- Monthly maintenance visits
- Dedicated support hotline

## Results

### Quantitative Results

| Metric | Crane Method (Projected) | ARC-C32 (Actual) | Improvement |
|--------|--------------------------|------------------|-------------| 
| Total Duration | 12 months | 6 months | -50% |
| Equipment Cost | 3 cranes | 1 ARC-C32 | -60% |
| Crew Size | 12 workers | 3 workers | -75% |
| Daily Output | 400 sqm | 800 sqm | +100% |

### Qualitative Benefits

- **Simplified Logistics**: Single equipment, single crew
- **Consistent Quality**: Same equipment, same operators throughout
- **Knowledge Transfer**: Skills developed on Building 1 applied to all others
- **Client Satisfaction**: All tenants moved in on schedule

## Lessons Learned

1. **Single equipment strategy works**: Using one unit across all buildings simplified management and ensured consistency.

2. **Pilot building approach**: The first building served as a training ground, with efficiency improving on subsequent buildings.

3. **Long-term investment**: The developer has since purchased the unit for future projects.
