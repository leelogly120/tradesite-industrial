# Logistics Center Roof: 12,000 sqm in 3.5 Weeks

> "We've built 20+ logistics centers. This was the fastest roof installation we've ever achieved." — Wang Chen, VP Operations, Guangzhou Distribution Center

## Project Overview

### Client Background

Guangzhou Distribution Center operates a major e-commerce fulfillment network in southern China. They were constructing a new 35,000 sqm regional distribution center.

### The Challenge

The project had an aggressive 5-week timeline for the roofing phase due to upcoming peak season. The previous contractor had been dismissed, leaving the project behind schedule.

**Key Issues:**
- 5-week deadline with zero flexibility
- Previous contractor produced only 300 sqm/day
- Site had limited access due to adjacent operational facilities
- Ground conditions varied across the site

### Previous Solutions Attempted

The original contractor used a combination of mobile cranes and manual placement:
- Average output: 300 sqm/day
- Required 5 workers per shift
- Frequent repositioning delays
- Weather sensitivity

## The ARCLIFT Solution

### Product Selection

The [ARC-C25 Crawler Roll Forming Lift](/products/arc-c25-crawler-roll-forming-lift/) was selected for:

| Specification | Value |
|---------------|-------|
| Model | ARC-C25 |
| Working Height | 25m |
| Platform Size | 6m × 2m |
| Roll Forming Speed | 15m/min |
| Gradeability | 15° |

**Why ARC-C25:**
- Maximum roof height: 20m (25m provided margin)
- Self-propelled crawler handled variable terrain
- Integrated roll forming for continuous production
- Compact footprint for limited access areas

### Implementation

**Week 1: Mobilization and Training**

- Day 1: Equipment delivery and assembly (5 hours)
- Day 2: Operator training and calibration
- Day 3-5: Production ramp-up: 400 → 600 → 750 sqm/day

**Weeks 2-3: Peak Production**

The crew achieved consistent 800-900 sqm/day:
- Continuous roll forming and placement
- Single operator controlled both functions
- Crawler tracks enabled smooth repositioning

**Week 4: Completion**

Final 2,000 sqm completed by Day 24. Project finished 11 days ahead of deadline.

### Training & Support

- 1.5-day intensive training
- On-site technical support for first week
- Daily quality verification
- 24/7 emergency support

## Results

### Quantitative Results

| Metric | Previous Contractor | ARC-C25 | Improvement |
|--------|--------------------|---------|-------------| 
| Daily Output | 300 sqm | 850 sqm | +183% |
| Total Duration | 40 days (projected) | 24 days | -40% |
| Crew Size | 5 workers | 2 workers | -60% |
| Rework Rate | 8% | 0.5% | -94% |

### Qualitative Benefits

- **Record Speed**: Fastest roof installation in company history
- **Quality Improvement**: Minimal rework due to roll forming precision
- **Labor Savings**: 60% reduction in crew size
- **Client Confidence**: Company has since ordered 3 additional units

## Lessons Learned

1. **Speed comes from integration**: Combining roll forming and placement in one unit eliminated the biggest bottleneck.

2. **Training pays dividends**: The 1.5-day training investment produced a highly efficient 2-person crew.

3. **Terrain versatility**: The crawler tracks handled the site's variable ground conditions without modification.
