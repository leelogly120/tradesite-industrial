# Power Line Maintenance: 500km of Transmission Lines

> "The truck-mounted lift gave us safe, efficient access to transmission towers across the province." — Chief Engineer Liu, Henan Electric Power Company

## Project Overview

### Client Background

Henan Electric Power Company maintains 500km of high-voltage transmission lines across the province. Annual maintenance includes tower inspections, insulator replacement, and conductor repairs.

### The Challenge

Transmission line maintenance requires access to tower tops (15-30m) in remote locations with limited road access.

**Key Issues:**
- 500km of transmission lines
- Remote tower locations
- Limited road access
- Safety critical work at height

### Previous Solutions Attempted

The company used telescopic boom trucks:
- Limited reach for tall towers
- Slow repositioning between towers
- High fuel consumption
- Limited platform capacity

## The ARCLIFT Solution

### Product Selection

The [ARC-T18 Truck-Mounted Roll Forming Lift](/products/arc-t18-truck-mounted-roll-forming-lift/) was selected for:

| Specification | Value |
|---------------|-------|
| Model | ARC-T18 |
| Working Height | 18m |
| Platform Size | 5m × 2m |
| Off-Road Capability | Yes |
| Platform Capacity | 300 kg |

**Why ARC-T18:**
- Sufficient height for most towers
- Off-road capability for remote locations
- Quick setup for multiple towers
- Platform capacity for tools and materials

### Implementation

**Phase 1: Pilot Section (Month 1)**

- 50km test section
- 25 towers inspected and maintained
- Crew trained on transmission protocols
- Time per tower: 45 minutes

**Phase 2: Full Deployment (Months 2-5)**

- 100km per month average
- 50+ towers per month
- Night work for populated areas
- Day work for rural sections

**Phase 3: Completion (Month 6)**

- Final 50km completed
- Quality inspection
- Documentation

### Training & Support

- Electrical safety training
- Fall protection certification
- Emergency response procedures
- Equipment maintenance training

## Results

### Quantitative Results

| Metric | Telescopic Boom | ARC-T18 | Improvement |
|--------|-----------------|---------|-------------| 
| Time per Tower | 1 hour | 45 minutes | -25% |
| Towers per Day | 8 | 12 | +50% |
| Total Duration | 9 months | 6 months | -33% |
| Fuel Consumption | High | Moderate | -35% |

### Qualitative Benefits

- **Safety**: Zero incidents during the project
- **Accessibility**: Off-road capability reached remote towers
- **Efficiency**: Quick setup maximized productive time
- **Documentation**: Complete maintenance records

## Lessons Learned

1. **Off-road capability essential**: Remote tower locations require rugged equipment.

2. **Quick setup key**: Moving between towers in minutes maximizes productivity.

3. **Platform capacity matters**: Carrying tools and materials saves trips to ground.
