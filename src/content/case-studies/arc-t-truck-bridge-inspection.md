# Bridge Inspection: 50 Bridges in 3 Months

> "The compact truck-mounted lift could access areas that larger equipment couldn't reach." — Engineer Zhang, Shandong Transportation Department

## Project Overview

### Client Background

The Shandong Transportation Department was conducting routine inspections of 50 bridges across the province, ranging from small overpasses to major river crossings.

### The Challenge

Bridge inspections require access to undersides, piers, and structural elements that are difficult to reach with standard equipment.

**Key Issues:**
- 50 bridges of varying sizes
- Access to undersides and piers
- Limited traffic disruption
- Comprehensive documentation required

### Previous Solutions Attempted

The department previously used under-bridge inspection vehicles:
- Very expensive equipment
- Limited availability
- Slow deployment
- High traffic disruption

## The ARCLIFT Solution

### Product Selection

The [ARC-T12 Truck-Mounted Roll Forming Lift](/products/arc-t12-truck-mounted-roll-forming-lift/) was selected for:

| Specification | Value |
|---------------|-------|
| Model | ARC-T12 |
| Working Height | 12m |
| Platform Size | 4m × 2m |
| Setup Time | 10 minutes |
| Compact Footprint | Yes |

**Why ARC-T12:**
- Compact size for bridge access
- Quick setup for multiple inspections
- Highway-legal transport
- Sufficient height for most bridges

### Implementation

**Phase 1: Small Bridges (Month 1)**

- 25 small overpasses inspected
- Average time per bridge: 2 hours
- 2 inspections per day

**Phase 2: Medium Bridges (Month 2)**

- 15 medium bridges inspected
- Average time per bridge: 3 hours
- 1-2 inspections per day

**Phase 3: Large Bridges (Month 3)**

- 10 large river crossings inspected
- Average time per bridge: 4 hours
- 1 inspection per day

### Training & Support

- Inspection protocol training
- Documentation procedures
- Safety certification
- Equipment maintenance

## Results

### Quantitative Results

| Metric | Under-Bridge Vehicle | ARC-T12 | Improvement |
|--------|---------------------|---------|-------------| 
| Equipment Cost | ¥500,000 | ¥150,000 | -70% |
| Time per Bridge | 4 hours | 3 hours | -25% |
| Total Duration | 5 months | 3 months | -40% |
| Traffic Disruption | High | Low | -75% |

### Qualitative Benefits

- **Accessibility**: Compact size reached difficult areas
- **Efficiency**: Quick setup for multiple inspections
- **Documentation**: Photos and videos captured easily
- **Cost Savings**: 70% less equipment cost

## Lessons Learned

1. **Compact equipment wins**: Bridge inspections need mobility, not just height.

2. **Quick setup essential**: Moving between bridges in minutes maximizes productivity.

3. **Documentation capability**: Platform stability enables accurate photography.
