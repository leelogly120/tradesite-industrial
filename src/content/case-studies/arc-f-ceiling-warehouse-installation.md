# Warehouse Ceiling Installation: 30,000 sqm in 3 Weeks

> "The pick & carry feature allowed us to work continuously without stopping for repositioning." — Warehouse Manager Zhang, JD Logistics

## Project Overview

### Client Background

JD Logistics operates one of China's largest e-commerce fulfillment networks. A new 80,000 sqm distribution center required complete ceiling installation.

### The Challenge

The 30,000 sqm ceiling area required installation of lighting, sprinklers, and HVAC systems while the warehouse was partially operational.

**Key Issues:**
- 30,000 sqm ceiling area
- Partial warehouse operations ongoing
- Tight 3-week deadline
- Floor protection required (epoxy coating)

### Previous Solutions Attempted

Previous projects used scissor lifts:
- Limited reach for high ceilings
- Frequent repositioning needed
- Slow progress (300 sqm/day)
- Epoxy floor damage risk

## The ARCLIFT Solution

### Product Selection

The [ARC-F20 Crawler Ceiling Platform](/products/arc-f20-crawler-ceiling-platform/) was selected for:

| Specification | Value |
|---------------|-------|
| Model | ARC-F20 |
| Working Height | 20m |
| Platform Size | 12m × 3m |
| Ground Pressure | 0.5 MPa |
| Pick & Carry | Yes |

**Why ARC-F20:**
- 20m height for warehouse ceiling (18m peak)
- Pick & carry for continuous work
- Low ground pressure for epoxy floors
- Compact size for operational areas

### Implementation

**Week 1: Phase 1 Section (10,000 sqm)**

- Pick & carry covered large areas
- Continuous work without lowering
- 1,000 sqm/day average

**Week 2: Phase 2 Section (10,000 sqm)**

- Crew proficiency improved
- 1,200 sqm/day average
- Minimal disruption to operations

**Week 3: Final Section (10,000 sqm)**

- Peak efficiency achieved
- 1,500 sqm/day average
- Project completed 2 days ahead of schedule

### Training & Support

- 1-day operator training
- Floor protection protocols
- Operational coordination
- 24/7 technical support

## Results

### Quantitative Results

| Metric | Scissor Lift | ARC-F20 | Improvement |
|--------|--------------|---------|-------------| 
| Daily Coverage | 300 sqm | 1,200 sqm | +300% |
| Total Duration | 10 weeks | 3 weeks | -70% |
| Crew Size | 6 workers | 2 workers | -67% |
| Floor Damage | 8 incidents | 0 | -100% |

### Qualitative Benefits

- **Operational Continuity**: Warehouse remained partially operational
- **Floor Protection**: Epoxy coating undamaged
- **Speed Record**: Fastest ceiling installation in company history
- **Cost Savings**: 60% reduction in labor costs

## Lessons Learned

1. **Pick & carry eliminates downtime**: Continuous work without repositioning saved weeks.

2. **Low ground pressure critical**: Epoxy floors required careful equipment selection.

3. **Compact size wins**: Smaller footprint navigated operational areas easily.
