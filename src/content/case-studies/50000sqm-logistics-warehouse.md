## Project Overview

A 50,000 sqm logistics warehouse required full ceiling panel installation at 18m height across 12 bays. The original plan called for scaffolding — estimated at 90 days and $600K in labor and materials.

The contractor deployed two ARC-F25 platforms instead, with pick & carry allowing continuous panel installation across each 60m×30m bay.

## The Challenge

- 50,000 sqm ceiling area across 12 bays
- 18m working height throughout
- Tight delivery schedule — warehouse needed to be operational within 3 months
- Limited floor space for scaffolding staging
- Pre-engineered steel structure with exposed purlins

## The Solution

Two ARC-F25 platforms worked in parallel across adjacent bays. The pick & carry capability meant each machine covered a full 60m×30m bay without stopping — the platform traveled along the ceiling line while workers installed panels continuously.

Diesel power handled the rough concrete floor of the partially completed building. The 12m×3m platform fit perfectly between the steel column grid, and the 500kg load capacity carried stacks of 4 ceiling panels per trip.

## Key Results

The project was completed in 60 days — a full month ahead of the scaffolding estimate. Total cost savings exceeded $200,000 when scaffolding rental, labor, and teardown were eliminated.

> "We calculated that scaffolding would cost us $600K and take 90 days. The ARC-F25 did the same job in 60 days for about $400K all-in. That's a 33% cost reduction and a month saved."
> — Project Director, logistics facility developer
