# Agricultural Greenhouse: 20,000 sqm Structure Installation

> "We needed to complete the structure before the growing season. The ARC-C17 got us there with time to spare." — Chen Li, Farm Manager, Modern Agricultural Development Co.

## Project Overview

### Client Background

Modern Agricultural Development Co. operates large-scale greenhouse farms across eastern China. They were expanding with a new 20,000 sqm high-tech greenhouse complex.

### The Challenge

The greenhouse required installation of steel arch structures and roof panels. The project had to complete before the spring growing season, leaving only 4 weeks.

**Key Issues:**
- 4-week deadline before growing season
- Soft soil conditions from recent rain
- Tall arch structures (15m peak height)
- Limited access roads

### Previous Solutions Attempted

The farm had previously used manual installation methods:
- Workers on scaffolding installing panels one by one
- Average: 100 sqm/day
- Weather delays significant
- Labor-intensive and slow

## The ARCLIFT Solution

### Product Selection

The [ARC-C17 Crawler Roll Forming Lift](/products/arc-c17-crawler-roll-forming-lift/) was selected for:

| Specification | Value |
|---------------|-------|
| Model | ARC-C17 |
| Working Height | 17m |
| Platform Size | 4m × 2m |
| Ground Pressure | 0.6 MPa |
| Roll Forming | Integrated |

**Why ARC-C17:**
- 15m arch height (17m provided margin)
- Low ground pressure for soft soil
- Compact size for narrow access roads
- Integrated roll forming for arch panels

### Implementation

**Week 1: Setup and Calibration**

- Equipment delivered on flatbed truck
- Assembly completed in 3 hours
- Roll forming calibrated for arch profiles
- Operator training: 1 day

**Weeks 2-3: Production**

The crew achieved 500-600 sqm/day:
- Roll forming created arch panels on-site
- Continuous placement without lowering platform
- Crawler tracks handled soft soil conditions

**Week 4: Completion**

Final sections completed with 2 days to spare. Growing season started on schedule.

### Training & Support

- 1-day operator training
- Arch profile calibration assistance
- Daily technical support calls
- Emergency parts delivery

## Results

### Quantitative Results

| Metric | Manual Method | ARC-C17 | Improvement |
|--------|---------------|---------|-------------| 
| Daily Output | 100 sqm | 550 sqm | +450% |
| Total Duration | 20 weeks | 4 weeks | -80% |
| Crew Size | 8 workers | 2 workers | -75% |
| Weather Delays | Frequent | Minimal | -90% |

### Qualitative Benefits

- **Seasonal Timing**: Project completed before growing season
- **Soil Protection**: Low ground pressure preserved field conditions
- **Quality Consistency**: Roll forming ensured uniform panels
- **Labor Efficiency**: Small crew handled entire operation

## Lessons Learned

1. **Agricultural applications work**: The crawler lift performed excellently in farm conditions.

2. **Ground pressure matters**: The 0.6 MPa ground pressure was critical for soft soil.

3. **Compact equipment wins**: The smaller footprint navigated narrow farm roads easily.
