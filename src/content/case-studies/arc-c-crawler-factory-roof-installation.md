# Factory Roof Installation: 8,000 sqm Completed in 3 Weeks

> "We were behind schedule on our new factory building. The ARC-C25 got us back on track and finished ahead of deadline." — Zhang Wei, Project Manager, Zhengzhou Heavy Industries

## Project Overview

### Client Background

Zhengzhou Heavy Industries is a leading manufacturer of construction machinery in central China. They were expanding their production facilities with a new 12,000 square meter factory building.

### The Challenge

The project was already 3 weeks behind schedule when the roofing phase began. Traditional methods—crane-assisted panel placement—were too slow for the 8,000 square meter roof area.

**Key Issues:**
- Previous contractor's methods were producing only 200 sqm per day
- Weather delays had already consumed the buffer time
- The client faced penalty clauses for late delivery
- Ground conditions were uneven with loose gravel

### Previous Solutions Attempted

The contractor initially tried using a mobile crane to lift pre-formed panels. This required:
- 2 workers on the roof guiding placement
- 1 crane operator
- 1 ground crew member
- Average setup time: 15 minutes per panel
- Daily output: 15-20 panels (200 sqm)

## The ARCLIFT Solution

### Product Selection

The project manager selected the [ARC-C25 Crawler Roll Forming Lift](/products/arc-c25-crawler-roll-forming-lift/) based on:

| Specification | Value |
|---------------|-------|
| Model | ARC-C25 |
| Working Height | 25m |
| Platform Size | 6m × 2m |
| Power System | Diesel-Electric Dual |
| Ground Pressure | 0.8 MPa |

**Why ARC-C25:**
- Factory peak height: 22m (25m provided safety margin)
- Self-propelled crawler tracks handled the gravel surface
- Integrated roll forming eliminated pre-formed panel transport
- Diesel power for continuous outdoor operation

### Implementation

**Week 1: Setup and Initial Production**

Day 1: Equipment delivered and assembled in 4 hours. Operator training completed by end of day.

Days 2-7: Production ramped from 400 sqm/day to 600 sqm/day as the crew gained proficiency.

**Week 2: Peak Production**

The team achieved consistent 700-800 sqm/day output. Key advantages:
- Roll forming on-site eliminated transportation damage
- Crawler tracks allowed continuous movement along the roofline
- Platform capacity (500kg) supported workers, tools, and materials

**Week 3: Completion and Punch List**

Final 1,500 sqm completed by Day 18. Remaining time used for quality inspection and punch list items.

### Training & Support

ARCLIFT provided:
- 2-day operator training on-site
- Daily technical support calls
- Replacement parts delivered within 24 hours

## Results

### Quantitative Results

| Metric | Before (Crane) | After (ARC-C25) | Improvement |
|--------|-----------------|-----------------|-------------|
| Daily Output | 200 sqm | 650 sqm | +225% |
| Crew Size | 4 workers | 2 workers | -50% |
| Setup Time | 15 min/panel | Continuous | N/A |
| Total Duration | 40 days (projected) | 18 days | -55% |

### Qualitative Benefits

- **Safety**: No crane-related incidents during the project
- **Quality**: On-site roll forming reduced panel damage by 90%
- **Flexibility**: Easy repositioning around obstacles
- **Client Satisfaction**: Project completed 2 weeks ahead of revised schedule

## Lessons Learned

1. **Early equipment selection saves time**: If the ARC-C25 had been specified from the start, the project would have been on schedule from Day 1.

2. **Operator proficiency matters**: The 2-day training investment paid dividends in productivity and safety.

3. **Ground conditions matter**: The crawler tracks performed well on gravel, but a site survey would have confirmed this earlier.
