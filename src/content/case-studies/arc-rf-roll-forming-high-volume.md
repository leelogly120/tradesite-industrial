# High Volume Production: 50,000 sqm per Month

> "The ARC-RF8 doubled our production capacity while reducing waste by 60%." — Production Director Li, Henan Zhongyuan Steel

## Project Overview

### Client Background

Henan Zhongyuan Steel is a major steel building materials manufacturer, requiring increased production capacity to meet growing demand.

### The Challenge

The company needed to double production capacity from 25,000 sqm to 50,000 sqm per month while maintaining quality and reducing waste.

**Key Issues:**
- Current capacity: 25,000 sqm/month
- Target capacity: 50,000 sqm/month
- Quality requirements: 98%+ pass rate
- Waste reduction target: 50%

### Previous Solutions Attempted

Previous production methods:
- Multiple roll forming lines
- High maintenance costs
- Frequent quality issues
- Excessive material waste

## The ARCLIFT Solution

### Product Selection

The [ARC-RF8 Roll Forming Machine](/products/arc-rf8-roll-forming-machine/) was selected for:

| Specification | Value |
|---------------|-------|
| Model | ARC-RF8 |
| Production Speed | 15m/min |
| Operating Hours | 20/day |
| Quality Rate | 99.5% |
| Waste Rate | <2% |

**Why ARC-RF8:**
- High production speed
- Continuous operation capability
- Consistent quality output
- Low waste rate

### Implementation

**Month 1: Installation and Calibration**

- Equipment installed and calibrated
- Operator training completed
- Production optimization
- Quality verification

**Month 2: Production Ramp-Up**

- Production increased from 25,000 to 40,000 sqm/month
- Quality maintained at 99%+
- Waste reduced to 3%

**Month 3+: Full Production**

- Production achieved 50,000 sqm/month
- Quality maintained at 99.5%
- Waste reduced to 2%

### Training & Support

- Intensive operator training
- Production optimization
- Quality control procedures
- Ongoing technical support

## Results

### Quantitative Results

| Metric | Before | After | Improvement |
|--------|--------|-------|-------------| 
| Monthly Production | 25,000 sqm | 50,000 sqm | +100% |
| Quality Rate | 96% | 99.5% | +4% |
| Waste Rate | 5% | 2% | -60% |
| Maintenance Cost | High | Low | -50% |

### Qualitative Benefits

- **Production Doubled**: Capacity doubled to meet demand
- **Quality Improved**: Higher pass rate, fewer returns
- **Cost Reduced**: Lower waste and maintenance costs
- **Competitive Advantage**: Faster delivery to customers

## Lessons Learned

1. **Speed matters**: 15m/min production speed enabled high volume.

2. **Continuous operation**: 20-hour daily operation maximized output.

3. **Quality consistency**: 99.5% quality rate reduced rework and returns.
