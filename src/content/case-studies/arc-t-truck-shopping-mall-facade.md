# Shopping Mall Facade: Complete Renovation in 6 Weeks

> "The truck-mounted lift was perfect—drive up, extend, work. No disruption to our customers." — Zhao Min, Mall Director, Zhengzhou Central Plaza

## Project Overview

### Client Background

Zhengzhou Central Plaza is a major shopping mall in central Zhengzhou. The 15-year-old facade needed complete replacement to modernize the building's appearance.

### The Challenge

The mall remained open during renovation. Work had to be performed without disrupting customer access or retail operations.

**Key Issues:**
- 3,500 sqm facade area
- Mall remained open during work
- Customer access maintained at all times
- Night work restrictions due to noise

### Previous Solutions Attempted

The mall previously used scaffolding for facade work:
- 2 weeks to erect scaffolding
- Visible construction affected customer traffic
- Required partial store closures
- Night work generated noise complaints

## The ARCLIFT Solution

### Product Selection

The [ARC-T25 Truck-Mounted Roll Forming Lift](/products/arc-t25-truck-mounted-roll-forming-lift/) was selected for:

| Specification | Value |
|---------------|-------|
| Model | ARC-T25 |
| Working Height | 25m |
| Platform Size | 6m × 2m |
| Setup Time | 10 minutes |
| Electric Mode | Available |

**Why ARC-T25:**
- Truck-mounted: Drive directly to work position
- No crane or transport required
- Electric mode for quiet operation during business hours
- Quick repositioning along the facade

### Implementation

**Week 1: South Facade (800 sqm)**

- Day 1: Equipment positioned, operator trained
- Days 2-7: Work performed during off-hours (10 PM - 6 AM)
- Zero customer disruption

**Weeks 2-3: East and West Facades (1,400 sqm)**

- Daytime work on upper sections (above customer areas)
- Night work on ground-level sections
- Continuous progress along the facade

**Weeks 4-5: North Facade (800 sqm)**

- Most visible facade—careful execution
- Daytime work with safety barriers
- Customer flow maintained

**Week 6: Completion and Cleanup**

- Final sections completed
- All equipment removed
- Facade cleaning and inspection

### Training & Support

- 1-day operator training
- Electric mode training for night work
- Daily safety briefings
- Emergency support hotline

## Results

### Quantitative Results

| Metric | Scaffolding Method | ARC-T25 | Improvement |
|--------|--------------------|---------|-------------| 
| Setup Time | 2 weeks | 10 minutes | -99% |
| Total Duration | 12 weeks | 6 weeks | -50% |
| Business Disruption | 30% sales drop | 0% | -100% |
| Noise Complaints | 15 | 0 | -100% |

### Qualitative Benefits

- **Zero Business Disruption**: Mall remained fully operational
- **No Noise Complaints**: Electric mode during business hours
- **Professional Appearance**: Clean, modern equipment
- **Customer Positive Feedback**: Customers noticed the improvement

## Lessons Learned

1. **Truck-mounted wins for commercial**: The ability to drive to position eliminated all setup disruption.

2. **Electric mode essential**: Night work with zero noise was critical for customer satisfaction.

3. **Quick repositioning**: Moving along the facade in minutes, not hours.
