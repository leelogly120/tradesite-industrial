# Factory Ceiling: 15,000 sqm Installation in 2 Weeks

> "The pick & carry feature allowed us to cover the entire factory floor in record time." — Factory Manager Liu, BYD Manufacturing

## Project Overview

### Client Background

BYD Manufacturing is one of China's largest electric vehicle manufacturers, requiring rapid facility expansion to meet production demands.

### The Challenge

A new 25,000 sqm production facility required complete ceiling installation of lighting, ventilation, and fire suppression systems within 2 weeks.

**Key Issues:**
- 15,000 sqm ceiling area
- 2-week deadline for production start
- Complex ceiling systems (lighting, ventilation, fire)
- Floor protection required (polished concrete)

### Previous Solutions Attempted

Previous projects used scaffolding:
- 1 week setup time
- Limited coverage per section
- Slow progress (200 sqm/day)
- Production delay risk

## The ARCLIFT Solution

### Product Selection

The [ARC-F25 Crawler Ceiling Platform](/products/arc-f25-crawler-ceiling-platform/) was selected for:

| Specification | Value |
|---------------|-------|
| Model | ARC-F25 |
| Working Height | 25m |
| Platform Size | 12m × 4m |
| Ground Pressure | 0.6 MPa |
| Pick & Carry | Yes |

**Why ARC-F25:**
- 25m height for factory ceiling (20m peak)
- Pick & carry for continuous work
- Low ground pressure for polished concrete
- Large platform for multiple systems

### Implementation

**Week 1: Production Area (10,000 sqm)**

- Pick & carry covered large areas
- Multiple systems installed simultaneously
- 1,500 sqm/day average

**Week 2: Support Areas (5,000 sqm)**

- Peak efficiency achieved
- Quality inspection throughout
- Project completed 2 days ahead of schedule

### Training & Support

- Factory safety protocols
- Production coordination
- Floor protection training
- 24/7 technical support

## Results

### Quantitative Results

| Metric | Scaffolding | ARC-F25 | Improvement |
|--------|-------------|---------|-------------| 
| Setup Time | 1 week | 30 minutes | -99% |
| Daily Coverage | 200 sqm | 1,500 sqm | +650% |
| Total Duration | 8 weeks | 2 weeks | -75% |
| Production Delay | 2 weeks | 0 | -100% |

### Qualitative Benefits

- **Production On Time**: Factory started production as scheduled
- **Floor Protection**: Polished concrete undamaged
- **Quality Improvement**: Precise positioning for systems
- **Cost Savings**: 70% reduction in installation costs

## Lessons Learned

1. **Pick & carry eliminates setup**: No scaffolding meant immediate start.

2. **Large platform essential**: 12m × 4m platform allowed multiple work zones.

3. **Low ground pressure critical**: Polished concrete required careful equipment selection.
