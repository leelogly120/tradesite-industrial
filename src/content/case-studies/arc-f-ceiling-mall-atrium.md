# Shopping Mall Atrium: Lighting Upgrade in 2 Weeks

> "The electric mode meant we could work during business hours without disturbing customers." — Mall Director Wang, Hangzhou MixC Mall

## Project Overview

### Client Background

Hangzhou MixC Mall is a premium shopping destination requiring regular upgrades to maintain its luxury brand image.

### The Challenge

The 8,000 sqm atrium required complete lighting upgrade from traditional fixtures to energy-efficient LED systems while maintaining mall operations.

**Key Issues:**
- 8,000 sqm atrium ceiling
- Mall remained open during work
- Customer experience paramount
- Aesthetic sensitivity high

### Previous Solutions Attempted

Previous lighting work used boom lifts:
- Limited reach for high atrium
- Customer disruption from noise
- Slow progress (150 sqm/day)
- Safety concerns in public areas

## The ARCLIFT Solution

### Product Selection

The [ARC-F20 Crawler Ceiling Platform](/products/arc-f20-crawler-ceiling-platform/) was selected for:

| Specification | Value |
|---------------|-------|
| Model | ARC-F20 |
| Working Height | 20m |
| Platform Size | 12m × 3m |
| Ground Pressure | 0.5 MPa |
| Electric Mode | Yes |

**Why ARC-F20:**
- 20m height for atrium ceiling (16m peak)
- Electric mode for quiet operation
- Low ground pressure for marble floors
- Professional appearance for luxury environment

### Implementation

**Week 1: Main Atrium (5,000 sqm)**

- Electric mode during business hours
- Quiet operation throughout
- 700 sqm/day average

**Week 2: Secondary Atriums (3,000 sqm)**

- Consistent quality throughout
- Customer positive feedback
- Project completed on schedule

### Training & Support

- Mall safety protocols
- Customer interaction training
- Electric mode optimization
- Emergency response plan

## Results

### Quantitative Results

| Metric | Boom Lift | ARC-F20 | Improvement |
|--------|-----------|---------|-------------| 
| Daily Coverage | 150 sqm | 600 sqm | +300% |
| Total Duration | 6 weeks | 2 weeks | -67% |
| Customer Complaints | 25 | 2 | -92% |
| Noise Level | 75 dB | 58 dB | -23% |

### Qualitative Benefits

- **Zero Business Disruption**: Mall remained fully operational
- **Customer Satisfaction**: Minimal disturbance reported
- **Quality Improvement**: Precise positioning for fixtures
- **Energy Savings**: New LED system reduced consumption 40%

## Lessons Learned

1. **Electric mode essential**: Quiet operation in occupied spaces.

2. **Floor protection matters**: Low ground pressure for marble floors.

3. **Professional appearance**: Clean equipment for luxury environments.
