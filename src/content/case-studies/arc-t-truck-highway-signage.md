# Highway Signage Installation: 200km in 8 Weeks

> "The truck-mounted lift was perfect for highway work—drive to position, extend, install, move on." — Director Wang, Henan Highway Authority

## Project Overview

### Client Background

The Henan Highway Authority was upgrading signage along a 200km stretch of national highway, requiring installation of 500+ new signs.

### The Challenge

Highway work presents unique challenges: traffic management, limited work windows, and the need for rapid deployment and repositioning.

**Key Issues:**
- 500+ signs along 200km
- Limited work windows (night work required)
- Traffic management complexity
- Equipment must be mobile between sites

### Previous Solutions Attempted

The authority previously used bucket trucks:
- Limited reach for overhead signs
- Required separate transport for materials
- Slow repositioning between signs
- High fuel consumption

## The ARCLIFT Solution

### Product Selection

The [ARC-T25 Truck-Mounted Roll Forming Lift](/products/arc-t25-truck-mounted-roll-forming-lift/) was selected for:

| Specification | Value |
|---------------|-------|
| Model | ARC-T25 |
| Working Height | 25m |
| Platform Size | 6m × 2m |
| Highway Legal | Yes |
| Roll Forming | Integrated |

**Why ARC-T25:**
- Highway-legal truck mounting
- 25m height for overhead signs
- Integrated roll forming for sign panels
- Quick setup and repositioning

### Implementation

**Phase 1: Pilot Section (Weeks 1-2)**

- 20km test section
- 50 signs installed
- Crew trained on highway protocols
- Time per sign: 15 minutes

**Phase 2: Full Deployment (Weeks 3-7)**

- 40km per week average
- 100+ signs per week
- Night work for major intersections
- Day work for rural sections

**Phase 3: Completion (Week 8)**

- Final 20km completed
- Quality inspection
- Documentation

### Training & Support

- Highway safety training
- Night work protocols
- Emergency response procedures
- Equipment maintenance training

## Results

### Quantitative Results

| Metric | Bucket Truck | ARC-T25 | Improvement |
|--------|--------------|---------|-------------| 
| Time per Sign | 30 minutes | 15 minutes | -50% |
| Signs per Day | 20 | 40 | +100% |
| Total Duration | 16 weeks | 8 weeks | -50% |
| Fuel Consumption | High | Moderate | -30% |

### Qualitative Benefits

- **Traffic Safety**: Quick setup minimized traffic disruption
- **Night Work**: Electric mode for urban areas
- **Quality**: Roll forming ensured consistent sign panels
- **Documentation**: Complete project records

## Lessons Learned

1. **Highway-legal mounting essential**: No special permits required for transport.

2. **Quick repositioning key**: Moving between signs in minutes, not hours.

3. **Night work capability**: Electric mode critical for urban highway sections.
