# National Stadium Ceiling Renovation: 25,000 sqm in 5 Weeks

> "The pick & carry capability was revolutionary. We covered the entire stadium ceiling in record time." — Director Chen, National Stadium Authority

## Project Overview

### Client Background

The National Stadium Authority manages one of China's premier sports venues, hosting major international events and requiring regular maintenance to meet world-class standards.

### The Challenge

The 25,000 sqm ceiling required complete renovation of acoustic panels, lighting systems, and HVAC ducts. The work had to be completed between major events with a 6-week window.

**Key Issues:**
- 25,000 sqm ceiling area
- 6-week deadline between events
- Multiple ceiling systems (acoustic, lighting, HVAC)
- Floor protection critical (synthetic turf)

### Previous Solutions Attempted

Previous renovations used scaffolding:
- 3 weeks to erect scaffolding
- Limited coverage per section
- High risk of floor damage
- Event schedule delays

## The ARCLIFT Solution

### Product Selection

The [ARC-F31 Crawler Ceiling Platform](/products/arc-f31-crawler-ceiling-platform/) was selected for:

| Specification | Value |
|---------------|-------|
| Model | ARC-F31 |
| Working Height | 31m |
| Platform Size | 12m × 6m |
| Ground Pressure | 0.5 MPa |
| Pick & Carry | Yes |

**Why ARC-F31:**
- 31m height for stadium ceiling (28m peak)
- 12m × 6m platform for large sections
- Low ground pressure protected synthetic turf
- Pick & carry eliminated repositioning time

### Implementation

**Week 1: South Stand Section (5,000 sqm)**

- Platform positioned in 30 minutes
- Continuous work while moving
- 700 sqm/day average

**Weeks 2-4: Main Stand and North Stand (15,000 sqm)**

- Pick & carry covered large areas efficiently
- Multiple workers on platform simultaneously
- 800 sqm/day average

**Week 5: VIP and Media Areas (5,000 sqm)**

- Precision positioning for delicate systems
- Quality inspection throughout
- Project completed 1 week ahead of schedule

### Training & Support

- 2-day operator training
- Daily technical support
- Floor protection protocols
- Emergency response plan

## Results

### Quantitative Results

| Metric | Scaffolding | ARC-F31 | Improvement |
|--------|-------------|---------|-------------| 
| Setup Time | 3 weeks | 30 minutes | -99% |
| Daily Coverage | 400 sqm | 750 sqm | +88% |
| Total Duration | 8 weeks | 5 weeks | -38% |
| Floor Damage | 15 incidents | 0 | -100% |

### Qualitative Benefits

- **Zero Floor Damage**: Low ground pressure protected synthetic turf
- **Event Schedule Met**: Project completed before next major event
- **Quality Improvement**: Precise positioning for delicate systems
- **Operator Efficiency**: Small crew handled entire renovation

## Lessons Learned

1. **Pick & carry is essential**: Moving while elevated saved weeks of repositioning time.

2. **Floor protection matters**: Low ground pressure eliminated damage risk.

3. **Platform size impacts efficiency**: 12m × 6m platform allowed multiple work zones.
