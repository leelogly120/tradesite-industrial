## Project Overview

A new international airport terminal required ceiling panel installation across 80,000 sqm of terminal space, with heights ranging from 18m to 32m. The critical constraint: the terminal had to remain fully operational throughout the installation period, with passengers walking below the work zones.

## The Challenge

- Terminal had to stay open during construction — no gate closures allowed
- Strict zero-emission requirement for indoor operation
- Multiple ceiling heights across different terminal zones
- Night-shift work required near-silent operation
- Security restrictions limited equipment staging areas

## The Solution

Three FDDPT-31M platforms were deployed in rotation across the terminal zones. Electric-mode operation meant zero exhaust emissions, and the near-silent electric motors allowed overnight work without noise complaints from nearby residential areas.

The crawler tracks distributed weight evenly across the terminal's polished stone flooring — no point-load damage, no protective covering required. The 12m×3m platform accommodated 6-8 workers per shift with all necessary tools and materials.

## Key Results

The ceiling installation was completed 45 days ahead of the original schedule, with zero disruption to terminal operations. Airport authority praised the zero-emission approach and requested ARCLIFT for the terminal's Phase 2 expansion.

## Technical Specifications Used

- **Model:** FDDPT-31M High-Reach
- **Working Height:** 31m
- **Platform Size:** 12m × 8m (extended)
- **Load Capacity:** 500kg
- **Power:** Electric (indoor mode)
- **Control:** Full wireless remote
- **Units Deployed:** 3
