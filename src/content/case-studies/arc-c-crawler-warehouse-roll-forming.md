# Warehouse Roll Forming: 15,000 sqm Project in 4 Weeks

> "The integrated roll forming capability was a game-changer. No more damaged panels from transport." — Li Ming, Construction Director, Shanghai Logistics Group

## Project Overview

### Client Background

Shanghai Logistics Group operates one of China's largest e-commerce fulfillment networks. They were constructing a new 50,000 sqm distribution center in the Shanghai Free Trade Zone.

### The Challenge

The warehouse required 15,000 sqm of standing seam roofing. Previous experiences with pre-formed panels had resulted in significant waste due to transportation damage.

**Key Issues:**
- Pre-formed panels: 8-12% damage rate during transport
- Large roof area required multiple crane positions
- Tight timeline due to e-commerce peak season
- Limited ground space due to adjacent operational facilities

### Previous Solutions Attempted

On a previous project, the client used traditional crane placement:
- Pre-formed panels transported by truck
- 10% damage rate requiring replacement
- 3 crane positions needed, each taking 4 hours to set up
- Total project time: 8 weeks

## The ARCLIFT Solution

### Product Selection

The [ARC-C28 Crawler Roll Forming Lift](/products/arc-c28-crawler-roll-forming-lift/) was selected for its:

| Specification | Value |
|---------------|-------|
| Model | ARC-C28 |
| Working Height | 28m |
| Platform Size | 6m × 2m |
| Roll Forming Speed | 15m/min |
| Ground Pressure | 0.9 MPa |

**Why ARC-C28:**
- 28m height provided comfortable working range
- Integrated roll forming eliminated transport damage
- Self-propelled movement covered the entire roof area
- Diesel power for continuous outdoor operation

### Implementation

**Phase 1: Setup (Days 1-2)**

- Equipment delivered and assembled in 6 hours
- Roll forming calibration: 2 hours
- Operator training: 1 day
- Test panels produced and verified

**Phase 2: Production (Days 3-25)**

The crew achieved consistent 500-600 sqm/day output:
- Roll forming speed: 15 meters per minute
- Panel length: Up to 12 meters
- Continuous operation: 10-12 hours/day

**Phase 3: Completion (Days 26-28)**

Final sections completed and quality inspection performed. Zero rework required.

### Training & Support

- 1-day operator training
- Roll forming technique workshop
- Daily quality checks
- 24-hour technical support hotline

## Results

### Quantitative Results

| Metric | Previous Project | ARC-C28 Project | Improvement |
|--------|------------------|-----------------|-------------|
| Panel Damage | 10% | 0% | -100% |
| Total Duration | 8 weeks | 4 weeks | -50% |
| Crew Size | 6 workers | 3 workers | -50% |
| Rework Required | 15% | 0% | -100% |

### Qualitative Benefits

- **Zero Waste**: No damaged panels to dispose of or replace
- **Consistent Quality**: Roll forming on-site ensured uniform panels
- **Space Efficiency**: Single equipment footprint vs. multiple cranes
- **Weather Resilience**: Continued operation in light rain

## Lessons Learned

1. **Integrated roll forming pays off**: The 10% material savings more than covered the equipment cost difference.

2. **Training investment**: The 1-day training session paid for itself many times over in reduced waste and faster production.

3. **Single equipment solution**: Eliminating crane coordination simplified project management significantly.
