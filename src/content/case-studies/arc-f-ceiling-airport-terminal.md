# Airport Terminal Ceiling: Complete Upgrade in 4 Weeks

> "The dual power system was perfect for our terminal—electric mode during operations, diesel for overnight work." — Airport Director Li, Guangzhou Baiyun International Airport

## Project Overview

### Client Background

Guangzhou Baiyun International Airport is one of China's busiest airports, serving over 70 million passengers annually. Terminal B required a complete ceiling system upgrade.

### The Challenge

The 18,000 sqm ceiling area required replacement of acoustic panels, LED displays, and fire suppression systems while maintaining normal airport operations.

**Key Issues:**
- 18,000 sqm ceiling area
- Airport remained open 24/7
- Passenger safety paramount
- Night work required (11 PM - 5 AM)

### Previous Solutions Attempted

Previous maintenance used rope-access teams:
- Slow progress (200 sqm/day)
- Weather dependent
- Limited platform capacity
- Safety concerns at height

## The ARCLIFT Solution

### Product Selection

The [ARC-F25 Crawler Ceiling Platform](/products/arc-f25-crawler-ceiling-platform/) was selected for:

| Specification | Value |
|---------------|-------|

| Working Height | 25m |
| Platform Size | 12m × 4m |
| Ground Pressure | 0.6 MPa |

**Why ARC-F25:**
- 25m height for terminal ceiling (22m peak)
- Electric mode for quiet overnight work
- Low ground pressure for terminal floors
- Quick setup for limited work windows

### Implementation

**Week 1: Domestic Departure Hall (6,000 sqm)**

- Electric mode during operating hours
- Diesel mode for overnight work
- 500 sqm/day average

**Weeks 2-3: International Terminal (8,000 sqm)**

- Precision positioning for delicate systems
- Multiple work zones on platform
- 600 sqm/day average

**Week 4: Security and Check-in Areas (4,000 sqm)**

- Enhanced safety protocols
- Continuous monitoring
- Project completed on schedule

### Training & Support

- Airport safety certification
- Night work protocols
- Emergency response training
- 24/7 technical support

### Quantitative Results

| Daily Coverage | 200 sqm | 550 sqm | +175% |
| Total Duration | 12 weeks | 4 weeks | -67% |

### Qualitative Benefits

- **Zero Flight Disruptions**: Airport operations unaffected
- **Passenger Safety**: Enhanced safety protocols throughout
- **Quality Improvement**: Precise positioning for delicate systems
- **Noise Reduction**: Electric mode eliminated complaints

## Lessons Learned

1. **Dual power is essential**: Electric mode for occupied spaces, diesel for extended work.

2. **Airport protocols matter**: Specialized training for airport environments.

3. **Quick setup critical**: 30-minute setup maximized productive time in limited windows.
