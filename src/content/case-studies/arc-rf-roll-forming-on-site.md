# On-Site Roll Forming: 20,000 sqm Project

> "Producing panels at 4,000m altitude seemed impossible until we brought the ARC-RF8 on-site." — Project Manager Zhang, China Construction Third Bureau

## Project Overview

### Client Background

China Construction Third Bureau was building a high-altitude facility in Tibet, requiring 20,000 sqm of standing seam roof panels.

### The Challenge

The remote location at 4,000m altitude made transportation of pre-formed panels impractical. On-site production was the only viable option.

**Key Issues:**
- Remote location (4,000m altitude)
- No pre-formed panel transportation possible
- Extreme weather conditions
- Limited local resources

### Previous Solutions Attempted

Previous remote projects:
- Transportation damage: 15% waste
- Weather delays: 40% of project time
- Quality inconsistencies
- High logistics costs
