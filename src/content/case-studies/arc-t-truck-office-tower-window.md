# Office Tower Window Cleaning: 40% Faster Cycles

> "We went from dreading window cleaning day to completing it in record time." — Sun Tao, Facility Manager, Beijing Office Properties

## Project Overview

### Client Background

Beijing Office Properties manages 12 commercial office towers in Beijing's Central Business District. Each tower requires quarterly window cleaning.

### The Challenge

Traditional window cleaning methods were slow, expensive, and disruptive to tenants.

**Key Issues:**
- 5 days per tower for complete cleaning
- High labor costs (8-person crew)
- Tenant complaints about noise and disruption
- Weather delays extending timelines

### Previous Solutions Attempted

The company used traditional rope-access cleaning:
- 8-person crew per tower
- 5 days per cleaning cycle
- Manual positioning along each floor
- Weather-dependent schedule

## The ARCLIFT Solution

### Product Selection

The [ARC-T18 Truck-Mounted Roll Forming Lift](/products/arc-t18-truck-mounted-roll-forming-lift/) was selected for:

| Specification | Value |
|---------------|-------|
| Model | ARC-T18 |
| Working Height | 18m |
| Platform Size | 5m × 2m |
| Setup Time | 10 minutes |
| Reposition Time | 2 minutes |

**Why ARC-T18:**
- Optimal height for mid-rise office buildings
- Quick repositioning between window sections
- Truck-mounted for easy access to multiple buildings
- Electric mode for quiet operation during business hours

### Implementation

**Pilot Building (Month 1)**

- Selected one 15-story tower for testing
- 3-day cleaning cycle achieved
- Tenant satisfaction survey: 95% positive

**Fleet Deployment (Months 2-3)**

- Purchased 2 additional units
- Trained 6 operators
- Deployed across all 12 towers

**Ongoing Operations (Month 4+)**

- Quarterly cleaning cycles
- Average 3 days per tower
- Consistent quality and speed

### Training & Support

- 2-day operator training
- Safety certification program
- Quarterly refresher training
- 24/7 technical support

## Results

### Quantitative Results

| Metric | Rope Access | ARC-T18 | Improvement |
|--------|-------------|---------|-------------| 
| Cleaning Time | 5 days | 3 days | -40% |
| Crew Size | 8 workers | 3 workers | -62% |
| Tenant Complaints | 12 per cycle | 1 per cycle | -92% |
| Weather Delays | 2-3 per year | 0 | -100% |

### Qualitative Benefits

- **Tenant Satisfaction**: Dramatically reduced complaints
- **Consistent Quality**: Same crew, same equipment every cycle
- **Weather Independence**: Electric mode works in light rain
- **Fleet Efficiency**: Same equipment serves all 12 buildings

## Lessons Learned

1. **Fleet approach works**: Multiple units serving multiple buildings maximizes utilization.

2. **Operator consistency**: Training the same operators ensures consistent quality.

3. **Electric mode essential**: Quiet operation during business hours is critical.
