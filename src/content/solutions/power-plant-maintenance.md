# Power-Facility Access Project Planning

Access work in a power-generation or utility facility may involve energized systems, controlled areas, heat, dust, restricted routes and site-specific permits. These conditions cannot be reduced to a generic model recommendation.

ARCLIFT can help screen a crawler or truck-mounted platform concept after the asset owner and contractor define the work boundary. The facility's engineering, safety and regulatory teams retain authority over the method statement, isolation plan, hazardous-area classification and permission to operate.

## Evidence required before selection

The equipment configuration must be checked against dimensioned site data, verified support conditions and the actual task load. Electrical, hydraulic and emergency functions should be agreed in the technical schedule. Where a hazardous, nuclear, corrosive or high-temperature environment is involved, project-specific material, protection and documentation requirements must be supplied and reviewed; no standard suitability is implied.

ARCLIFT does not publish incident records, continuous-operation results or maintenance-duration comparisons without a verified project record.

## Project inputs to prepare

- Facility area classification and owner safety requirements
- Work coordinates, required reach and obstruction drawings
- Access route, doorway, turning and setup dimensions
- Floor or support capacity along travel and work positions
- Personnel, tool and component loads
- Ambient temperature, dust, moisture and corrosion conditions
- Isolation, exclusion-zone and emergency-recovery procedures
- Power availability and any electrical protection requirements
- Local inspection, registration, training and documentation rules
