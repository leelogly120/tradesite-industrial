# Power Plant Maintenance Solutions

## Why Power Plants Require Specialized Aerial Equipment

Power plants—thermal, nuclear, hydroelectric—operate in extreme environments with stringent safety requirements. Maintenance crews need equipment that can handle high temperatures, confined spaces, and continuous operation in demanding conditions.

### Common Pain Points in Power Plant Maintenance

1. **Extreme Operating Conditions**: Temperatures can exceed 50°C near boilers and turbines. Equipment must operate reliably in these harsh environments.

2. **Confined Space Access**: Turbine halls, boiler rooms, and electrical substations have limited access. Equipment must navigate tight spaces while maintaining stability.

3. **Heavy Component Handling**: Turbine blades, boiler tubes, and electrical components weigh hundreds of kilograms. Platforms must support both workers and heavy tools.

4. **Continuous Operation**: Power plants operate 24/7. Maintenance windows are limited to scheduled outages, requiring rapid deployment and extended operation.
