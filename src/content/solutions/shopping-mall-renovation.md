# Shopping Mall High-Level Work Planning

High-level work in a shopping mall must be planned around public separation, floor finishes, delivery routes, fire access and operating hours. The correct access method depends on the building and task; it cannot be inferred from a promotional height or platform-size figure.

ARCLIFT can help assess a large-deck crawler platform concept when the project team provides dimensioned information. The final configuration remains subject to technical review, approved drawings and destination requirements.

## Practical selection questions

- Can the machine enter, turn and reach every work position without relying on unverified clearances?
- What loads can the finished floor and any suspended slab support?
- How will the work zone be isolated from tenants and visitors?
- What power, emissions, noise and fire restrictions apply indoors?
- Which tools and materials must travel with the crew?

No setup-time, coverage, labour or downtime result should be assumed from a generic application page. These depend on the work sequence, access restrictions, crew, surface protection and owner approvals.

## Project inputs to prepare

- Plans showing loading entrance, corridors, turns and atrium positions
- Door, lift and ramp dimensions along the delivery route
- Finished-floor and slab load limits
- Required work elevation, offset and ceiling obstructions
- Personnel, tools, fixtures and material loads
- Surface-protection, housekeeping and indoor-power rules
- Public-isolation plan and permitted work windows
- Emergency access and local equipment-inspection requirements
