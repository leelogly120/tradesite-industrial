# Commercial Building Access Planning

Facade and atrium work can involve public circulation, restricted delivery hours, delicate finishes and narrow setup areas. Equipment should therefore be evaluated against the actual route and work zone, not selected from height alone.

Truck-mounted and crawler platform concepts solve different logistics problems. Road access, outrigger or crawler support, indoor entry, overhead obstructions and the permitted exclusion zone all influence the feasible configuration. ARCLIFT treats listed model classes as starting points for discussion; current capability must be confirmed in the signed technical schedule.

## Selection boundaries

The project team remains responsible for the work method, structural assessment, traffic management and local approvals. ARCLIFT can support equipment selection and documentation review after receiving project-specific data. Claims about setup time, daily output, labour savings or surface protection are not valid without a controlled comparison and recorded site conditions.

## Project inputs to prepare

- Building elevations and the coordinates of each work position
- Required vertical reach, horizontal offset and below-grade constraints
- Site entrance, turning radius, parking and setup dimensions
- Floor, slab or pavement load limits at travel and setup positions
- Personnel, tools and material loads for each task
- Public-separation, traffic and permitted working-hour requirements
- Indoor power, ventilation and noise restrictions where applicable
- Destination inspection, registration and operator requirements
