# ARCLIFT Two Evidence-First Articles — Design

Date: 2026-08-08

## Outcome

Publish exactly two English B2B technical articles that extend the existing 41-page blog library without deleting, renaming, or weakening any published route.

## Selected buyer decisions

1. **Change Control for Roll-Forming Lift Configurations** — help a buyer decide whether a late profile, material, forming-line, carrier, site, control, or documentation change can proceed, must be re-reviewed, or must remain on hold.
2. **Destination Receipt Plan for Roll-Forming Lift Systems** — help a receiving team preserve identity, condition, discrepancy, storage, and release evidence between carrier handoff and installation.

## Options considered

- Extend another site-planning topic. Rejected because route, floor, weather, work-zone, clearance, and commissioning coverage is already dense.
- Publish one broad buyer guide and one comparison. Rejected because the library already has selection and comparison pages, creating avoidable cannibalization.
- Add two lifecycle-control decisions. Selected because configuration change and destination receipt are distinct gaps, directly relevant to lift-system procurement, and support high-intent project discussions without unverified specifications.

## Content and evidence boundaries

- ARCLIFT is described only as an integrated equipment supplier and technical selection and supply partner.
- Public copy contains no source-factory, customer, project, or private-path identity.
- No universal capacity, dimension, price, delivery, certification, acceptance, or performance claim is introduced.
- ISO 10007 is used only as general configuration-management context; it does not certify ARCLIFT or approve a configuration.
- The IMO/ILO/UNECE CTU Code is used only as general intermodal receipt/unpacking context; it does not replace carrier, customs, destination, installation, or equipment-specific requirements.
- HSE work-equipment inspection guidance is used as general planning context, not as universal destination law.
- Every editorial visual is explicitly disclosed as AI-assisted and non-evidentiary.

## Page contract

Each article will have a 50–60 character title, 150–160 character description, 1,500–3,000 visible words, 4–6 H2 sections, at least 12 H3/H4 headings, four real FAQs, exactly three body visuals plus a distinct cover, at least two internal links, at least one authoritative external link, all six audit markers, and a 100/100 content-audit result.

## Release design

- Extend executable content audit coverage from 41 to 43 pages.
- Add one accessible 1600×900 decision diagram per article and classify both in the public asset manifest.
